4. The stark paradox: fatwa versus reality:

In Britain (2023):

Islamic alternatives cover 100% of the country.

Nevertheless: 38% of its Muslims still use interest-based loans.

Statement by Dr. Muhammad al-Hassan al-Dadu (President of the Center for Formation Studies): "Permissive fatwas have created a legal camouflage... the data proves that 60% of those who use interest in the West are not in real necessity."

5. The impact of updating fatwas (Canada as a model)

Before 2015: the percentage of interest usage was 71% (with alternatives available since 2003).

After 2016: when the Canadian Islamic Council canceled the permissive fatwa:

The percentage dropped to 34% over 5 years.

Islamic financing transactions increased by 300%.

Conclusion: the data proves that permissive fatwas:

Created a continuous legal cover despite the removal of their justification.

Contributed to delaying the spread of alternatives by at least 10 years.

Transformed "license of necessity" into a permanent permissibility in the consciousness of many Muslims.
