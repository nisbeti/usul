A definitive legal proof (dalil shar'i qati) rejects the presumptive texts for its sake, because those who assert this have stated that they are not considered unless supported by the texts of the Sharia in general, due to their compatibility with the actions of the Sharia overall.
As for al-Tufi, he said that if public interest (maslaha) contradicts the text and consensus, it is necessary to prioritize the preservation of public interest over them, even if the Sharia has not testified to the nature of this public interest, by specifying the public interest to the text and consensus and clarifying it for them, not by encroaching upon them or nullifying them(78).
Given the seriousness of this opinion and its current spread among the proponents of the liberal and secular currents, and their intention to challenge the rulings of Islamic law and ultimately revert them to what their minds find preferable while rejecting what they do not find preferable, and unfortunately seeking assistance from some who claim to belong to Islamic scholarship, the researcher wished to pause at this point and refute their arguments, and there is no power and no strength except with Allah.
