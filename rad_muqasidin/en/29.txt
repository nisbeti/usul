So there is no need here to cite the words of Ibn al-Qayyim, because the matter returns to what we say: that all the rulings of the Shari‘a are in themselves wisdom, justice and ease — not by our measures but by the measure of the Shari‘a. This is the school of those who affirm giving reasons, except al-Tufi. So there is no need to argue that the majority say that rulings have reasons, because they mean something other than what he means, as Ibn al-Qayyim explained.
