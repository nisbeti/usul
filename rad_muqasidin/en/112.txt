Aisha did not burden herself as the commentators do now, as they say, and she states, as they say, that when He commanded them to pray, it was a hardship for them, but as for fasting, no; she only justified it with the text, and that is sufficient.

And look, may Allah have mercy on you, how when the Companions (may Allah be pleased with them) justified the prohibition of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) and acted based on this justification in neglecting the text, what did the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) do to them?

In Sahih al-Bukhari, Aisha said: “We gave the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) medicine in his illness, and he kept signaling to us not to give him medicine. We said: It is because the sick person dislikes medicine. When he regained consciousness, he said: ‘Did I not forbid you from giving me medicine?’ We said: It is because of the dislike for the medicine. The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: ‘There will not remain among you anyone except that he will be given medicine, and I am looking at al-Abbas, for he did not witness you.’”
(We gave him medicine) means: we placed medicine in the side of his mouth without his choice, and it is said that it is the practice of rubbing dates on the gums and inserting medicine with the finger into the mouth, which is a painful matter.

It is mentioned in "Umdat al-Qari Sharh Sahih al-Bukhari" (18/73)
