(Al-Radd): I do not understand what the benefit is for the people in raising the door of the Kaaba to prevent anyone from entering except for those they wish, while their benefit lies in having the door flush with the ground. Likewise, their benefit is in including the Hijr (the area of the stone) within the Kaaba so that there is no confusion during the Tawaf (circumambulation), making the Kaaba a single structure that includes the Hijr, instead of some people performing Tawaf from inside the Hijr, which invalidates their Tawaf. Thus, there is no benefit in maintaining the Kaaba as it is in the eyes of the people. In the hadith, Aisha (may Allah be pleased with her) said: "I asked the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) about the wall of the House. He said, 'Yes.' I said, 'Then why did they not include it in the House?' He said, 'Your people have limited resources.' I said, 'What about the door being raised?' He said, 'Your people did that to allow whom they wished to enter and to prevent whom they wished. If it were not for the fact that your people are recently out of ignorance, I would fear that their hearts would reject my including the wall in the House and my having its door flush with the ground.'" (4).

Secondly: No one has said or transmitted that the construction of the Kaaba on the foundations of Ibrahim was an obligatory matter, so we cannot say that here the obligation conflicts with the harm of people's denial, leading to the abandonment of the obligation due to this harm. Otherwise, the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) did many things that contradicted the customs of the people of ignorance during Hajj and Umrah, and he did not abandon them out of fear of people's denial. And there is no contradiction in the hadith between an obligation and a recommended act.

Among the matters that he (peace and blessings be upon him) did in opposition to what people might disapprove of is what al-Bukhari narrated from Ibn Abbas (may Allah be pleased with him) who said: "They used to consider that performing Umrah (lesser pilgrimage) during the months of Hajj was a sinful act on earth, and they used to call Muharram Safar. Then the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) arrived with his companions in a state of ihram for Hajj, and the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) commanded them to perform it as Umrah." They said, "O Messenger of Allah, which is the area of permissibility?" He said, "All of it is permissible."(99).
