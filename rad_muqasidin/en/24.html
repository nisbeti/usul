And His action, Exalted is He, is not for a reason or wisdom. This is the view chosen by many of our companions and some of the Malikis and Shafi'is. It was also stated by the Zahiris, the Ash'aris, and the Jahmiyyah.

The second opinion is that they are for a reason and wisdom. This was chosen by al-Tufi, and Sheikh Taqi al-Din, and Ibn al-Qayyim, and Ibn Qadi al-Jabal. It was reported from the consensus of the predecessors, and it is the doctrine of the Shi'a and the Mu'tazila, but the Mu'tazila assert the necessity of righteousness. They have two opinions regarding what is more righteous.

Those who oppose them say that they are for a reason, but not according to the methodology of the Mu'tazila.

A group has permitted both matters. Sheikh Taqi al-Din said: Among the Ahl al-Sunnah, there are two opinions regarding the reasoning behind the actions and rulings of Allah, Exalted is He, and most are on the side of reasoning.
