Al-Tabari said, "Allah, Exalted is He, said: 'The increase from the aspect of sale is not like the increase from the aspect of usury (riba), for I have made lawful sale and made usury unlawful. The command is Mine, and the creation is Mine. I decree in both as I wish and enslave them as I desire. No one among them has the right to object to My judgment or to oppose My command; rather, they must obey Me and submit to My decree.'"(37)

And they ask about the difference between someone who lends another one dinar for a month— is this permissible? From their statement, this is permissible. And they ask about someone who sells another one dinar for a month— from their statement, this is usurious delay (riba nasi'a) due to the hadith "Gold for gold must be exchanged hand to hand, equal for equal, and the same for the same."(38) It is said to them, what is the difference in reason between the two? If they say there is no difference, they acknowledge that the judgment belongs to Allah, Exalted is He, for He has ruled that the first is an act of goodness and kindness, while He has ruled that the second is prohibited usury, cursed is its consumer, and it is ugly. The two issues are very similar, but Allah does as He wills and is not questioned about what He does, and we have no choice but to submit.

Thus, there is no general principle governing the actions of Allah, Exalted is He, such that He must apply it in every branch of the Sharia and refer to the explicit and authentic texts, and all praise is due to Allah.

2- From their statement, the legislator described some actions as good before permitting them and described others as bad before forbidding them. Among this is His saying, Exalted is He: {And when they commit an immorality, they say, 'We found our forefathers doing it, and Allah has commanded us to do it.' Say, 'Indeed, Allah does not command immorality. Do you say about Allah that which you do not know?'}(39) They said: He does not command them with what is immoral in reason.
