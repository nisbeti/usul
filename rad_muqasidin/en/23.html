"And His action, Exalted is He, is not for a reason or wisdom" in the statement "Many of our companions and some of the Malikis and Shafi'is have chosen this." And this was said by the Dhahiri school, the Ash'ari school, and the Jahmi school.

The second opinion is that they are for a reason and wisdom. This was chosen by al-Tufi, and Sheikh Taqi al-Din, and Ibn al-Qayyim, and Ibn Qadi al-Jabal. And it was reported from the consensus of the Salaf, and it is the doctrine of the Shi'a and the Mu'tazila, but the Mu'tazila say that righteousness is obligatory. And they have two opinions regarding what is more righteous.

And those who oppose them say that they are for a reason, not according to the methodology of the Mu'tazila.

And a group has permitted both matters. Sheikh Taqi al-Din said: For the people of the Sunnah regarding the reasoning behind the actions of Allah, Exalted is He, and His rulings, there are two opinions, and the majority are on the reasoning.)) Ibn al-Najjar (898 - 972 AH = 1492 - 1564 CE)
