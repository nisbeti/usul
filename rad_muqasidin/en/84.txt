This response was accepted by al-Qurtubi(175). Thus, the ruling changed due to the change in the people's custom from intending by repeating divorce to confirming their intention by repeating it as a foundation, so Umar interpreted their statement according to the verbal custom and the apparent meaning.
Thirdly: Some have interpreted the hadith by saying that "people during the time of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) would issue one divorce, but during the time of Umar, they would issue three." The essence of this is that the divorce enacted during Umar's time as three was previously enacted as one, because they did not use three at all, or they used it rarely. However, in Umar's era, its usage became widespread. The meaning of his statement, "and he enforced it upon them and permitted it," among other things, is that he ruled on the divorce as it had been ruled before him. Ibn al-Arabi favored this interpretation and attributed it to Abu Zur'ah. Al-Nawawi said that, based on this, the report occurred due to the differing customs of the people specifically, not due to a change in the ruling regarding the single divorce(176). I do not know, with the abundance of these interpretations, what drives them to attribute Umar's action to prioritizing public interest (maslaha) over the text, and Umar did not say that either, and he is among the furthest from that, may Allah be pleased with him. And may Allah grant success.
