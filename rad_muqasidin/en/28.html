This is despite the fact that what the intellect is unable to grasp regarding the goodness and badness of actions is not less than what it can comprehend. Thus, the need for messengers is essential. In fact, it is above all needs. The world is in greater need of them than anything else, the messengers, may Allah's blessings be upon them all."

It is noteworthy that after al-Raisuni elaborated on the idea that reasoning is the true doctrine and took Ibn al-Qayyim's words on reasoning to great heights, he was surprised to find that Ibn al-Qayyim does not mean exactly what he has interpreted, that human intellect is completely independent in grasping benefit as al-Raisuni wants to assert, thereby negating the texts. Rather, Ibn al-Qayyim often attributed the matter to the incapacity of the intellect to perceive goodness and badness, justice and injustice in detailed issues. Thus, the argument based on Ibn al-Qayyim's words returned to the original point.
