Not everything claimed by these contemporary individuals is a hardship that nullifies the legal ruling. The statement of Allah, “Allah intends for you ease and does not intend for you hardship” { يريد الله بكم اليسر ولا يريد بكم العسر } (263) is undoubtedly true, but what ease are they talking about, and what hardship has Allah removed from us in His legislation? Is it every hardship? Certainly not. For instance, fasting the obligatory fasts in the heat of the day, performing ablution with cold water, fighting, and enduring the preservation of the religion despite trials and hardships, as Allah said { وَإِن كَانَتْ لَكَبِيرَةً إِلَّا عَلَى الْخَاشِعِينَ } (264). Allah did not permit His Messenger to change the law to align with the desires of the polytheists, while He knows the extent of the hardship and difficulty that would befall him and his companions as a result. All of this leads to the conclusion that the hardships of the Hereafter and the hardships after death are the greatest hardships that Allah intended to lift from us, and these major hardships can only be lifted by implementing the commands of Allah, Exalted is He.
