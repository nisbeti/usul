(Al-Radd) First: As for the statement of the angels, let us look into these narrations.

It is mentioned in al-Targhib wa al-Tarhib by Qawam al-Sunnah (1/440)

He said: The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said:

"Indeed, the first to respond were the angels. Allah said: {I am placing a vicegerent on earth. They said: Will You place therein one who causes corruption therein and sheds blood, while we glorify You with praise and sanctify You?} He turned away from them, and they circled the Throne for six years, saying: Here we are, here we are, as an excuse to You; here we are, here we are, we seek Your forgiveness and turn to You."

It is mentioned in the authentic collection of tafsir by transmission (1/62)

Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal said: Yahya ibn Abi Bakir narrated to us, Zuhair ibn Muhammad narrated to us from Musa ibn Jubair from Nafi, the freed slave of Abdullah ibn Umar, from Abdullah ibn Umar that he heard the Prophet of Allah (peace be upon him) say: When Allah sent Adam (peace be upon him) down to earth, the angels said: O Lord, will You place therein one who causes corruption therein and sheds blood, while we glorify You with praise and sanctify You? He said: Indeed, I know what you do not know. They said: Our Lord, we are more obedient to You than the children of Adam. Allah, Exalted is He, said to the angels: Bring forth two angels so that they may descend to earth and see how they act. They said: Our Lord, Harut and Marut. So they descended to earth, and a woman of the most beautiful of humans was presented to them. She came to them and they asked her for herself, and she said: By Allah, not until you speak this word of associating partners. They said: By Allah, we will never associate partners with Allah. Then she left them and returned with a child in her arms, and they asked her for herself, and she said: By Allah, not until you kill this child.
