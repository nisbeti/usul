The address killed a group, five or seven, by one man whom they killed treacherously, and Umar said, "If the people of Sana'a had banded together against him, I would have killed them all."(177) Sheikh al-Albani authenticated it and said in one of its chains that the Hafiz said in al-Fath: This narration is connected to Umar with the most authentic chain.(178) It was said: When Umar established the killing of the group by one, he did so based on public interest (maslaha) in opposition to the noble text (prescribed for you is retaliation for the murdered: the free for the free, and the slave for the slave) { كُتِبَ عَلَيْكُمُ القِصَاصُ فِي القَتْلَى الحُرُّ بِالْحُرِّ وَالْعَبْدُ بِالْعَبْدِ }(179), and His saying (and We prescribed for them therein that a soul for a soul and an eye for an eye) { وَكَتَبْنَا عَلَيْهِمْ فِيهَا أَنَّ النَّفْسَ بِالنَّفْسِ وَالْعَيْنَ بِالْعَيْنِ }(180). The free person is one, and the slave is one, and the soul is one, so it is not permissible for the ruling to extend to multiple souls; rather, only one soul should be killed, while the other participants should not be killed because the original ruling is the sanctity of their blood.

(Response): "Umar's action was not legislation as some have imagined, but rather an application of the text with deep understanding. The definite article in (the soul) refers to (the type) and does not mean (the individual), and the 'by' in the following soul is a causal 'by'. Thus, the text means that every soul that participated in the killing is to be killed by the soul that killed, that is, because of this killed soul. Furthermore, Umar's ijtihad (jurisprudential reasoning) to kill the group by one makes the application of the text in this manner a remedy for the heart, indeed a complete remedy.
