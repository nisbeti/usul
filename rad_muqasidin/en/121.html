Tenth: Allah, Exalted is He, said: “And do not say about what your tongues describe, ‘This is lawful and this is unlawful,’ to invent falsehood against Allah. Indeed, those who invent falsehood against Allah will not succeed”(240). Al-Razi said, “Know that Allah, Exalted is He, when He restricted the prohibited things to those four, emphasized this restriction and refuted the way of the disbelievers in adding to these four and subtracting from them others. They have added to the prohibited things and also added to the permissible things, and it has been made clear that the things they say are lawful and unlawful are lies and fabrications against Allah”(241). Likewise, anyone who permits or prohibits based on what they see as beneficial without legislation.

Eleventh: Allah, Exalted is He, said: “Follow what has been revealed to you from your Lord, and do not follow other than Him as allies”(242). Al-Tabari said, “He, Exalted is He, is saying to His Prophet Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him), ‘O Muhammad, to these polytheists from your people who worship idols and statues, follow, O people, what has come to you from your Lord with clarity and guidance, and act upon what your Lord has commanded you, and do not follow anything other than what has been revealed to you from your Lord’”(243). Thus, anyone who follows what they see as beneficial without permission from Allah is following other than what Allah has revealed, and Allah, Exalted is He, knows best what is correct.
