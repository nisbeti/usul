And others took the apparent meaning and its reality"(106).
And the claim that he (peace and blessings be upon him) approved them is not found in the hadith at all; rather, it only indicates that he (peace and blessings be upon him) did not reprimand them, and refraining from reprimanding is one thing, while approval is another. The correct opinion is one, as is established, and one of them is correct without a doubt. The Prophet may have refrained from reprimanding, but he clarified the truth. Al-Nawawi says, "It can be inferred from this that every mujtahid (one who exerts effort in legal reasoning) is correct, and the other speaker can say: He did not explicitly state the correctness of both groups, but he refrained from reprimanding them, and there is no disagreement regarding the refraining from reprimanding a mujtahid even if he is mistaken, provided he exerted his utmost effort in ijtihad, and Allah knows best"(107). Thus, there is no evidence in the hadith for abandoning the text for the sake of public interest (maslaha), nor is there evidence of the Prophet's (peace and blessings be upon him) approval of that, and Allah knows best, and success is from Allah, the Exalted.
Seventh: They cited the fact that the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) did not kill Abdullah ibn Ubayy ibn Salul for the sake of public interest (maslaha) even though he was a disbeliever, which indicates the permissibility of abandoning the hadith for the sake of public interest.
(Response) It appears that Abdullah ibn Ubayy ibn Salul was a hypocrite inwardly, and he did not manifest his disbelief to the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) with testimony from someone whose testimony is valid to establish the punishment against him. The hadith reported by Zaid ibn Arqam, where he said, "The honored among them will be expelled from the humiliated," is only established by the testimony of one person, which is not sufficient to establish the punishment for apostasy. If that is the case, we are commanded to apply the rulings based on the apparent, and Allah takes care of the secrets.
Ibn Hazm (may Allah have mercy on him) says: "And likewise his (peace and blessings be upon him) saying, 'People should not say that Muhammad kills his companions.'"
