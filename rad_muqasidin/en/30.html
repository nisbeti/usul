Do not be like that. For all things, in relation to their original state, are equal; there is no judgment from reason regarding their goodness or badness. Thus, the existence of an interest (maslaha) as an interest is from the legislator, such that reason affirms it and the soul finds peace in it"3)) and he says ("The interests that pertain to the conditions of the servant are known in their true essence only by their Creator and Legislator, and the servant has no knowledge of them except from certain aspects"4.))

Thus, the inference from al-Shatibi's words collapses fundamentally, as he means by improvement and disapproval and interest all that Allah has decreed to be so, not according to human whims. Therefore, the inference from the words of al-Shatibi and Ibn al-Qayyim is entirely misplaced, as is the opinion of the majority attributed to them regarding the reasoning based on interest.
