Introduction
The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) continued to receive revelation from his Lord, Exalted is He, and he would inform his companions (may Allah be pleased with them) about it, and they would receive it from him with listening and obedience. Sometimes they would be astonished, as when the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) spoke to them about a cow that speaks, and they were amazed and said: "A cow that speaks?" He said: "I believe in this, I and Abu Bakr and Umar"(1). At other times, they would become angry, as when the Quraysh were upset about giving the people of Najd and leaving them(2), or they would hesitate at times, such as their hesitation in slaughtering and shaving after the story of the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah(3). However, despite all of this, the command of Allah does not change due to their astonishment, nor their anger, nor their hesitation (may Allah be pleased with them), even though such occurrences were rare and not the norm. In the end, everyone submits to the command of Allah, Exalted is He.
There is no report of them saying what those with sickness in their hearts said: "What did Allah intend by this as an example?"(4), nor did they ask Him, Exalted is He, why did You do this? And Allah, Exalted is He, said: "He is not to be questioned about what He does"(5). The matter remained in this state until, after several centuries and the passing of the best early generations, some began to ask this question: What did Allah, Exalted is He, intend by legislating this ruling?
