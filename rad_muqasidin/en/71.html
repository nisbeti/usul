By making Hajj (pilgrimage) equivalent to Umrah (lesser pilgrimage), they said: "How could that be when we have named it Hajj?" They hesitated, and this is a contradiction to the text based on custom(135).

(Response): This reasoning is strange, as the Companions executed the command of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) and did not oppose him; rather, they were only astonished by it (and it was a great matter for them)(136) because they previously believed that performing Umrah during the months of Hajj was sinful, yet they executed the command, as indicated by what is reported in Sahih Muslim: "We had relations with our women, we used perfume, and we wore our garments, and there were only four nights left until Arafat."(137). So where in the hadith did they abandon the text for custom and for presumed benefit? Glory be to You, this is a great slander.

Secondly: They cited Abu Bakr's (may Allah be pleased with him) compilation of the Qur'an, and the point of evidence is that this contradicts the hadith: "Whoever performs an action that is not in accordance with our command, it is rejected."(138)

(Response): I do not see where Abu Bakr opposed the text here and prioritized the imagined benefit. Ibn Hazm says: "As for Abu Bakr's (may Allah be pleased with him) compilation of the Mushaf, yes, and the reason for that is clear. The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) used to receive the Qur'an in parts and would command that the verse revealed be joined to such and such verse from such and such surah. Thus, it was not possible to write the Qur'an in a comprehensive Mushaf for that reason. When he passed away (peace and blessings be upon him), and the revelation was established, and it was known that there would be no more added to it or altered, Abu Bakr wrote it down at that time and confirmed it."(139).
