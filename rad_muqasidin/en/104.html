I said (Abu Mazen): "It has been reported in the authentic collection of interpretations based on transmission (4/127)."

It was narrated by al-Hakim (al-Mustadrak 2/416) through the chain of Ibn Abi Najih from Mujahid from Umm Salamah, with this wording. He said: It is authentic according to the conditions of the two shaykhs, although they did not narrate it, and al-Dhahabi agreed with him. It was narrated by al-Tirmidhi, who graded it as good, and al-Albani authenticated it (al-Sunan 5/354 - Book of Tafsir, Chapter on Surah Al-Ahzab, Hadith 3211). Al-Hafiz Ibn Hajar also graded it as good after he narrated it through its chains and supporting evidence (Muwafaqat al-Khabar 2/21-25) and al-Nawawi said: Its chain is authentic (see the authentication of the hadith in al-Kashaf 3/109). Al-Suyuti also authenticated it (al-Jami al-Saghir with Fayd al-Qadir 1/277 Hadith 434).

It is mentioned in Musnad Ahmad (44/199)

Its chain of narration is authentic, its narrators are trustworthy narrators of the Sahih. Shaybah is the son of Uthman al-Qurashi, and al-Nasa'i narrated from him, and he is trustworthy. He was corroborated, as it was narrated by Uthman ibn Hakim from Abdur-Rahman ibn Shaybah and Abdullah ibn Rafi'.
