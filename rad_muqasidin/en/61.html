A clear statement regarding the apparent wording, cut off on the absence of a correct inner meaning that Abdullah ibn Ubayy is among the companions of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) by the apparentness of his Islam, and that he is among the Muslim companions who are subject to the rulings of Islam, and whose blood Allah, Exalted is He, has made sacred except by its due right? And we certainly know that if the blood of Ibn Ubayy had been lawful, the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) would not have favored him, and if it had been obligatory upon him, he would not have neglected it - peace be upon him? And whoever thinks that the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) would not kill someone whose killing was obligatory among his companions has indeed disbelieved, and his blood and wealth have become lawful, due to his false association with the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), and his opposition to Allah, Exalted is He. By Allah, the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) has indeed killed his virtuous companions who were assured of faith and Paradise when their killing was obligatory, such as Ma'iz, the Ghamidiyya, and the Juhaniyya (may Allah be pleased with them). It is certainly false and a clear misguidance, and pure immorality - rather, it is outright disbelief - to believe or to think that a Muslim would believe that the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) would kill virtuous Muslims from the people of Paradise among his companions in the most heinous manner with stones, and that he would kill Al-Harith ibn Suwayd al-Ansari in retaliation against Al-Mujaddir ibn Khiyar al-Balawi with his knowledge - peace be upon him - without anyone knowing that, and the woman whom he commanded Unays to stone if she confessed. And for cutting off the hand of the woman from the Makhzum clan - and he says, "If Fatimah were to steal, I would cut off her hand." And by his saying - peace be upon him - "The Children of Israel were destroyed because when a weak person among them committed a crime, they would punish him, but when a noble person committed it, they would leave him." Then he himself - peace be upon him - does that, and he neglects the establishment of the obligatory right in killing the apostate who knows that he has just apostatized, then he is not satisfied with this until he prays for him and seeks forgiveness for him - while he knows that he is a disbeliever. And Allah, Exalted is He, has previously prohibited him from seeking forgiveness for the disbelievers. And we bear witness by the testimony of Allah, Exalted is He, that whoever adheres to this and believes it is indeed a disbeliever, a polytheist, an apostate, whose blood and wealth are lawful - we disassociate ourselves before Allah, Exalted is He, from him and from his allegiance - whoever thinks of him as a hypocrite without dispute, the matter is with those below him without exception.
