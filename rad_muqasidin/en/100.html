Al-Bazzar's chain of narration is sound, and al-Albani said: "It is the closest," and al-Haythami said: "Its chain is good"(224). This hadith indicates that everything the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) remained silent about is excused, thus it is established that every incident has a ruling, as incidents do not fall outside the previous classification, and success is from Allah, the Exalted.
