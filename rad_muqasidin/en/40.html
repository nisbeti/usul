It is commanded as a test, and the intention is not to carry out the commanded action??? But here there is a pressing question: has the command not come, and we are certain that Allah does not command immorality? And Ibrahim (peace be upon him) and his son (peace be upon him), the noble prophets, did not say or ask, "Why, O Lord, have You done this?" as these people ask, nor did they say, "What is the wisdom?" and "What is the benefit?" nor did they claim that this contradicts the objectives of the Sharia (maqasid al-sharia) of preserving life, how could it be when it is a pure and virtuous soul? Here, the definitive objectives of the Sharia take precedence over the command of slaughter, rather they accepted the command with submission and compliance.

The question is: does this not indicate that they understood that this command, despite its apparent ugliness to limited intellect, is indeed the truth, justice, and goodness when commanded by Allah? And if the threefold division mentioned by Ibn Taymiyyah is correct, then why should many of the established rulings of the Sharia based on the Book and the Sunnah not belong to this third category, which is intended merely as a test for the servants, while we are unaware that it is so? As for Ibn Taymiyyah's statement that the commanded action is not intended, it is refuted by His saying, Exalted is He: “And if We had decreed upon them, ‘Kill yourselves’ or ‘Leave your homes,’ they would not have done it, except for a few of them. But if they had done what they were instructed, it would have been better for them and a firmer position”(49). Al-Tabari said: "If we were to suppose that those who claim to have believed in what has been revealed to you and are judging by the tyrant were commanded to kill themselves or to emigrate from their homes to another land (they did not do it), meaning: they did not kill themselves with their own hands nor did they emigrate from their homes"(50). “And if they had done what they were instructed” by following the Sharia and submitting to the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), it would have been better for them in this world and the Hereafter and a firmer support for them in adhering to the truth(51).
