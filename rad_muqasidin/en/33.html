This is rejected because it is known from the Sharia (Islamic law) that one of its objectives is to "remove hardship" (raf' al-harj), and there is hardship in destroying the meat and broth. Thus, they hastened to condemn this as irrational in their view.

However, al-Nawawi said, "Know that what is commanded regarding the spilling of pots is merely the destruction of the broth itself as a punishment for them, whereas the meat itself was not destroyed; rather, it is assumed that it was collected and returned to the spoils, and it should not be thought that it was commanded to be destroyed because it belongs to the spoils, and it is prohibited to waste wealth..." And if it is said, why is it not reported that they carried the meat to the spoils, we say it is also not reported that they burned it or destroyed it, and if there is no explicit transmission regarding it, then it must be interpreted in accordance with the legal principles) (31).

The evidence of those who advocate for rational good and evil, whether they consider them to be dependent on revelation or not, and the response to them:

1- If we look at actions, we find in them qualities that the mind may perceive as good or evil... Among them is justice, which is a good act... and among them is oppression, which is an evil act.

(Response) The meaning of justice is to judge by the truth(32), and the essence of oppression is placing something in an inappropriate place(33).

If that is the case, then the truth is what Allah, Exalted is He, judges, as He says: "And Allah judges with truth"(34).
