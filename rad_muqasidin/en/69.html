This issue is on the tongue of Umar and in his heart, and the intended meaning of "the truth" here is not the absolute truth, so the definite article (الـ) here is not for generality but rather for mental reference, meaning the truth that is known to you regarding this raised issue. As for the addition of "he says" in the hadith, it has been weakened by al-Albani(128). Therefore, it should not be used as evidence. Perhaps the intended truth is what was stated on his tongue in the hadith narrated by Anas, who said: Umar said: "I agreed with my Lord in three matters. I said, O Messenger of Allah, should we take from the standing place of Ibrahim a place of prayer?" Then the verse was revealed: {And take from the standing place of Ibrahim a place of prayer}(129). And I said, O Messenger of Allah, your women are visited by both the righteous and the wicked, so should I command them to veil themselves? Then the verse of hijab was revealed. And the wives of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) gathered around him out of jealousy, and I said to them: {Perhaps his Lord, if he divorces you, will give him instead wives better than you}(130). She said, and it was revealed likewise. This indicates that the intended meaning is a specific issue, as evidenced by what has been established about him (may Allah be pleased with him) regarding his mistakes in several matters, including

His statement in the story of Hudaybiyyah (peace be upon him) "So we did not concede anything in our religion" (131) and then his return and his statement "I did actions for that."

His statement regarding dowries and the exaggeration in them, then his retraction, and his statement "A woman was correct and Umar was mistaken."

His denial of Abu Bakr regarding fighting those who say "There is no god but Allah," then his statement after that: "By Allah, it is nothing but that...
