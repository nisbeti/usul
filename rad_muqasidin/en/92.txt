"Like the wind"(200). It is noted that Yahya ibn Abi Kathir said regarding him, "Abu Hatim, Abu Zur'ah, al-Bukhari, and others stated that no one from the Companions was met except for Anas ibn Malik, for he saw him but did not hear from him. This is the wording of Abu Hatim"(201). Based on this, the hadith is considered disconnected, and a disconnected hadith is not a proof according to the majority of hadith scholars(202). The hadith was narrated as connected through various chains from Yahya ibn Abi Kathir, from Ibn Zaher, from Husayn ibn Hudayr, who said: I heard Umar say: "There is no cutting in a cluster, nor in a year of famine." Al-Albani said, "Weak."
Ibn Abi Shayba narrated it (11/74/2) from Hisham al-Dastawayh from Yahya ibn Abi Kathir from Hassan ibn Zaher from Husayn ibn Hudayr who said: I heard Umar saying: "There is no cutting in (ghadq), nor in the year of abundance."
Al-Hafiz cited in "al-Talkhis" (4/70) from Ibrahim ibn Ya'qub al-Jawzajani in "al-Jami'" from Ahmad ibn Hanbal from Harun ibn Isma'il from Ali ibn al-Mubarak from Yahya ibn Abi Kathir with it and added: "He said: So I asked Ahmad ibn Hanbal about it? He said: (al-ghadq): the date palm, and the year of the year: the year of famine. I said to Ahmad: Do you say this? He said: Indeed, by my life." I said: And he was silent about its chain of narration, and there is a lack of clarity in it; for Hassan ibn Zahir and Husayn ibn Hudhayr have ambiguity in them, as Ibn Abi Hatim mentioned them (1/2/236, 191) and did not mention any criticism in them nor...
