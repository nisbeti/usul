The realm of Islamic economics has been affected by the dust of these ideas, and some have ventured into issues of Islamic economic and financial jurisprudence, attempting to manipulate them, bending the evidence to their will by using the principle of "its contradiction to the objectives of the legislator." However, Allah has mentioned a purpose or goal for this ruling or that one only in rare instances. Rather, He, Exalted is He, said, “And Allah knows, while you do not know” {وَاللَّهُ يَعْلَمُ وَأَنْتُمْ لاَ تَعْلَمُونَ}(9), and He said, “And if the truth had followed their desires, the heavens and the earth and whoever is in them would have been corrupted” {وَلَوِ اتَّبَعَ الحَقُّ أَهْوَاءَهُمْ لَفَسَدَتِ السَّمَوَاتُ وَالأَرْضُ وَمَن فِيهِنَّ}(10).
