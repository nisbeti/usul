"This religion, in all its components of belief, worship, and legislation, is established; there is no modification or change in it, for this religion has been completed and its matter has ended"(234).

Allah, Exalted is He, said: “O you who have believed, obey Allah and obey the Messenger and those in authority among you. And if you disagree over anything, refer it to Allah and the Messenger if you should believe in Allah and the Last Day. That is the best [way] and best in result”(235). Thus, He commanded to refer back in case of dispute to Allah and the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him), not to what we see as beneficial.

Sixth: Allah, Exalted is He, said: “And whatever you disagree over, its ruling is to Allah”(236). "So whatever is ruled by the Book and the Sunnah and is affirmed as authentic is the truth, and what is after the truth except misguidance"(237). Therefore, any presumption outside the Book and the Sunnah is misguidance.

Eighth: Allah, Exalted is He, said: “And We have sent down to you the Book as clarification for all things and as guidance and mercy and good tidings for the Muslims”(238). Al-Khazin said: “And We have sent down to you the Book” meaning the Qur'an, “as clarification for all things” is a name from clarification. Mujahid said: it means what He commanded and what He prohibited, and the scholars of meanings said: “as clarification for all things” means matters of religion"(239). Thus, it is not correct, after the clarification of the commanded and prohibited, to return to any source other than it, as it has indicated the Sunnah.
