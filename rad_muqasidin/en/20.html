In any case, there was a motive for legislating the ruling that the spoils (fay’) must be divided: (“What Allah has bestowed on His Messenger from the people of the towns belongs to Allah and the Messenger, and to the near of kin, the orphans, the poor and the wayfarer, so that it may not be a thing circulating among the rich among you”). This legislation of the ruling has an end, aim, purpose or wisdom — all words they use to mean one thing — and the purpose here is (“so that it may not be a thing circulating among the rich”). A practical example: a person’s anger (a motive, cause or reason, depending on how they differ in terminology) leads to taking revenge, and this act of revenge has an (aim, purpose or wisdom), which is the feeling of ease in the soul.
