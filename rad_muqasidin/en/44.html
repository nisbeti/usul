Truth, justice, and wisdom, even if some of that from us is injustice and folly. And everything that Allah, Exalted is He, did not do is injustice, falsehood, futility, and disparity. You all acknowledge that Allah, Exalted is He, has always been One, without any creation alongside Him, nor anything existing, neither body, nor accident, nor essence; there is neither intellect nor intelligible, nor folly nor anything else. Then you have acknowledged without disagreement among you that He created souls and brought them into existence after they did not exist, and He created intellects and instilled them in the souls after intellects did not exist at all. Therefore (63)(*), it is necessary that you do not impose upon the Creator, Exalted is He, a judgment that is obligatory upon Him due to some of His creation. If they say that the ugly has always been ugly in the knowledge of Allah, Exalted is He, and the good has always been good in His knowledge, we say to them: Suppose this is as you say, then you have two invalid judgments regarding your corrupt statement. One is that you have made the judgment in that matter based on the intelligible, not based on what preceded in the knowledge of Allah, Exalted is He. Thus, you have not made the prohibition of doing what is ugly in your view except because the intellects deem it ugly. Then, "what is your evidence for this? Perhaps He, Exalted is He, has always known that a matter is good for a period of time, then He deems it ugly, and it becomes ugly when He deems it so, not before that, as He has done with all the abrogated religions.

And we also say to them: Inform us, who has made good good in the intellects and who has made ugly ugly in the intellects? If they say: Allah, Exalted is He, we say to them: Was Allah, Exalted is He, able to reverse that order, so that He arranges it contrary to how He arranged it, making the ugly good and the good ugly? If they say yes, they must affirm that nothing became ugly except after Allah, Exalted is He, judged it to be ugly, and nothing became good except after Allah, Exalted is He, judged it to be good. And if they say no, they have incapacitated their Lord. Or it may be that He, Exalted is He, has made that obligatory upon Himself after He was not always obligated to it. If one says this, it is said to him: Then He was not obligated to it until He made it obligatory. Thus, it is as such.
