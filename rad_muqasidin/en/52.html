I attempt in this section to shed light on the evidence of those who claim that Allah, Exalted is He, intends through legislation what a person believes to be in their interest, and to refute it, with Allah's help, and to clarify its flaws.

Evidence of those who advocate for the objective of public interest (maslaha) and the response to it:

First: "Indeed, Allah, Mighty and Majestic, has provided us with a way to know our interests by custom, so we should not abandon it for an ambiguous matter (meaning the legal texts) that may or may not lead to an interest, and it cannot be said that the Sharia is more knowledgeable of their interests, so it should be derived from its evidence... because this is only said regarding acts of worship that conceal their interests from the workings of minds and customs, whereas the interest of the governance of the obligated in their rights is known to them by virtue of customs and reason"(79).

(Response) Allah, Exalted is He, has not provided us with a way to know our interests except through Him, and this is supported by the following evidence:

1- Allah, Exalted is He, says: {And who is more astray than one who follows his desire without guidance from Allah}(80).

2- And He says: {And if the truth had followed their desires, the heavens and the earth and whoever is in them would have been corrupted}(81).

3- And He says: {Have you seen the one who has taken as his god his desire? Then would you be responsible for him?}(82).

4- And the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) reports from his Lord, Exalted is He: {O My servants, all of you are astray except for whom I have guided.}

__________

(79) The previous source, vol. 1, p. 349, 390.

(80) Surah Al-Qasas, verse 50.

(81) Surah Al-Mu'minun, verse 71.

(82) Surah Al-Furqan, verse 43.
