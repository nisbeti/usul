And Abraham the Friend, peace be upon him, asked Allah the Exalted and said: {My Lord, show me how You give life to the dead} 2 Al-Qurtubi said, “He only asked for sight, because souls long to see what they have been told of; then he, peace be upon him, gave as the reason for his question reassurance.

Of this kind also is the question of both Zechariah and Mary, peace be upon them, when each was given the glad tidings of a child:

Zechariah: {He said, “My Lord, how shall I have a boy when old age has reached me and my wife is barren?”} 4.

And Mary: {She said, “My Lord, how shall I have a child when no man has touched me?”} 5.

Each of them was amazed and bewildered before Allah’s command and power, so their tongue moved to ask the Lord of the command — glory be to Him, He is capable of all things and knows all things.
