Secondly: What has been reported from the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) that he said, "So adhere to my Sunnah and the Sunnah of the rightly guided caliphs after me"(115)(*) narrated by Abu Dawood. Al-Albani said, and al-Tirmidhi said, "This is a good and authentic hadith," and al-‘Amrawi said, "This is among the best hadiths of the people of Sham," and al-Bazzar said, "This hadith is established and authentic," and al-Hakim said, "It is authentic and has no defect"(116).
(Response): First: A. The companions themselves did not understand from this hadith the obligation to follow the traditions of the four caliphs even if they contradicted the text. Al-Razi said, "They all agreed on the permissibility of opposing any one of the individual companions, for neither Abu Bakr nor Umar objected to those who opposed them, nor did either of them object to his companion in matters in which they disagreed"(117).
Thus, we say that whoever wishes to follow the Sunnah of the caliphs that they have all agreed upon should not object to those who oppose their Sunnah and method due to the emergence of the text that contradicts it for him.
