Non-chaste, meaning not chaste, said al-Samarqandi, “And the chaste women from those who were given the Scripture” means the chaste ones from the People of the Book(210).

Thus, Umar acted according to the texts at all times and did not contradict them at all; he believes that marrying women from the People of the Book is not forbidden, but when he feared the mixing of the lawful with the unlawful, which is their being from the prostitutes, due to the previous verse, and due to Allah's saying, “And the adulteress should not marry except an adulterer or a polytheist, and that is forbidden to the believers” (al-Nur: 123). He acted here according to the hadith, “Whoever avoids doubtful matters has cleared himself in regard to his religion and honor”(211). He disliked that, and may Allah have mercy on him, did not contradict any text at all, and may Allah grant success.

This is what we have thoroughly examined, and all praise is due to Allah. All that they have cited has no proof for them, as the Companions (may Allah be pleased with them) abandoned the text for the sake of public interest (maslaha), and if it were correct, they are far removed from that; it is personal ijtihad that does not rise to the level of being a proof. They are excused and rewarded, if Allah wills, and we are excused, if Allah wills, for abandoning their opinions for the saying of our leader (peace and blessings be upon him), and may Allah grant success.

The evidence of the Malikis and those who agreed with them on their doctrine:

The evidence of the Malikis in their theory that they adopted is mostly inductive evidence, not legal evidence from the Book and the Sunnah, and it is based on the actions of the Companions (may Allah be pleased with them), which have been previously addressed. Thus, among their evidences.
