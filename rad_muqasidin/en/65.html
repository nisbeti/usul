The following examples.

1- The opposition of Abu Bakr to Umar regarding the text:

Narrated by Abu Huraira (may Allah be pleased with him) who said: When the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) passed away and Abu Bakr succeeded him, and some of the Arabs became disbelievers, Umar said: "O Abu Bakr, how can you fight the people when the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said, 'I have been commanded to fight the people until they say there is no god but Allah. So whoever says there is no god but Allah has protected his wealth and himself from me, except for his rights, and his reckoning is with Allah.'" Abu Bakr replied: "By Allah, I will fight those who differentiate between prayer and zakat, for zakat is the right of wealth"(118). Here, Umar opposed the caliph with the text and the caliph responded to him with an understanding of the text as well, even though his opposition was not permissible, and he prioritized his opinion over the text; otherwise, he would have denied him that. Thus, we learned that the practice of Abu Bakr is to prioritize the text over Umar's opinion.

2- The opposition of a woman to Umar regarding the text:

In the presence of the companions, and no one objected to her, not even Umar himself, when he prohibited excessive dowries, the woman opposed him with the verse (And if you have given one of them a qintar, do not take anything from it) (119)(120). If Umar did not see the opposition of his opinion to the text as permissible, he would have denied her, but instead, Umar accepted her statement. Thus, we learned that the practice of Umar (may Allah be pleased with him) is to prioritize the text over the opinion of the caliphs, of which he is one.
