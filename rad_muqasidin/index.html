About the book

This book replies to those who treat the objectives of the Shari‘a (maqasid) as a free-standing proof that can override the texts. It sets out what the objectives mean for those who invoke them and why they do so, then takes their arguments for putting benefit (maslaha) first and answers them one by one.

It then gives al-Shatibi's understanding of the objectives, including his division of them into original and secondary objectives, and discusses the rulings that have been built on it.
