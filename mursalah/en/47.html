Seyf al-Din al-Amidi sees that the most accurate definition of suitability (munasaba)(108) is: "It is a clear, defined description that necessitates the arrangement of the ruling according to it, resulting in what can be deemed as the intended purpose of the legislation of the ruling." This definition differs from previous definitions in that it does not show a role for reason in establishing suitability, and consequently in establishing the cause, and thus in legislating through reason.

The differing understandings of the reality of suitability lead to differences and details regarding its acceptance as a principle in indicating causation or its rejection or elaboration. Therefore, we find that the imams of usul (principles of jurisprudence) mentioned categories of suitability: influential, appropriate, and strange.

Upon investigating the meaning of suitability, it becomes clear that it is used in relation to two meanings. The first: what reason cannot judge or determine, and it is only understood through the text or texts that indicate it. Suitability in this sense is the suitability between the ruling and the description, and it is the suitability that some imams have made one of the proofs of the causality of that description for that ruling. Suitability in this sense must be indicated by the Sharia, and the jurist or mujtahid must understand that indication from the text or texts. To further emphasize and clarify, this meaning does not entail that the jurist or mujtahid determines the suitability through his reason, as this is mere inclination and legislation through reason.

The second meaning of suitability is what reason can judge and determine - and this must be outside the matters of legislation, as legislation involves beautification and vilification, and reason has no role in them - or it may be something that it is permissible for a person to judge. This meaning has types; it may involve what can be referred to in terms of error and correctness, or what can be referred to in terms of custom or nature and temperament. An example of the first is entering into war with old weapons against an enemy using the latest types of weapons; it can be said that this is a mistake and inappropriate. The second is like visiting after midnight or during the time of the afternoon nap; this is also described as "inappropriate." The third is when it is said: this color matches this color or does not match it, or these grains...
