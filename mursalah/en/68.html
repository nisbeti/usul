As for our era, most writers on usul (principles of jurisprudence) or those addressing it hold the view of the authority of unregulated public interests. Among them, for example, are Sheikh Muhammad al-Khudari Bey(168), Jad al-Haq Ali Jad al-Haq(169), Muhammad Said Ramadan al-Bouti(170), Abdul Karim Zidan(171), Sheikh Abdul Wahab Khalaf(172), Muhammad Ibrahim al-Hafnawi(173), Khalifa Babikr al-Hassan(174), and Mustafa al-Khin(175).
