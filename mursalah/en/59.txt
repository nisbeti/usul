(136) These examples from (A) to (K) can be found in many books of usul (principles) that mention public interests (masalih mursala). You can find them, for instance, in al-Muwafaqat and al-I'tisam by al-Shatibi. They are also found in Sharh Tanqih al-Fusul by al-Qarafi. The last example was mentioned by Muhammad Said Ramadan al-Bouti in his book Al-Dhawabit al-Maslahah.
