The authority of public interest (maslaha murasala). Then after that, they attempt to cite some actions of the Companions (may Allah be pleased with them) as evidence that they were deriving rulings based on public interest (maslaha murasala).

As for the induction (istiqra) they speak of, al-Shatibi has provided examples of it, and I will present them as they appeared in his work. He says (119): "What is relied upon is that we have deduced from the Sharia that it was established for the interests of the servants, an induction that neither al-Razi nor anyone else disputes. For Allah, Exalted is He, says regarding the sending of the messengers, which is the basis: {Messengers as bringers of good tidings and warners, so that there will be no excuse for the people against Allah after the messengers}(120). {And We have not sent you, [O Muhammad], except as a mercy to the worlds}(121). And He said regarding the origin of creation: {It is He who created the heavens and the earth in six days, and His Throne was upon the water, that He might test you as to which of you is best in deed}(122). {And I did not create the jinn and mankind except to worship Me}(123). {Who created death and life to test you as to which of you is best in deed}(124) ... And if the induction indicates this, and it is beneficial for knowledge in such a matter, we conclude that the matter is continuous in all details of the Sharia..."(125). These are examples of the particulars deduced to indicate that the public interest (maslaha mursala) is a legal proof.

Similarly, one of the contemporary scholars (126) presents evidence for the consideration of public interests (maslaha) in the Sharia, and we will mention some of it. He says: "The saying of Allah, Exalted is He: {Indeed, Allah commands you to render trusts to whom they are due and when you judge between people to judge with justice. Excellent is that which Allah instructs you. Indeed, Allah is ever Hearing and Seeing.}"(127). He then says: "The essence of justice between two things or two persons is the balance and equivalence between them in a matter, so the aim is to consider moderation between the extremes of excess and deficiency in everything. The true organization of people's interests and their harmony with one another is nothing but a straight line that separates the extremes of excess and deficiency in their affairs.
