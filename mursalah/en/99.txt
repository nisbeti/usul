The religion is complete by the text of the Qur'an. The sources of our religion are all that was revealed from Allah to the Prophet Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him) as legislation for us. The estimation of public interests (maslaha) is not revelation, so making the unregulated interests (masalih mursala) a source is legislation without revelation. The claim that these actions taken based on public interest have not been legislated by the Sharia implies that the Sharia is incomplete. This contradicts His saying, Exalted is He: “Today I have completed for you your religion” {اليوم أكملت لكم دينكم}(270). And His saying: “We have not neglected in the Book anything” {ما فرطنا في الكتاب من شيء}(271). There is an intervention here, as some may say: These actions that we take based on public interest are indicated by the Sharia to be permissible through a general or comprehensive evidence, and the response is that if the Sharia indicates their permissibility, then they are not unregulated at that point, and they are taken based on the evidence that permitted them, not merely based on being a public interest.
