The prohibition, the actions of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) and his silence, and the condition of the one who derives evidence, meaning the knowledge of what ijtihad (independent juristic reasoning) is, and who the mujtahid (jurist) is, including the method of deriving evidence, such as balancing and weighing the evidence(2).
