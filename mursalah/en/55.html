By His saying, Exalted is He: “This is insight for the people and guidance and mercy for a people who are certain” {هذا بصائر للناس وهدىً ورحمة لقوم يوقنون}(128), and by His saying, Exalted is He: “And We have not sent you except as a mercy to the worlds” {وما أرسلناك إلا رحمة للعالمين}(129).

The summary of this explanation is that it can be said that the Legislator has established the genus of description within the genus of rulings, and since the description is the public interest (maslaha), which is a common description in all rulings, -according to their statement- they said that the Legislator has established the genus of public interest within the genus of rulings. The proof for the validity of the unregulated interests (masalih mursala) –which is the explanation we just mentioned- is that the Legislator has established the genus of interests within the genus of rulings.
