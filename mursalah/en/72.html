للتي هي أقوم)(183) This is what the texts inform us, and this is what occurred in reality when this Sharia was applied to the people, and there is nothing in these texts that indicates, even remotely, that interests (masalih) exist, or that the objectives of the legislator (maqasid al-sharia) are interests, let alone going further in the reasoning to say that if it is established that the matter is an interest, then it is Sharia. It is true that these objectives or goals or outcomes are, in reality, interests for the people, but it is not their being interests that indicates the Sharia or its correctness or acceptance. Rather, what indicates its correctness and acceptance as a whole is the creed (aqidah). And there is nothing in these texts that we have cited that indicates that interests are legislated because they are interests. Moreover, these texts inform us that our Sharia is a Sharia of mercy and healing and guidance and righteousness, and ... it does not clarify to us what mercy is or what righteousness is... Likewise, it is not at all indicated that interest is the basis of the Sharia, let alone that it is said to be the cause of the Sharia. Furthermore, the meaning that must be understood and nothing else when we say the Sharia is mercy, or the Sharia is guidance, or the Sharia is healing, is that the rulings that the Sharia has detailed and clarified are the mercy and the healing, and they are the righteousness and the justice, so they must be adhered to as they were revealed. And these texts do not indicate detailed rulings of the Sharia; rather, they inform us of the reality of the Sharia, that it is guidance, mercy, and healing, and the texts did not say that it is an interest, but rather this generalization of "interest" is a figurative expression from humans, because the Sharia can only be said to be an interest, goodness, and truth, and this is not based on studying its reality, but only based on it being from the Creator, the Sovereign.
