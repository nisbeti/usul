One can deduce a wisdom or multiple wisdoms, meaning that the error lies in the statement: "this is the wisdom." It is possible to say: "this is a wisdom," without introducing the definite article "the."
Accordingly, the objectives of the rulings must be indicated by the texts. Whatever the legislator has informed us about his intention therein, we accept it, whether it pertains to customs or acts of worship. Whatever the law indicates as being a reason, we consider it a legal reason, whether in customs or acts of worship. What the legislator has not informed us about regarding his intention or its reason, it is not valid to specify an objective for it or to provide a rationale.
