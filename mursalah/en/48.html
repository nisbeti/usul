Corresponding with each other to be in a single category. There may be matters that are difficult to place under a specific type, such as saying: "These two spouses complement each other," as the correspondence may pertain to their temperaments, inclinations, interests, or other factors.

This second meaning of correspondence does not aid in reasoning because it is determined solely by the intellect. Thus, the cause is an intellectual cause, and the ruling is an intellectual ruling. The cause is neither legal nor is the ruling legal.

The jurists have agreed to consider the correspondence we mentioned in the first meaning. It is the correspondence indicated by the Sharia. Or as they said: the correspondence that the Sharia has acknowledged as valid. As for the correspondence that the Sharia has not acknowledged as valid, it falls under the second type, that is, correspondence by the judgment of the intellect alone. This is divided into two categories: one category that the Sharia has acknowledged its annulment, and this is unanimously rejected. The second category has not been acknowledged by the Sharia either as valid or annulled, and there is a difference of opinion regarding it. Therefore, some have argued that the criterion is not the correspondence, as it often has no effect on the ruling. The term "strange correspondence" has been used for that which is non-influential. Ibn Taymiyyah said(109): "If the effect of the attribute on the ruling of the original is known through deduction and the attribute is suitable, then either its effect on something other than the original is known through text or consensus, or its effect on something other than the original is not known. The first is the influential and suitable correspondence, and the second is the strange." He then said(110): "The words of the judge and the Iraqis imply that they do not rely on strange correspondence and they rely on the influential, whether it is suitable or not." This indicates that correspondence is sometimes considered and sometimes not, and that the influential is always considered. This is because the correspondence may be indicated by the Sharia - as Ibn Taymiyyah said, through text or consensus - and thus it is considered, or it may be purely by intellect and thus it is not considered.
