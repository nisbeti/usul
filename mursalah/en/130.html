It adopts it. These fatwas are not of the kind of correct legal reasoning (ijtihad), but rather legislation from outside of revelation.

The permissibility of usury (riba), for example, cannot be said to be an Islamic ijtihad opinion for which its proponent is excused, because usury has a definitive text that is certain in its authenticity and meaning, firm, not abrogated, and not specified or restricted: "And He has forbidden usury"(319). Likewise, participation in ruling by disbelief has definitive texts with no room for ijtihad. Allah, Exalted is He, said: "And whoever does not judge by what Allah has revealed, then it is they who are the wrongdoers"(320), and He said: "the disbelievers" and He said: "the wicked". So whoever rules or participates in ruling by disbelief is wicked with interpretation, and a disbeliever if we do not interpret for him. And the one who issues a fatwa permitting it based on public interest (maslaha) is fabricating lies against Allah.

Such rulings are not of the kind of permissible disagreement in opinion, but rather they are a form of misleading in religion, and taking legal rulings from outside the religion of Islam (Have you not seen those who claim to believe in what has been revealed to you and what was revealed before you, wanting to refer to Taghut while they were commanded to disbelieve in it, and Satan wants to lead them astray into a far astray) (321).
