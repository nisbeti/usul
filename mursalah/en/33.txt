He says(64): "For example, the five objectives that they say have not been neglected by any community or law, which are the preservation of religion, life, intellect, lineage, and wealth, are not all that is necessary for society as a society. The preservation of the state, the preservation of security, and the preservation of human dignity are also necessities of society. Therefore, the necessities in reality are not five, but rather eight. As for the legal rulings for which they were legislated, religions differ in their perspectives on one another... So how can these be the objectives of every community?" He distinguishes between the objectives of the Sharia (Islamic law) and the objectives of certain rulings, saying(65): "These objectives of the Sharia are the objectives of the Sharia as a whole, that is, the objectives of the Islamic religion, and they are not the objectives of every specific ruling." He states(66): "Thus, we find that the legislator, at the same time that he clarified the objectives of the Sharia as a whole, also clarified his objective in legislating certain specific rulings, as he said regarding the creation of jinn and humans: 'And I did not create the jinn and mankind except to worship Me,' and he said otherwise in many rulings... So, the objective of Allah in legislating a specific ruling is not His objective for the Sharia as a whole, nor is His objective for the Sharia as a whole His objective for every specific ruling."

We have mentioned here the statements regarding the objectives due to their role or because they serve as a prelude to the discussion of public interest (maslaha), as they say that these objectives indicate through induction that they bring about benefits and avert harms, and then they count them as reasons for the Sharia and for the rulings, legislating rulings based on the benefits. Therefore, I will not discuss the objectives here in terms of their division into necessary, need-based, and embellishing, but rather in terms of whether they are primarily about bringing benefits and averting harms. Are they reasons for the Sharia and for the rulings or not?

The objectives of the Sharia are its goals, that is, the results of its application.
