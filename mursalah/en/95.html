It indicates the permissibility of abandoning the ruling of the Sharia for the sake of public interest (maslaha). The support of the Companions for Umar becomes a consensus on the permissibility of abandoning the Sharia for the sake of public interest. This type of understanding is barren and flawed. The reality of the matter is that the spoils of war (anfal), the gains (ghanimah), and the revenue (fay') are matters entrusted to the imam, who acts according to his opinion based on the interest of the Muslims. The land of the black soil (Ard al-Sawad) in Iraq, Sham, and Egypt is a matter entrusted to the caliph; if he wishes, he can divide it and distribute it, and if he wishes, he can withhold it. Thus, the action is originally permissible according to the evidence of the Sharia, not based on the argument of public interest, rather the public interest or what is better is the desired outcome through the choice among the permissible actions. Then, when Umar (may Allah be pleased with him) consulted the Companions and sought their opinions, he presented his view and supported his action with the following verses: {What Allah has given as booty to His Messenger from the people of the towns, it is for Allah and for the Messenger and for [his] near relatives and the orphans, the needy, and the traveler, so that it will not be a perpetual distribution among the rich among you. And whatever the Messenger has given you - take it; and what he has forbidden you - refrain from it. And fear Allah; indeed, Allah is severe in penalty} {7} {For the poor emigrants who were expelled from their homes and their properties, seeking bounty from Allah and [His] pleasure and supporting Allah and His Messenger. Those are the truly righteous} {8} {And [also for] those who were settled in the abode and [in faith] before them. They love those who emigrated to them and find not in their chests any need of what they were given, but give [them] preference over themselves, even though they are in privation. And whoever is protected from the stinginess of his soul - it is those who will be the successful} {9} {And those who came after them say, "Our Lord, forgive us and our brothers who preceded us in faith and put not in our hearts any resentment toward those who have believed. Our Lord, indeed You are Kind and Merciful"} {10}(257). Then he said: "This verse encompasses all Muslims in general – referring to His saying: {And those who came after them} – and if I live, the shepherd will come, and he will receive his share from it without having sweated for it"(258).

Thus, Umar ibn al-Khattab (may Allah be pleased with him) used the Quran to justify his action, and he chose a permissible action from among several permissible actions, but he selected what was better because he is the caliph of the Muslims, and it is obligatory upon him to advise them. The evidence for the permissibility of the action and the permissibility of other actions is the action of the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him), for he divided the land in Khaybar among the fighters. However, before that, when he opened the land of Banu al-Nadir, he did not divide the land.
