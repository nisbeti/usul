For absolute takfeer ( saing that whoever does this thing is disbeliver )  , like an absolute threatening, does not require the  accusing of the appointed person of being kafer (disbeliver) until the proof is based on the one who denies it.
As it was proven in the Sahih on the authority of the Prophet, peace be upon him, who said ((one man said   that if I died, then   burn me and then crush me, then shed me in the sea, if God is able to torture me,  he will torture me with a torture he does not torture anyone from the worlds. Then God said to him: What makes you did what u did?  he said Your fear, so  he forgave him))
This man believed that God could not collect him if he did that. or doubted that, and that he did not resurect him and each of these two beliefs is a disbelief (kofr), a one will be disbeliever  if  the argument is based on him, but because  he was ignorant of that and had no knowledge about that which can  makes him away of his ignorance  .and had faith in God and his command and forbidding and his promise and his threatening  and feared his punishment so God forgave him because of  His fear
He whoever  has erred in some matters of belief from the people of faith in God and His Messenger and on the other day and have  righteous deed is not worse than this  man then God will forgive his mistake. or torment him if he neglects to follow the truth according to the extent of his religion.  As for saying  that  an appointed  person who known  that he is a believer is disbeliever  simply because of bmaking a mistake , it is a  great(sin).
It was proven in Al-Sahih on the authority of Thabet Bin Al-Dahhak on the authority of the Prophet, PBUH. He said: The curse of the believer is like his murder, and whoever throws a believer in disbelief(saying that he is kafer) is like killing him((
