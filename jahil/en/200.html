("The tip of his sword") means its sharp edge. ("His deeds were rendered void") means that Amir's deeds were nullified because he killed himself with his sword. ("He would strive") means that he would exert himself in obedience. ("A fighter") means one who fights in the path of Allah, Exalted is He. ("By it") means by this praiseworthy quality, namely, striving in jihad with exertion. ("He grew up") means he matured and reached adulthood.

In this hadith, the Companions ruled that Amir's deeds had been nullified, based on their interpretation. This was a matter of belief, yet the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) did not declare them unbelievers. Perhaps this was because they could have inferred it from general texts such as: "Whoever kills himself with an iron weapon will have that weapon in his hand, stabbing himself in the belly with it, abiding therein forever." They thus inferred, on the basis of their interpretation, that one who kills himself is an unbeliever and that his deeds are nullified, and applied this ruling to Amir.

Their saying, "We are doomed, by the Lord of the Kaaba: hypocrisy, hypocrisy," in the hadith

Imam Abu Ya'la, may Allah have mercy on him, said (vol. 6, p. 58): Abd al-Wahid narrated to us; Ghassan ibn Barzin, meaning al-Tahawi, narrated to us; Thabit al-Bunani narrated to us, from Anas ibn Malik, who said: One day, the Companions of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him and his family) came in the morning and said, "Messenger of Allah, we are doomed, by the Lord of the Kaaba!" He said, "What is the matter?" They said, "Hypocrisy, hypocrisy." He said, "Do you not testify that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner, and that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger?" They said, "Yes." He said, "That is not hypocrisy." He said: Then they came back a second time and said, "Messenger of Allah, we are doomed, by the Lord of the Kaaba!" He said, "What is the matter?" They said, "Hypocrisy, hypocrisy." He said, "Do you not testify that there is no god but Allah and that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger?" [p. 81] They said, "Yes." He said, "That is not hypocrisy." He said: Then they came back a third time and said, "Messenger of Allah, we are doomed, by the Lord of the Kaaba!"
