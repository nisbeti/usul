Abu Muhammad said: The majority of those who use this report as evidence do not declare a person to be an unbeliever for saying to a Muslim, "You unbeliever," during an exchange of insults between them. In this, they have contradicted the report they cited as evidence.
Abu Muhammad said: The truth is that anyone whose status as a Muslim has been established does not cease to be one except by a text or consensus. Mere claims and false accusations do not suffice. Therefore, no one should be declared an unbeliever for something he has said unless he contradicts what he knows to have been said by Allah, Exalted is He, or by the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), and thus deems it permissible to oppose Allah, Exalted is He, and His Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him). This applies equally whether the matter concerns a religious belief, a sect, or a legal opinion, and whether what is authentically reported from the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) has been transmitted by mass transmission establishing consensus or by solitary reports. However, one who opposes a consensus whose authenticity is certain and definitive is more clearly subject to the severing of his proof and the obligation to declare him an unbeliever, because everyone agrees that the consensus is known and that one who opposes it is to be declared an unbeliever. The proof that our position is correct is the saying of Allah, Exalted is He: {Whoever opposes the Messenger after guidance has become clear to him, and follows a path other than that of the believers, We shall leave him to what he has chosen and admit him to Hell—and what an evil destination.}
Statements of those who say that ignorance is no excuse:
Ibn Jarir, may Allah have mercy on him, said in his commentary on the saying of Allah, Exalted is He, in Surat al-A'raf: "A group He guided, while another group deserved misguidance. They took the devils as allies instead of Allah, while thinking that they were rightly guided." He said: The group that deserved misguidance strayed from Allah's path and deviated from the straight way by taking the devils as supporters and allies instead of Allah, out of ignorance of the error in what they were doing.
