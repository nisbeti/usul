The blameworthy kind, as we have explained. We find that He said: {We send the messengers only as bearers of good news and warners. Those who disbelieve argue with falsehood in order to refute the truth by it, and they take My signs and the warnings given to them in mockery.} **
Thus, as you see, Exalted is He condemned arguing without proof and arguing for falsehood. In this way, Exalted is He refuted the claim of the deluded: that every person led astray is furnished with an argument. Exalted is He made clear that the deluded person is one who is not furnished with an argument, whereas the one in the right is truly furnished with an argument—and they are the people of truth. Exalted is He also said: {Those who argue about the signs of Allah without any authority having come to them—it is greatly hateful in the sight of Allah and in the sight of those who believe. Thus does Allah seal the heart of every arrogant tyrant.} These verses together explain both blameworthy argumentation and praiseworthy, obligatory argumentation. The obligatory kind is that in which the person arguing seeks to make the truth manifest. The blameworthy kind has two forms, as stated in the verses we have cited: first, arguing without knowledge; and second, arguing in support of falsehood through contentiousness and deception after the truth has become clear to him. This makes clear that the truth is one, and that nothing is valid except what is established by rational proof. These blameworthy people are those of whom Exalted is He said: {Have you not seen those who argue about the signs of Allah—how they are turned away?} And His saying: {Among the people is one who argues about Allah without knowledge and follows every rebellious devil.} And His saying: {Among the people is one who argues about Allah without knowledge, guidance, or an illuminating Book, turning his neck aside to lead others astray from the path of Allah. For him there is disgrace in this world, and on the Day of Resurrection We shall make him taste the punishment of the Fire.} And His saying:
Exalted is He: {None argue about the signs of Allah except those who disbelieve, so let not their movement through the lands deceive you. Before them, the people of Noah and the factions after them denied the truth,

and every nation plotted against its messenger to seize him, and they argued with falsehood in order to refute the truth by it. So I seized them; and how was My punishment?} Thus, as you see, Exalted is He made clear that forbidden argumentation is argumentation used to support falsehood and refute the truth without knowledge.))
