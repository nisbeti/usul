I said, "What are they?"
They said: As for one of them, he appointed men to judge in a matter belonging to Allah, while Allah says, {Judgment belongs to Allah alone} (1). What have men to do with judgment?!
I said, "This is one point."
They said, "As for the second: he fought, but took no captives and seized no spoils. If they were unbelievers, taking them captive would have been lawful; but if they were believers, neither taking them captive nor fighting them would have been lawful."
I said, "Those are two; what is the third?"
They said, "He erased from himself the words 'Commander of the Faithful'; if he is not the Commander of the Faithful, then he is the Commander of the Unbelievers!!"
I said, "Do you have anything besides this?"
They said, "This is enough for us."
I said to them, "What do you think: if I recite to you from the Book of Allah, Glorious is His praise, and the Sunnah of His Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) something that refutes your position, will you recant?"
They said, "Yes."
