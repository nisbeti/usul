For the Sunnah serves as an explanation of the Book and a clarification of what is intended; it is what makes known to us what Allah intends by His Book. If it were possible for it to be false and mistaken, Allah's proof against His servants would be invalidated. Anyone confronted with a Sunnah that clarifies and explains the Quran could say, "This is a solitary report that does not yield knowledge, so no proof can be established against me by something that does not yield knowledge." This is the inevitable consequence of this corrupt doctrine, and those who carry it through most consistently are the furthest from knowledge and faith.

What is truly astonishing is that they refuse to rely on the reports of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), claiming that they do not yield knowledge, yet they rely on mental fancies and false doubts that they received from the people of philosophy, Jahmism, and Mu'tazilism, and claim that these are rational proofs.

Ibn al-Qayyim quoted Ibn Hazm: "The eleventh proof: Those who deny that the reports of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) yield knowledge bear decisive, categorical testimony concerning their imams and their doctrines and statements, affirming that they said such things. Were someone to say that these statements had not been authentically transmitted from them, they would reject that most vehemently and marvel at the ignorance of whoever said it. Yet it is well known that those doctrines were transmitted from them only by one, two, or three people, or the like; they were not transmitted from them by a number sufficient to establish mass transmission. This is known with certainty.

How, then, do they attain necessary knowledge, or knowledge approaching the necessary, that their imams and those whose religion they followed issued such-and-such a legal opinion and held such-and-such a view, while they do not attain it from what Abu Bakr al-Siddiq, Umar ibn al-Khattab, and all the Companions reported from the Messenger of Allah"
