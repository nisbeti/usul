Those who are capable of understanding the truth are also of two kinds. The first seeks guidance, prefers and loves it, but is unable to attain it or seek it because there is no one to guide him. His case is like that of those who lived in an interval between prophets and those to whom the message did not reach. The second turns away, has no desire for guidance, and does not even consider anything other than the way he is on. The first says, "My Lord, if I knew of a religion better for You than the one I follow, I would follow it and abandon what I am on. But I know nothing other than what I am on, and I am unable to do anything else. This is the utmost of my effort and the limit of my knowledge." The second is content with what he is on, prefers nothing else to it, and seeks nothing beyond it. To him, there is no difference between being unable and being able; both are equally beyond his reach. The latter should not be classed with the former, given the difference between them: the first is like someone who sought the religion during an interval between prophets but did not find it, and so turned away from it after exhausting every effort to seek it, out of inability and ignorance; the second is like someone who did not seek it at all, but died in his polytheism, even though he would have been unable to attain it had he sought it. There is a difference, then, between the inability of one who seeks and the inability of one who turns away. Reflect on this point. Allah will judge His servants on the Day of Resurrection with His judgment and justice, and He will punish only those against whom His proof has been established through the messengers. This is certain with regard to people in general. As for whether the proof was established against a particular person, Zayd or Amr, that is not something one can intrude upon between Allah and His servants. Rather, a servant must believe that everyone who follows a religion other than Islam is an unbeliever, and that Allah, Exalted is He, does not punish anyone until the proof has been established against him through a messenger.
