10. We did not create the heavens and the earth and all that is between them except in truth and for an appointed term. Yet those who disbelieve turn away from what they have been warned of. Say, "Consider those whom you invoke besides Allah: show me what they have created of the earth, or do they have any share in the heavens? Bring me a scripture from before this, or some remnant of knowledge, if you are truthful."
The verse indicates that whoever offers no proof or knowledge for what he says is not truthful. And since there are only the truthful and the liars in this world, he is a liar in what he claims and is not excused.
11. Say, "Consider: if this is from Allah and you disbelieve in it, while a witness from among the Children of Israel testifies to something like it and believes, whereas you are arrogant—indeed, Allah does not guide the wrongdoing people."
