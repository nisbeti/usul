Despite the fame of this hadith, their transmitting and narrating it, and its having reached them as well, al-Ashari explicitly stated, "All the Kharijites say that the Quran is created" [Maqalat al-Islamiyyin, 108]. I will cite the hadith in full; it is a hasan hadith, to clarify their doctrine.
The Imam, hadith master Abu Abd al-Rahman al-Nasa'i, said: Amr ibn Ali informed us, saying: Abd al-Rahman ibn Mahdi narrated to us, saying: Ikrima ibn Ammar narrated to us, saying: Abu Zumayl told me, saying: Abdullah ibn Abbas told me, saying:
"When the Haruriyya went out, they withdrew to a house. They numbered six thousand. I said to Ali, 'Commander of the Faithful, delay the prayer until it is cooler; perhaps I can speak with these people.'
He said, 'I fear they may harm you.'
I said, 'No.' So I put on my clothes, combed my hair, and went in to them at the house at midday, while they were resting. They said, 'Welcome, Ibn Abbas! What brings you here?'
I said to them, 'I have come to you from the Companions of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him)—the Emigrants and the Helpers—and from the Prophet's cousin and son-in-law. The Quran was revealed among them, so they know its interpretation better than you do, and there is not one of them among you. I have come to convey to you what they say and to convey to them what you say.'
A group of them stepped aside to speak with me.
I said, 'Tell me what you object to in the Companions of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) and his cousin.'
They said, 'Three things.'
