Is it not less harmful to help a disbeliever by voting for him, in order to free Muslim prisoners or prevent them from being killed and imprisoned? But in this matter, people must be made aware that voting for him is not so that he may rule by what Allah has revealed. Rather, the reason for voting is to reduce harm to Muslims, free their prisoners, and stop them from being killed. This is so that people do not think that such voting is inherently permissible under Islamic law. If it is said that someone might see it and think the act is permissible, I say: the same danger exists in the case of someone who fought in the army of the polytheists, yet most scholars permitted him to ransom himself in this way. They likewise permitted uttering explicit words of disbelief under coercion. Therefore, people must be alerted that this vote is only to remove a state of necessity, while also explaining Allah's ruling on these matters. And Allah knows best.
