Upon him, and no two people on earth disagree—not merely among the Muslims, but among people of every religion—that the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) declared as unbelievers the adherents of every religion other than Islam, whose people disavowed every religion except the one he brought, peace be upon him. So we stopped at that. Nor do two people disagree that he, peace be upon him, designated as believers all who followed him, affirmed everything he brought, and disavowed every religion besides that. So we likewise stopped at that, and there is nothing more. Thus, anyone for whom there is an explicit text establishing that he has left Islam, after the designation of Islam had applied to him, we declare outside it, whether or not there is consensus that he has left it. Likewise, if the Muslims unanimously agree that someone has left Islam, it is obligatory to follow the consensus in that matter. As for someone whose departure from Islam, after Islam had applied to him, is established neither by an explicit text nor by consensus, it is not permissible to remove him from what has been established with certainty as applying to him. Allah Most High has explicitly stated what we have said, saying: {Whoever seeks a religion other than Islam, it will never be accepted from him, and in the Hereafter he will be among the losers.} And He Most High says: {They wish to make a distinction between Allah and His messengers, and say, "We believe in some and disbelieve in others," and wish to adopt a way in between. Those are truly the unbelievers.} And He Most High says: {Say, "Was it Allah, His signs, and His Messenger that you were mocking? Make no excuses; you have disbelieved after your faith."} All of these people are unbelievers by explicit text. It is established by consensus that anyone who denies something which, as we know by consensus, the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) brought has disbelieved.
