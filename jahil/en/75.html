The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said, "Erase it, Ali. O Allah, You know that I am the Messenger of Allah. Erase it, Ali, and write: This is what Muhammad ibn Abd Allah agreed to."

By Allah, the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) is better than Ali. He erased his own name, but erasing his name did not erase him from prophethood. Have you drawn this conclusion from that?

They said, "Yes."

Two thousand of them then turned back, while the rest went out and were killed in their misguidance; the Emigrants and the Helpers killed them."

Good. It was transmitted by al-Nasa'i in al-Sunan al-Kubra (5/165–167/8575), or no. 190 in al-Khasa'is; Ahmad in al-Musnad (1/342), or no. 3187 (Shakir)—in abridged form; Abu Dawud (4037)—in abridged form, and this wording is not found in his version; Abd al-Razzaq in al-Musannaf (10/157/18678); al-Hakim (2/150) and (4/182); al-Bayhaqi in al-Sunan al-Kubra (8/179); al-Tabarani in al-Mu'jam al-Kabir (10, no. 15098); Ibn Abd al-Barr in Jami Bayan al-Ilm (2/962/1834); Abu Nuaym in Hilyat al-Awliya (1/318–320); al-Fasawi in al-Marifa wa al-Tarikh (1/522–524); al-Khwarizmi in al-Manaqib (244); Abu Ubayd al-Qasim ibn Sallam in al-Amwal (no. 444); Ibn al-Jawzi in Talbis Iblis (pp. 91–93); and al-Muafa al-Nahrawani in al-Jalis al-Salih (1/558–560, Alam al-Kutub).
