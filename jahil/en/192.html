Shaykh al-Islam said: The wording "we affirm" means that we attest to its meaning, not that we testify about a specific individual. Otherwise, he said, "We know that it is as it has come down," and this entails that it conveys knowledge.

Moreover, it is part of his foundational position that he testifies that the ten are in Paradise, on the basis of the report that has been transmitted; and that report is a solitary report. He said, "I testify" and "I know" as one and the same thing. This is evidence that he testifies on the basis of what a solitary report entails, although Ibn al-Madini and others disagreed with him.

Ibn al-Madini said: They say, "The reports of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) convey knowledge." Allah, Exalted is He, says: {He does not speak from his own desire; it is only revelation sent down to him} [al-Najm: 3].

And He, Exalted is He, said, commanding His Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) to say: {I follow only what is revealed to me} [al-An'am: 50]. And He, Exalted is He, said: {Indeed, We have sent down the Reminder, and indeed, We will preserve it} [al-Hijr: 9]. And He, Exalted is He, said: {And We sent down the Reminder to you so that you may make clear to people what was sent down to them} [al-Nahl: 44]. They said: It is thus known that all of the Messenger of Allah's (peace and blessings be upon him) speech concerning religion is revelation from Allah, and every revelation from Allah is a Reminder that Allah sent down. Allah, Exalted is He, said: {And Allah sent down to you the Book and the Wisdom} [al-Nisa: 113]. The Book is the Quran, and the Wisdom is the Sunnah. The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said: "I have been given the Book and something like it along with it." He thus informed us that he was given the Sunnah just as he was given the Book. Allah, Exalted is He, has guaranteed the preservation of what He revealed to him and sent down upon him, so that through it He may establish His proof against His servants until the end of time. They said: If it were possible for these reports to be false, they would not be from Allah, nor would they be among what He sent down.
