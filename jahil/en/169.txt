A ruling is made on the basis of evidence and the testimony of one upright witness, two upright witnesses, or upright witnesses, according to us, even if in reality they are liars or mistaken. A judgment based on any of this is true in the sight of Allah, Exalted is He, and, in our view, its unseen reality is beyond our knowledge. The proof of this is that if two people bring a dispute before a judge, and the claimant has no evidence, but the judge does not rule in favor of the defendant on the basis of an oath; or if two upright witnesses testify before him, but he does not rule on the basis of their testimony, then that judge is a sinner, disobedient to Allah, Exalted is He, whose testimony is discredited, and an oppressor—whether the defendant is wrong in his denial or in the right, or the witnesses are liars, mistaken, or truthful, so long as he does not know the reality of their case. We are certainly commanded by Allah, Exalted is He, to kill this innocent person against whom false testimony has been given, and to declare lawful this inviolable person and this unlawful property concerning which false testimony has been given. It is forbidden for the wrongdoer to take any of that. Our Lord has decreed that if we do not rule accordingly, we are sinners and disobedient to Him in religion, oppressors, threatened with the Fire for doing so. Allah, Exalted is He, has never commanded us to rule in religion on the basis of a report fabricated by a sinner or misunderstood by someone who is mistaken. Allah, Exalted is He, said: {O you who believe, do not follow the footsteps of Satan, and whoever}
"...follows the footsteps of Satan, for he commands indecency and wrongdoing. Were it not for Allah's grace and His mercy upon you, none of you would ever have been purified; but Allah purifies whom He wills, and Allah is All-Hearing, All-Knowing."} This is a distinction of the utmost clarity. A third distinction is that we say: Allah, Exalted is He, has made it obligatory upon us to say, throughout the entire Sharia, "The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said," and "Allah, Exalted is He, has commanded such-and-such," because He, Exalted is He, says: {O you who believe, obey Allah and obey the Messenger and those in authority among you.
