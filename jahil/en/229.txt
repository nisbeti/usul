6—Why did they not produce four witnesses to it? Since they did not produce the witnesses, they are, in the sight of Allah, the liars. (13) Were it not for Allah's grace and mercy upon you in this world and the Hereafter, a tremendous punishment would have touched you for what you plunged into. (14)
7—When you passed it from tongue to tongue, and said with your mouths what you had no knowledge of, thinking it a trivial matter, while in the sight of Allah it was tremendous. (15)
This verse indicates that whoever speaks without knowledge has committed a grave matter and is not excused.
8—Or have We sent down to them an authority, so that it speaks of what they used to associate with Him?
9—And they made the angels, who are servants of the Most Merciful, females. Did they witness their creation? Their testimony will be recorded, and they will be questioned. (19)
