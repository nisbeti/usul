The Sunnah of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) is sufficient for this, and no one is excused for rejecting it. Otherwise, Allah would have excused these people and would not have struck them down.

13. And if they see a sign, they turn away and say, "An ongoing magic."

This verse is likewise relevant.

14. Or do they say, "He has fabricated it"? Say, "Then bring ten fabricated surahs like it, and call upon whomever you can besides Allah, if you are truthful." But if they do not respond to you, then know that it has been sent down only with Allah's knowledge, and that there is no god but He. Will you then submit?

Here Allah, Exalted is He, made the man's truthfulness contingent upon producing countervailing evidence. This shows that whoever is unable to do so and fails to produce countervailing evidence is lying in his claim and following his own desire.
