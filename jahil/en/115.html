Whoever says that he is created is an unbeliever; whoever does not declare him an unbeliever is an unbeliever; and whoever doubts his unbelief is an unbeliever."

The Statement of Ahmad ibn Hanbal: Imam Ahmad, may Allah have mercy on him, said: "Whoever says that the Quran is created is an unbeliever, and whoever doubts that he is an unbeliever is an unbeliever." [Tabaqat al-Hanabila (1/461)] He also said: "The Quran is the speech of Allah; He spoke it, and it is not created. Whoever claims that the Quran is created is a Jahmi unbeliever. Whoever claims that the Quran is the speech of Allah but stops short and does not say that it is not created is more vile than the first statement. Whoever claims that our utterances of it and our recitation of it are created, while the Quran is the speech of Allah, is a Jahmi. Whoever does not declare these people unbelievers is like them." (Weak) [Usul al-Sunna, as narrated by Abdus al-Attar(42), printed with al-Aqida, as narrated by Abu Bakr al-Khallal, p. 79, Dar Qutayba edition, Damascus, 1408 AH / 1988 CE]

__________

(42) He is Abdus ibn Malik al-Attar, Abu Muhammad. Abu Bakr al-Khallal mentioned him, saying: "He held a high standing with Abu Abd Allah Ahmad ibn Hanbal, in matters of gifts and otherwise. He was very close to him, and Ahmad would give him precedence. He narrated from Abu Abd Allah legal questions that no one else narrated, but not all of them reached us. He died before they were transmitted from him."

Tabaqat al-Hanabila by Ibn Abi Ya'la (338)
