And He commanded us to do such-and-such, but the Exalted never commanded us to say, "This witness has spoken truthfully," or, "This party has sworn truthfully," or, "What we have ruled in favor of this person is certainly his right." Nor did the Exalted say, "This witness has spoken the truth." Rather, the Exalted said to us: "Judge according to the testimony of upright witnesses and the oath of the defendant when no proof has been brought against him." This is a distinction that is unmistakable. Thus, in none of this have we judged on the basis of conjecture at all, praise be to Allah. Rather, we have judged with decisive knowledge and firm certainty that everything we have ruled upon on the basis of a report transmitted by an upright narrator from another upright narrator, back to the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), is truth from the Exalted Allah, revealed by our Lord, the Exalted, and attributed to the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), as something he is reported to have said. And everything we have ruled upon on the basis of testimony from witnesses we deem upright is a truth established with certainty from the Exalted Allah, because He commanded us to judge by it. He did not command us to say, regarding what they testified to or what the person taking an oath swore, that it is from the Exalted Allah or that it is a truth established with certainty. If they say, "The Exalted said only, {O you who believe, avoid much suspicion; indeed, some suspicion is sin}, and He did not say that all suspicion is sin," we reply: Allah, the Exalted, has made clear the distinction between sin and righteousness: to speak about Him without knowledge is forbidden. This is undoubtedly a kind of suspicion that is sin. Ali said: The Mu'tazilites resorted to refusing to rule on the basis of a solitary report, because of the arguments we have mentioned. They thought that by doing so they had escaped the problem, but they have not escaped it. Rather, everything we have shown to be incumbent upon others is incumbent upon them as well.
