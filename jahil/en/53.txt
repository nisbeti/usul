Whoever does not return to the truth he hears is among the people of the Fire. Allah, Exalted is He, says: {If they do not respond to you, then know that they are only following their desires. And who is more astray than one who follows his desire without guidance from Allah? Indeed, Allah does not guide the wrongdoing people.} Allah, Exalted is He, also condemned a person for denying what he does not know, saying: {Rather, they denied what they had no knowledge of, and whose interpretation had not yet come to them. Thus did those before them deny. So see what was the end of the wrongdoers.} Thus, all that we have mentioned establishes the duty to refrain from what we do not know and to return to what the proof requires once it has been established.

Allah, Exalted is He, says: {And who is more unjust than one who fabricates a lie against Allah or denies the truth when it comes to him? Is there not a dwelling in Hell for the disbelievers?} Abu Muhammad said: This verse suffices to establish that no one should affirm anything unless proof has been established for it, and that no one should do anything when proof has been established against it. Who is more unjust than one who knows what we have mentioned, yet follows a whisper arising within himself, or a report for which no proof has been established that it must be believed, or imitates another person like himself, who may, in Allah's sight, be mistaken in what he thinks? In any case, that person is not infallible; he may be right or wrong. Allah, Exalted is He, says: {And they say, "None will enter Paradise unless he is a Jew or a Christian." Those are their wishes. Say, "Produce your proof, if you are truthful."}
Thus, He, Exalted is He, required whoever is truthful in his claim to produce proof; if he does not produce proof,
then he is a liar, one who asserts falsehood, or an ignorant person. Allah, Exalted is He, says: {Here you are, disputing about that of which you have knowledge; why, then, do you dispute about that of which you have no knowledge? Allah knows, and you do not know.} Thus, He, Exalted is He, required disputation only on the basis of knowledge and prohibited it without knowledge.

Allah, Exalted is He, says: {They will say, "They were three, the fourth of them being their dog," and they will say, "They were five, the sixth of them being their dog," guessing at the unseen. And they will say, "They were seven, and the eighth of them was their dog." Say, "My Lord knows best their number; none knows them except a few." So do not argue about them except with an evident argument, and do not ask any of them about them.} Abu Muhammad said: Since we found that Allah, Exalted is He, commanded argument and debate in the verses we have mentioned, and did not require acceptance of anything except on the basis of proof, we were obliged to seek out arguments.
