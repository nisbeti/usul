If we are certain of his uprightness, we are certain that his report is sound; and if his discredit is established for us, we are certain that his report is invalid. If neither of these is established for us, we suspend judgment on the matter, while being absolutely certain that someone else must have established one of the two concerning him. Our error, if we err, and our ignorance, if we are ignorant, cannot be proof that Allah's religion must be lost. Rather, the truth is established and known to one group, even if another is ignorant of it; and likewise with falsehood, just as some people are ignorant of what we know. Grace is in Allah's hand; He grants it to whomever He wills. An error in a trustworthy narrator's report can be established only in one of three ways: either the narrator verifies it and admits that he made an error in it; or a reliable person testifies that he heard the report alongside its narrator and that so-and-so made a mistake in it; or direct observation makes it certain that he made an error. Ali said: Likewise, we are certain and decisive regarding any two sound reports that conflict, any two verses that conflict, any verse and sound report that conflict, and any two conflicting texts for which no explicit text has established that one abrogates the other: the ruling that adds to the ruling established by the original precedent is the abrogating one, while the ruling that accords with the original precedent is certainly and unquestionably the abrogated one. This follows from the proofs we have already presented: that the religion is preserved. For if it were possible for an abrogating text to be concealed among abrogated ones, or for there to be a general text that no sound text specifies, while its intended meaning is particular, then the religion would not be preserved, the proof in the Sharia would not be binding on anyone, and we would be charged with acting on false conjecture, which is forbidden, indeed with acting on something Allah, Exalted is He, never commanded. This is certainly false. Ali said: If one day it is found that we have said otherwise, then we repent to Allah, Exalted is He, for it. It is a slip for which we ask Allah, Mighty and Majestic, to forgive us, and we hope that no such thing will be found in our words.
