Its implied wording is: "Allah has indeed led them astray; would you then guide those whom Allah has led astray?" If this interpretation is sound, this verse is as explicit as a text in excusing one who offers an excuse on the basis of a mistaken interpretation. These people merely supposed that someone who was in fact an unbeliever was a Muslim whom it was impermissible to fight, interpreting his outward circumstances as indicating this. Yet neither Allah nor His Messenger declared them unbelievers. It is authentically reported that he (peace and blessings be upon him) was asked about the believers' seeing their Lord, Blessed and Exalted is He. He said, "Do you have any difficulty seeing the sun on a clear day at midday, with no cloud before it?" They said, "No." He did not declare them unbelievers for not knowing about the vision, and this is a matter of belief. It is authentically reported that he (peace and blessings be upon him) was asked, "Where was our Lord before the heavens and the earth were created?" He did not object to the questioner, but said, "He was in a cloud, with air above it and air below it." Ahmad related this. It is authentically reported that he (peace and blessings be upon him) was asked about the beginning of the creation of this world. He replied, "Allah existed, and there was nothing besides Him; His Throne was upon the water, and He wrote everything in the Record." Al-Bukhari related this. This is a matter of belief.

It is authentically reported that he (peace and blessings be upon him) was asked, "Where will people be on the Day when the earth is changed?" He replied, "On the Bridge." In another wording: "They will be in the darkness before the Bridge." He was then asked, "Who will be the first people to cross?" He replied, "The poor among the Emigrants." Muslim related it. There is no contradiction between the two answers, for the darkness is at the beginning of the Bridge: there the changing begins, and it is completed while they are on the Bridge. This is a matter of belief, and he did not declare them unbelievers.
