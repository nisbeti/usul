Abu Bakr al-Barqani(45) informed us, saying: I read to Bishr ibn Ahmad al-Isfarayini.(46) He said: Abu Sulayman Dawud ibn al-Husayn al-Bayhaqi(47) told you: "It reached me that al-Hasan ibn Ali al-Halwani(48) said, 'I do not declare anyone who suspends judgment concerning the Quran to be an unbeliever.'" So they abandoned his knowledge. Abu Sulayman said: "I asked Abu Salama ibn Shabib about al-Halwani's knowledge. He said, 'He should be thrown into the privy.'" Then Abu Salama said: "Whoever does not testify to the unbelief of an unbeliever is an unbeliever."(49) (Weak) A report conveyed indirectly
It appears in Tabaqat al-Hanabila (1 / 172)

(45) The imam, scholar, jurist, hadith master, reliable authority, and shaykh of the jurists and hadith scholars, Abu Bakr Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Ahmad ibn Ghalib al-Khwarizmi, then al-Barqani, a Shafi'i, author of works. Al-Khatib said: "Al-Barqani was trustworthy, devout, reliable, and discerning. We have seen no one among our shaykhs more reliable than him. He was knowledgeable in jurisprudence, had a good command of Arabic, and knew many hadiths."
Tarikh Baghdad, 4 / 374.
(46) The imam, hadith scholar, trustworthy and widely traveled authority, the leading transmitter of his time, Abu Sahl Bishr ibn Ahmad ibn Bishr ibn Mahmud al-Isfarayini, a leading figure of Isfarayin and one of those renowned for courage and bravery.
Siyar A'lam al-Nubala (16/228-229)
(47) Dawud ibn al-Husayn ibn Aqil, Abu Sulayman al-Bayhaqi: the hadith scholar, imam, trustworthy authority, and leading transmitter of Nishapur.
Siyar A'lam al-Nubala (13/579)
(48) Al-Hasan ibn Ali ibn Muhammad al-Halwani. Yaqub ibn Shayba said: "He was trustworthy, reliable, and precise." Abu Dawud said: "He did not criticize narrators." He also said: "He was knowledgeable about narrators, but did not put his knowledge to use." Al-Nasa'i said: "Trustworthy." Al-Dhahabi said: "A reliable authority and proof." Ibn Hajar said: "Trustworthy, a hadith master."
Imam Ahmad criticized him because he suspended judgment on declaring an unbeliever anyone who said, "My utterance of the Quran is created."
Tahdhib al-Kamal (6/262-263); Siyar A'lam al-Nubala: 11 / 398
(49) Its chain of transmission is sound back to Dawud ibn al-Husayn, but he did not name the person from whom he received this report, so the intermediary is unknown. Al-Khatib related from him in Tarikh Baghdad (7/365) that he said: "The Quran is the speech of Allah, uncreated. We know nothing other than this."
