Exalted is He, who urged disputation on the basis of truth and commanded the seeking of proof. It is established, by the text we have cited, that seeking evidence is the way of Allah, Mighty and Majestic. Whoever forbids this and turns people away from it is turning them away from the way of Allah, Exalted is He, and is an oppressor.

His saying, Exalted is He: {And when We appointed for Moses forty nights, then you took the calf after him, while you were wrongdoers. Then We pardoned you after that, so that you might be grateful. And when We gave Moses the Book and the Criterion, so that you might be guided. And when Moses said to his people, "My people, you have wronged yourselves by taking the calf. So repent to your Creator and slay yourselves. That is better for you with your Creator." Then He accepted your repentance. Indeed, He is the Accepting of repentance, the Merciful.}

They were not excused on the grounds that they had followed the Samaritan when he said to them, "This is your god and the god of Moses, but he forgot."
