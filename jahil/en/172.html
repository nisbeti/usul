Many of the laws of Islam have not been transmitted to us, since Allah Most High's guarantee of their preservation no longer applies to them. Moreover, no one is incapable of claiming that any report he wishes was transmitted by mass transmission (tawatur). Indeed, the proponents of chains of transmission have the stronger claim in this regard, given the large number of transmitters and the variation in their chains, which attest to the soundness of their claim that they were transmitted by mass transmission. And Allah Most High is the source of success. If someone resorts to saying that every report transmitted through the route of reliable individuals is a fabricated lie, none of which the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) ever said, we say—and Allah Most High is the source of success—that this is open defiance and a rejection of what we know with certainty to be otherwise. It is a denial of all the Companions, from the first to the last, of all the eminent Successors, and of every generation of scholars, one after another. For all those we have mentioned undoubtedly transmitted reports from the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him); some cited them as proof against others, acted upon them, and issued religious rulings based on them. This is a rejection of a consensus known with certainty, and it is plainly false, beyond any possibility of doubt. For we know necessarily that it is utterly impossible that every one of those we have mentioned never spoke truthfully in a single report he transmitted, and that they all fabricated everything they transmitted. Moreover, this would invalidate the laws that no Muslim or non-Muslim doubts are not set out in detail in the Quran, such as prayer, zakat, pilgrimage, and other matters, whose details were taken only from the words of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him).
