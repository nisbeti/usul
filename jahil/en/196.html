The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said: "Allah is the Greatest! This is just like what the Children of Israel said to Moses: {Make for us a god, just as they have gods.} You will follow the ways of those who came before you." Narrated by al-Tirmidhi (2180), who graded it authentic; also narrated by Imam Ahmad (21900), and this is his wording. Shaykh al-Albani graded it authentic.

They asked the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) to do something that constitutes major shirk: to prescribe for them attachment to a tree, as the polytheists used to do. For this reason, the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) likened what they said to what the Children of Israel said to Moses.

Muhammad Rashid Rida said: "Those who said to the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) what has been mentioned had only recently left polytheism, and so they thought that what the Prophet might prescribe for them in this regard would be lawful and would not contradict Islam."

The saying of the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him): "If a judge exercises independent reasoning and reaches the correct conclusion, he receives two rewards; if he errs, he receives one reward."

Ibn Hazm says:

If the ruling of the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) reached him and was established as authentic in his view, but he opposed it, exercising independent reasoning in a matter whose correct course had not been made clear to him, then he is mistaken, excused, and rewarded once, as he (peace and blessings be upon him) said: "If a judge exercises independent reasoning and reaches the correct conclusion, he receives two rewards; if he errs, he receives one reward." Everyone who holds a belief, makes a statement, or performs an act is a judge with respect to that matter. If he opposes it through his actions, knowingly resisting the truth and believing contrary to what he has done, then he is a sinful believer.
