A Companion who had heard from the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) would relate it to his family and neighbors. This establishes that the laws were laid down for all of them, from the first to the last. The Rafidites and Kharijites have never gone so far as this, even though it is a claim without proof. Whatever is like that is certainly false, according to three positions, as you can see; there is no fourth.
Either every report transmitted by a trustworthy narrator from another trustworthy narrator, reaching back to the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), is entirely false, fabricated from beginning to end—and this is certainly false, as we have shown—or else it would mean that every Companion, Successor, and scholar, without exception, agreed to invent the laws and lie about them in the name of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him). This is a renunciation of Islam. Or else some of these reports are true and some false, but no one can ever distinguish the true from the false. This is to deny Allah, Exalted is He, in His declaration that He would preserve the revealed Reminder, that He had perfected the religion for us, and that He accepts from us only the religion of Islam and nothing else. It also entails the corruption of the religion and its mingling with things Allah, Exalted is He, never commanded, and that no one in the world can know what Allah has commanded him in his religion and what He has never commanded him. It means that the reality of Islam and its laws have certainly been nullified. This is a renunciation of
Islam, or that they are all definitively true in their unseen reality with Allah, Exalted is He, and all necessarily yield certain knowledge, because Allah, Exalted is He, has stated that He preserves the Reminder He sent down; because He has forbidden judging in religion on the basis of conjecture and speaking about Him without knowledge; and because He has stated that He has made clear the right guidance as distinct from error. Right guidance is nothing other than what Allah, Exalted is He, sent down through the words of His Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) and in his actions, while error is nothing other than what Allah, Exalted is He, did not send down.
