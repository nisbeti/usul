Allah has stated that whoever cannot produce sound evidence for what he says or does is following his own desires. He, Exalted is He, says: ((Say, "Then bring a Book from Allah that is a better guide than these two, and I will follow it, if you are truthful." 49 But if they do not respond to you, then know that they are only following their own desires. And who is more astray than one who follows his own desires without guidance from Allah? Indeed, Allah does not guide the wrongdoing people." 50)) Thus, He, Exalted is He, called the one who brings no evidence for what he says a follower of his own desires, declared that none is more astray than him, and described him as one of the wrongdoers. All of this entails declaring him an unbeliever.

And He says: ((Indeed, those who dispute concerning the signs of Allah without any authority that has come to them have nothing in their hearts but pride, which they will never attain. So seek refuge in Allah; indeed, He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing. 56)) Thus, He, Exalted is He, described the one who disputes without knowledge as having pride: by Allah's description, he is arrogant, a wrongdoer, and astray.

And He, Exalted is He, says: ((They say, "Allah has taken a son." Glory be to Him! He is the Self-Sufficient; to Him belongs all that is in the heavens and all that is on the earth. You have no authority for this. Do you say about Allah what you do not know? 68 Say, "Indeed, those who fabricate lies about Allah will not succeed." 69 A brief enjoyment in this world, then to Us will be their return; then We will make them taste the severe punishment for their disbelief. 70)) Thus, He, Exalted is He, declared that those who fabricate lies about Allah will not succeed, and indicated that by doing so they disbelieve.

And He, Exalted is He, says: ((And among the people is one who disputes about Allah without knowledge, guidance, or an illuminating Book, (8) turning his neck aside to lead others away from the way of Allah. For him there is disgrace in this world, and on the Day of Resurrection We will make him taste the punishment of the Burning Fire. (9))) Thus, He, Exalted is He, made clear that the one who disputes without knowledge is punished and burdened with sin.

And he (peace and blessings be upon him) said: "And a man who did not know the truth and judged among people out of ignorance is in the Fire." Thus, he declared that the ignorant person who speaks without knowledge is in the Fire.

And He said: ((These are our people; they have taken gods besides Him. Why do they not bring clear authority for them? Who is more unjust than one who fabricates a lie about Allah? (15))) He called the one who brings no authority or proof a fabricator of lies about Allah, and declared that none is more unjust than him; this indicates his unbelief.

And He said: ((Those who dispute concerning the signs of Allah without any authority that has come to them: it is greatly hateful to Allah and to those who believe. Thus Allah seals the heart of every arrogant tyrant.)) Thus, He called the one who disputes without authority hateful.

And he (peace and blessings be upon him) said: "Whoever relates a hadith from me, believing it to be a lie, is one of the liars." Thus, he called the person who thought the hadith was false and transmitted it, attributing it to the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), a liar—even if he was not certain—and called him a liar.

And He, Exalted is He, says: ((Those who dispute concerning the signs of Allah without any authority that has come to them: it is greatly hateful to Allah and to those who believe. Thus Allah seals the heart of every arrogant tyrant.)) Thus, He called the one who disputes out of ignorance hateful.

From this, you can see that there are two men: one who has secured his general faith, then remains silent about what he does not know, saying, "If Allah and His Messenger said it, then I believe it."
