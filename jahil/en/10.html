Imam Ahmad (may Allah have mercy on him) said (vol. 4, p. 24): Ali narrated to us; Muadh ibn Hisham narrated to us; he said: My father narrated to me, from Qatada, from al-Ahnaf ibn Qays, from al-Aswad ibn Sari: The Prophet of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him and his family) said: "There are four on the Day of Resurrection: a deaf man who hears nothing, an imbecile, an elderly man, and one who died during an interval [between prophets]. The deaf man will say, 'My Lord, Islam came, but I could hear nothing.' The imbecile will say, 'My Lord, Islam came, while the children were pelting me with camel dung.' The elderly man will say, 'My Lord, Islam came, but I could understand nothing.' And the one who died during the interval will say, 'My Lord, no messenger of Yours came to me.' He will take their pledges that they will obey Him, then send word to them: 'Enter the Fire.' He said: By Him in whose hand is Muhammad's soul, if they entered it, it would be coolness and safety for them."

Ali narrated to us; Muadh ibn Hisham narrated to us; he said: My father narrated to me, from Qatada, from al-Hasan, from Abu Rafi, from Abu Hurayra, something similar, except that at the end he said: "Whoever enters it, it will be coolness and safety for him; and whoever does not enter it will be dragged to it."

This is an authentic hadith; its narrators are among the narrators of the Sahih collections." They said: "That is, the people before the sending of a messenger and the establishment of proof are excused with regard to some rulings, but not excused with regard to others.

They are excused in that they are not punished in this world or the Hereafter until the prophetic proof is established against them—and this is from Allah's mercy and grace.

They are not excused for committing polytheism and the rulings that follow from it, such as not burying them in Muslim cemeteries, not praying over them, and not tending their graves.
