It is clear.(76) Musa ibn Ubayda(77) narrated to us, from Ayyub ibn Khalid ibn Safwan,(78) from Ibn Umar, who said: "Whoever prays four rak'ahs after the sunset prayer is like one who follows one military expedition with another."(79) (Weak.)
Ziyad ibn Ayyub said: "Whoever says that the Quran is created is an unbeliever, without any doubt." He was asked, "Can one be heard from if he does not declare them unbelievers?" He replied, "No, absolutely not." He was asked, "I have relatives among them; should I treat them kindly and greet them with peace?" He replied, "No, absolutely not. Do not attend their funerals or visit them."(80)

Tahdhib al-Kamal (29/104-114)
(78) Ayyub ibn Khalid ibn Safwan ibn Aws ibn Jabir ibn Qurt ibn Qays al-Ansari al-Najjari al-Madani. Al-Azdi said: "His hadith is not of that quality; the hadith scholars criticized him." Yahya ibn Sa'id and those of his standing did not write down his hadith.

(79) Its chain of transmission is very weak, and Musa ibn Ubayda and Ayyub ibn Khalid are both weak.
(80) The chain of transmission back to Ziyad ibn Ayyub is strong, as mentioned earlier.
(81) Muhammad ibn al-Muzaffar ibn Ali ibn Harb, Abu Bakr al-Muqri al-Dinawari. Al-Khatib said: "We wrote hadith from him. He was a virtuous, distinguished, and truthful shaykh." Tarikh Baghdad (4/30)
(82) Al-Husayn ibn Muhammad ibn Habsh, Abu Ali al-Dinawari al-Muqri. He was advanced in the science of Quranic recitation, renowned for precision, trustworthy and reliable. Tarikh al-Islam (26/536)
(83) Abd al-Rahman ibn Muhammad ibn Idris ibn al-Mundhir ibn Dawud ibn Mihran. Al-Khalili said: "A group praised him for his asceticism, complete scrupulousness, and knowledge and practice." Al-Wafayat (18/135-136)
