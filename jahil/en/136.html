If you still possess some reason, some awareness of Allah, Exalted is He, and a measure of Islamic zeal, you know—and everyone who knows this religion knows—that the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) was asked about Islam and, explaining its reality and clarifying its meaning, said: "It is establishing prayer, paying zakat, performing the pilgrimage to the House, fasting Ramadan, and bearing witness that there is no god but Allah." Hadiths to this effect are mass-transmitted. Whoever fulfills these five pillars and observes them as they should be observed is a Muslim, whether anyone likes it or not, whoever that may be. So if someone comes to you with something contrary to this, whether worthless talk, false knowledge, or indeed ignorance, strike it in his face and say to him: "Your ravings have been preceded by the proof of Muhammad ibn Abd Allah, may Allah's blessings and peace be upon him."

Is it not incumbent upon us to recognize that the circumstances and situation of the three whom the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) shunned in the abode of Islam are not like those of Muslim youth today in the abode of disbelief and under conditions of weakness, and that the faith of those Companions is not like that of other people? That is why he did not shun the other people of weak faith who would offer him excuses for staying behind, and he would excuse them. (2)

(Interpretation, Ahmad's doctrine, and the sects) Ibn Taymiyya, al-Iman al-Awsat, 1/56
