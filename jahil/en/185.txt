It is possible that they made a mistake, but you judge by that according to the apparent truthfulness of their testimony; and Allah is the Guardian of what is hidden from you in this matter. End quote.
This is his explicit statement concerning a report open to interpretation, with nothing to support it other than its being a solitary report. There is no dispute about this, for it is open to error in both its chain and its wording. Our discussion concerns reports that were received with acceptance and became well known among the community, and that one person stated in the presence of a group, none of whom objected; rather, the hearer accepted it and affirmed by it an attribute of the Lord, Exalted is He, and denounced those who denied it, just as all the imams of Islam denounced those who denied the Lord's attributes reported in the texts and branded them innovators.
As for what he mentioned in his later book, he said: I said to him—meaning the person debating him: "What do you think if someone says to you, 'I suspect everything you have narrated from those from whom you narrated it, for I fear that every hadith transmitter among them may have erred in what he heard from the person who narrated to him, when something contrary to it is narrated from the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him)'?" It is not permissible to suspect the reports of trustworthy people. I said: "Did any of them narrate it except as one person from another?" He said: "No." I said: "And was it narrated from the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) as one person from another?" He said: "Yes." I said: "We know that the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said it only through the truthfulness of the transmitter in our view, and we know that the person whose statement we have cited said it on the basis of a single report." He said: "Yes." I said: "And our knowledge that the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said it is like our knowledge that the person we have named said it." He said: "Yes." I said: "So, when the two kinds of knowledge derived from the report of a truthful person are equal, which report should we give precedence to: the report from the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), and act upon it, or the report from someone below him?" He said: "Rather, the report from the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), if it is established." I said: "Their establishment..."
