(Faulty interpretation is not accepted)
Al-Qadi Iyad quoted in al-Shifa the scholars' statement: "Claiming an interpretation of an explicit expression is not accepted." End quote (2/217).
Shaykh al-Islam stated this explicitly in al-Sarim al-Maslul, p. 527.
Thus, if someone is known and notorious for his heresy and manipulation of the proofs of the Sharia, or engages in acts of unbelief that are explicit and clear and admit of no interpretation, his claim of interpretation is not accepted. There is no ijtihad or interpretation that makes engaging in explicit unbelief permissible, for no unbeliever is without faulty interpretations with which he tries to patch up his unbelief.
