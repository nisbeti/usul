Those who believe become clear. Just as Umar objected to Hisham ibn al-Hakam when he heard him reciting Surat al-Furqan differently from the way he had recited it. And just as a group of the early generations objected to some reciters over readings they did not recognize, until Uthman united them upon the master copy of the Quran. And just as a group of the early and later generations objected to the claim that Allah wills acts of disobedience, because they believed this meant that Allah loves and approves of them and commands them. Another group of the early and later generations objected to the claim that Allah wills acts of disobedience because they thought that "will" could only mean His willing their creation; yet they knew that Allah is the Creator of all things, that whatever He wills comes to be, and whatever He does not will does not come to be. The Quran uses the word "will" in both this sense and the other sense, but each group recognized one of the two meanings and rejected the other. And like the man who said to his family, "When I die, burn me, then scatter my ashes in the sea. For by Allah, if Allah has power over me, He will punish me with a punishment unlike that of anyone in all the worlds." And as a group of the early generations have mentioned regarding His saying: {Does he think that no one will have power over him?} and the disciples' saying: {Can your Lord send down to us a table spread with food from heaven?} And like the Companions who asked the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him), "Will we see our Lord on the Day of Resurrection?" They did not know that they would see Him; and many people do not know this, either because the hadiths have not reached them or because they thought them false or mistaken.))
