Allah Most High said concerning the Jews:
"Those who said, 'Allah has bound us by a covenant not to believe in any messenger until he brings us an offering that fire consumes.' Say, 'Messengers before me came to you with clear proofs and with what you have asked for, so why did you kill them, if you are truthful?'"
Thus He made clear that they were lying in their claim that the reason they did not believe was that this sign and proof had not appeared. For this proof had already existed before, yet they still did not believe. Their contradiction therefore showed that they were lying and had no excuse. 183
Allah Most High said: "When the truth came to them from Us, they said, 'Why was he not given the like of what Moses was given?' Did they not disbelieve in what Moses had been given before? They said, 'Two works of magic supporting one another,' and they said, 'We disbelieve in them all.'"
This verse is like the one before it.

(The reason for declaring the one who excuses them to be an unbeliever)
The question now is: What is the reason for declaring the one who excuses them to be an unbeliever? They say that the one who excuses them is now lying about Allah and His Messenger, since by excusing this person who errs in interpretation in what they call matters of creed, known by innate disposition, he is as though saying that Allah and His Messenger have lied by judging this person to be an unbeliever, when in fact he is a believing monotheist. The answer is that he never intended to deny Allah and His Messenger. Rather, in their view, someone who adopts an interpretation that has some basis is excused for his error, and this is the ruling of Allah and His Messenger concerning them; and, in his view, they are truthful in this ruling. Thus there is no basis here for declaring the one who excuses them to be an unbeliever. At the very least, he is interpreting their Islam charitably, for the sake of argument.
