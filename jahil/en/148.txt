Abu al-Muzaffar al-Sam'ani said: Every group among the innovators believes that what it says is the truth upheld by the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) and his Companions. For they all claim the law of Islam, outwardly adhere to its symbols, and believe that what Muhammad brought is the truth. But afterward, the paths diverged for them, and they introduced into the religion what Allah and His Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) had not authorized. So each group claimed that it was the one adhering to the law of Islam, and that the truth upheld by the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) was what it believed and professed.
However, Allah, Exalted is He, decreed that the truth and sound creed should be found only with the people of hadith and transmitted reports, because they received their religion and beliefs from one generation to the next, and from one era to the next, until they reached the Successors. The Successors received it from the Companions of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him), and the Companions received it from the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him). There is no way to know the upright religion and straight path to which the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) called people except by this path followed by the people of hadith.
As for the other sects, they sought religion by a path other than its own, because they turned to their own reasoning, thoughts, and opinions. When they heard something from the Book and the Sunnah, they measured it against the standard of their intellects: if it seemed sound to them, they accepted it, but if it did not weigh up according to their intellects, they rejected it; and if they were compelled to accept it, they distorted it.
Through far-fetched interpretations and repugnant meanings, they turned away from the truth and strayed from it, cast religion behind their backs, and trampled the Sunnah underfoot.
