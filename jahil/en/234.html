Upon Allah, a lie: they will not prosper. 69. A provision in this world; then to Us will be their return, and then We will make them taste the severe punishment for their disbelief. 70. The apparent meaning of this verse is that Allah, Exalted is He, called the one who fabricates lies against Allah a disbeliever and threatened him with punishment. It is also authentically reported that suspicion is the most false of speech, as the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) said. Thus, the Messenger called speech based on doubt a lie. Therefore, anyone who speaks without knowledge, saying something he doubts, is fabricating lies against Allah and is not excused, because he has followed that of which he has no knowledge.

And He, Exalted is He, said: "These are our people, who have taken gods besides Him. Why do they not bring clear authority for them? So who is more unjust than one who fabricates a lie against Allah?" He thus called one who brings no clear proof a fabricator of lies against Allah, and one in such a state is not excused.(15)

Moses said to them, "Woe to you! Do not fabricate lies against Allah, lest He destroy you with a punishment. Whoever fabricates lies has indeed failed." 61.
