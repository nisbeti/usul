My mother(36), from Ibn Battah,(37) who said: Abu Bakr Muhammad ibn Mahmud al-Sarraj(38) told us; Abu Hashim Ziyad ibn Ayyub al-Tusi, known as Dalawayh, told us, and he mentioned it.(39) (Weak)
The statement of Zuhayr ibn Harb is incorrect; the correct name is Mus'ab ibn Sa'id al-Misisi.(40) And he, may Allah have mercy on him, said: "Whoever claims that the Quran, the speech of Allah, is created is an unbeliever, and whoever doubts that he is an unbeliever is an unbeliever." [Sharh Usul al-I'tiqad (430)]
The statement of Yazid ibn Harun(41): In al-Ibana by Ibn Battah (257), it is reported that Yazid ibn Harun said: "Whoever says, 'The Quran is..."

(36) Ali ibn Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Ali al-Basri al-Baghdadi, al-Bundar, narrated from Ibn Battah, and Ibn Abi Ya'la narrated from him. Al-Khatib said: "I wrote down hadith from him, and he was truthful." Abu Sa'd al-Sam'ani said: "He was a righteous, learned, trustworthy shaykh." He died in the year 474 AH.
Tarikh Baghdad (11/335); Siyar A'lam al-Nubala' (18/402)
(37) Ubayd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Muhammad ibn Hamdan al-Ukbari al-Hanbali, Ibn Battah, author of the book al-Ibanah al-Kubra. Al-Dhahabi described him as "the imam, the exemplar, the devout worshipper, the jurist, the hadith scholar, the shaykh of Iraq," then said: "Despite his merits, Ibn Battah had misconceptions and made mistakes." Al-Siyar (16/529–530)
(38) Muhammad ibn Mahmud ibn Muhammad ibn al-Mundhir ibn Thumama, Abu Bakr al-Sarraj. Yusuf ibn Umar al-Qawwas mentioned him among his reliable teachers, and Abu al-Qasim al-Abanduni said: "There is no harm in him."
Tarikh Baghdad (4/25)
(39) "The chain of transmission is sound from Ibn Dalawayh, and the narrators in the chain are trustworthy, as stated above."
(40) An important note: Abu Khaythama mentioned in al-Lalaka'i's chain of transmission is not Zuhayr ibn Harb ibn Shaddad.
He is in fact Mus'ab ibn Sa'id al-Misisi; the report will be cited later in its correct form.
(41) Yazid ibn Harun, Abu Khalid al-Wasiti. Ahmad ibn Hanbal said: "He was a hadith memorizer, precise in hadith, and sound in his hadith."
Tahdhib al-Kamal (32/266)
