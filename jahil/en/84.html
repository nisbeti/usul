{Those are truly the disbelievers.} And the Exalted said: {Say, "Was it Allah, His signs, and His Messenger that you were mocking?" Make no excuses; you have disbelieved after your faith.} All of these are disbelievers, according to the text. Consensus has been established that anyone who denies something which, by consensus, is known to us to have been brought by the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) has disbelieved. The text also establishes that anyone who mocks Allah, the Exalted, one of the angels, one of the prophets (peace be upon them), a verse of the Quran, or one of the religious obligations—all of which are signs of Allah, the Exalted—after proof has been conveyed to him, is a disbeliever. Whoever claims there was a prophet after the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him), or denies something that he knows the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said, is a disbeliever, because he has not accepted the Prophet's judgment concerning the dispute between him and his opponent.

Ibn Hajar said in his commentary on the hadith: "Aisha said: 'We administered medicine to him through the side of his mouth during his illness, and he began gesturing to us, "Do not administer medicine to me through the side of my mouth." We said, "It is the sick person's aversion to medicine." When he recovered, he said, "Did I not forbid you to administer medicine to me through the side of my mouth?" We said, "It is the sick person's aversion to medicine." He said, "Let no one in the house remain without having medicine administered to him through the side of his mouth while I watch, except al-Abbas, for he was not present with you."'" Administering medicine through the side of the mouth means placing medicine at the side of the mouth, or putting it into the mouth with a finger and rubbing it around the mouth. (Fath al-Bari, Ibn Hajar, 8/147)

His words, "Let no one in the house remain without having medicine administered to him through the side of his mouth while I watch, except al-Abbas, for he was not present with you": It has been said that this establishes the legitimacy of retaliation for anything a person is deliberately subjected to.
