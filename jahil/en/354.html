They used to declare as unbelievers everyone who was not a Jahmi and in agreement with them, and to treat them as unbelievers … and so on, up to his statement: "It is well known that this is among the most extreme forms of Jahmism, for calling people to a doctrine is graver than merely holding it, and rewarding one who professes it and punishing one who abandons it is graver than merely calling people to it …"

Then Imam Ahmad prayed for the caliph and others who had beaten and imprisoned him, sought forgiveness for them, and absolved them of what they had done … Had they been apostates from Islam, it would not have been permissible to seek forgiveness for them, for seeking forgiveness for unbelievers is impermissible according to the Book, the Sunnah, and scholarly consensus. These statements and actions of his and of other imams clearly show that they did not declare individual Jahmis to be unbelievers(( ….

So consider, may Allah have mercy on you: despite the repugnance of the Jahmis' doctrine, he did not declare their individuals to be unbelievers. Rather, he prayed for them, did not support the polytheists and Crusaders against them, but rejoiced at the conquest of Amorium and openly expressed his joy at it. Nor did he surrender Muslim lands governed by the Sharia to the Crusaders on the pretext that the Jahmis and Mu'tazilites declare us unbelievers.

Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyya says in his response to Ibn al-Bakri: "For this reason, the people of knowledge and the Sunnah do not declare those who oppose them to be unbelievers, even if those opponents declare them unbelievers. For unbelief is a legal ruling, and a person may not retaliate with the like of it. If someone lies about you and commits adultery with your wife, you may not lie about him or commit adultery with his wife, because adultery and lying are forbidden as a right owed to Allah, Exalted is He. Likewise, declaring someone an unbeliever is a right owed to Allah, Exalted is He, so no one is declared an unbeliever except one whom Allah and His Messenger have declared an unbeliever." End quote, p. (257).

He also mentioned that "among the faults of the people of innovation is that they declare one another unbelievers, whereas among the merits of the people of knowledge is that they make mistakes but do not declare others unbelievers." End quote (9).
