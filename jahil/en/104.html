The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him)—his community: no one among Allah's creation against whom proof has been established may deny that the Quran was revealed with this, and that it is authentically reported from the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) through a reliable narrator. If someone contradicts this after proof has been established against him, he is an unbeliever in Allah. But before proof has been established against him through a report, he is excused on account of ignorance, because this knowledge cannot be attained through reason, reflection, thought, or the like. Allah, Glorified is He, has informed us that He is All-Hearing, and that He has two hands, in His words: "Rather, both His hands are outstretched"; that He has a right hand, in His words: "And the heavens will be folded in His right hand"; that He has a face, in His words: "Everything will perish except His face," and His words: "And the face of your Lord, full of majesty and honor, will remain"; and that He has a foot, in the Prophet's words (peace and blessings be upon him): "Until the Lord places His foot in it"—meaning Hell. He laughs at His believing servant, as the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said of the man who was killed in the path of Allah: "He met Allah while He was laughing at him." He descends every night to the lowest heaven, according to the report of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him). He is not one-eyed, as the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said when mentioning the Dajjal: "He is one-eyed, but your Lord is not one-eyed." The believers will see their Lord on the Day of Resurrection with their eyes, just as they see the moon on the night of the full moon. And He has a finger, as the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said: "There is no heart that is not between two fingers of the fingers of the Most Merciful, Mighty and Majestic." These meanings by which Allah has described Himself, and by which His Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) has described Him, are such that their true nature cannot be grasped through reflection or thought. No one is deemed an unbeliever for being ignorant of them unless the report concerning them has reached him.
