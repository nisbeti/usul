He laid down conditions for this excuse. The first is that the ignorant person must not have been negligent in seeking knowledge or turned away from it, for in that case he is not called ignorant or mistaken. Rather, he knows the path of truth but does not follow it, and deliberately turns away from the evidence. The verse therefore applies to him: {And those who disbelieve turn away from that of which they were warned.} He is subject to the punishment of a polytheist if he commits an act of polytheism, as judged by the rulings of this world. The punishments reported from the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) for those who died during the interval between prophets, such as the punishment of his father, peace be upon him, and the punishment of Amr ibn Luhayy, can be understood in two ways. The first is that remnants of the religion of Abraham had reached them, but they turned away from it. There was someone living among them, Zayd ibn Amr ibn Nufayl, who died following the religion of Abraham; he sought it and found it. So they have no excuse. The second is that the religion of Abraham had not reached them, but they turned away from seeking it. Their ignorance was thus due to their turning away, and one who turns away is not excused—especially since Zayd ibn Amr ibn Nufayl had sought the truth and found it. Likewise, whoever died during the interval without knowledge and then embraced polytheism was, in reality, fabricating lies about Allah, for he merely followed his forefathers in their beliefs based on conjecture, and conjecture is the most false of speech. Their conjecture was a lie about Allah for which they are held accountable. Since they found no one to teach them, they should have remained firm in what they were certain of: that Allah, Glorified and Exalted is He, alone is divine. They should have said, "O Allah, if we knew of a religion better than this, we would follow it," as Zayd ibn Amr ibn Nufayl said something similar. Either they fabricated about Allah what they did not know, following conjecture without proof—in which case they deliberately committed polytheism and spoke lies about Allah—or, as Allah Most High said: {And whoever calls upon besides ...}
