It is reported in al-Sunna by Abdullah ibn Ahmad (1/112): Ghayath ibn Ja'far (43) told me: I heard Sufyan ibn Uyaynah (44) say, "The Quran is the speech of Allah, Mighty and Majestic. Whoever says it is created is an unbeliever, and whoever doubts that he is an unbeliever is an unbeliever." // (Weak)

It is reported in Tarikh Baghdad (7/365)

(43) He was Sufyan's dictation assistant. ` ()

Ghayath ibn Ja'far al-Shami al-Rahbi was mentioned by Ibn Hibban in his book al-Thiqat, and al-Daraqutni said:

Uyaynah narrated many hadiths from Ibn Uyaynah.

Tahdhib al-Kamal (23/126)

(44) ` () Sufyan ibn Uyaynah ibn Abi Imran was one of the narrators of the canonical collections. Ahmad ibn Abdullah al-Ijli said: Sufyan ibn Uyaynah was a trustworthy Kufan, firmly established in hadith. Some hadith scholars used to say that he was the most reliable of people in narrating from al-Zuhri. He had good hadith, was counted among the wise hadith scholars, and his hadith numbered around seven thousand. He had no books.

Tahdhib al-Kamal (11/189)
