The attributes and the descent of the Lord, Blessed and Exalted is He, every night to the lowest heaven: they said, "The reports concerning this are established, and they are believed in without imagining how, and without asking, 'How is it?'" This is how it was related from Malik, Sufyan ibn Uyaynah, and Abdullah ibn al-Mubarak: they said regarding these hadiths, "Let them pass without asking how." This is also the position of the scholars among the people of the Sunnah and the community. As for the Jahmiyyah, they rejected these reports and said, "This is anthropomorphism." Allah, Mighty and Majestic is He, has mentioned the hand, hearing, and sight in more than one place in His Book. The Jahmiyyah interpreted these verses, explaining them in a way different from the explanation given by the scholars. They said that Allah did not create Adam with His hand, and said that "hand" here means power. Ishaq ibn Ibrahim said: "Anthropomorphism occurs only when someone says 'a hand like a hand,' 'a hand resembling a hand,' 'hearing like hearing,' or 'hearing resembling hearing.' If someone says 'hearing like hearing' or 'hearing resembling hearing,' that is anthropomorphism. But if someone says, as Allah, Exalted is He, has said, 'hand, hearing, and sight,' without saying how, or saying 'resembling hearing' or 'like hearing,' then this is not anthropomorphism. It is as Allah, Exalted is He, says in His Book: 'There is nothing like Him, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing.'" End quote.))
