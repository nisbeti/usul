He said: In this way, the difference between disagreement over subsidiary legal questions and disagreement over the creeds that form the foundations becomes clear. We found that the Companions of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), may Allah be pleased with them, disagreed after him over religious rulings, yet they did not split apart or become factions, because they did not depart from the religion. They considered matters in which they had been given latitude, and their statements and opinions differed on many questions, such as the questions of the grandfather, the polytheistic inheritance case, distant kindred, mothers of children, and other matters. Thus, their disagreement over these matters was praiseworthy, and this kind of disagreement was a mercy for this community, as Allah granted them success and certainty. He then gave scholars latitude to exercise judgment concerning matters whose rulings they did not find in the Revelation and the Sunnah. Despite this disagreement, they were people of affection and sincere counsel; the brotherhood of Islam remained between them, and the bond of harmony among them was not severed. But when these ruinous whims arose, calling their adherents to the Fire, and they became factions, brotherhood in religion was severed and harmony fell away. This shows that estrangement and division arose only over newly introduced matters, which Satan invented and put on the tongues of his allies so that they would disagree and denounce one another as unbelievers.
