The Exalted has commanded us to judge according to the testimony of one upright witness together with the claimant's oath, according to the testimony of two or more upright witnesses, and according to the oath of the defendant when the claimant has no evidence, in cases involving the unlawful taking of lives, unlawful sexual relations, unlawful bodily harm, and unlawful property. By your own admission, all of this could, in reality, be contrary to what the witness testified to and what the person swearing an oath swore to. This is judgment based on conjecture, which you condemned us for in our position on the report of a single narrator. There is no difference. We said to them:

By Allah's enabling grace, there are clear distinctions between the two matters, as clear as the sun. The first is that Allah, Exalted is He, has undertaken to preserve the religion, complete it, and make clear what belongs to it and what does not. But He has never undertaken to preserve our lives, our sexual integrity, our bodies, or our property in this world. Rather, the Exalted has decreed that much of all this will be taken unjustly in this world.

The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) explicitly stated this when he said, "You bring your disputes to me, and I am only a human being. Perhaps one of you is more persuasive in his argument than the other, so I may rule in his favor according to what I hear. If I rule in someone's favor concerning any part of his brother's right, let him not take it, for I am only allotting him a portion of the Fire." And when he said to the two spouses who had invoked mutual imprecation, "Allah knows that one of you is lying. Will either of you repent?" Or words to that effect, in all these cases. The second distinction is that our ruling on the basis of a witness's testimony and the oath of the person swearing is not judgment based on conjecture, as they claim.
