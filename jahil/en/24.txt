Nor do two people differ over the fact that the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) sent those of his messengers whom he sent to distant lands only so that they might convey to their people the Quran, the Sunnah, and the laws of religion; he did not send them to institute for them a religion that he himself had not brought from Allah, Exalted is He. It is therefore established by all this that everything reliably transmitted by one trustworthy person from another, reaching back to the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), whether from the Quran or the Sunnah, must be accepted, acknowledged, affirmed, believed in, and followed as religion. And anything reliably reported from a Companion, a Successor, or anyone after them, whether a recitation not traced back to the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him), or a legal opinion not traced back to him (peace and blessings be upon him), may not be accepted, for neither Allah, Exalted is He, nor His Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) made it binding. All such reports have indeed been authentically transmitted from individual Companions and Successors. Their virtue does not make it obligatory to accept their opinions, nor does it preclude the possibility that they may have erred in what they said based on their own judgment. Rather, their virtue covers every error they made and outweighs it, and entails that they be revered and loved. And Allah, Exalted is He, is the source of success. Another proof is that it has been established with certainty and is known necessarily that all the Companions, from the first to the last, agreed, with none of them dissenting, nor any of the Successors who lived in their time, that whenever any one of them faced a new case, he would ask a Companion about it and follow his opinion. They would ask him only about what the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) had made binding in religion from Allah, Exalted is He, concerning that particular case; no one ever asked any person …
