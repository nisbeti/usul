He imitated a human being like himself, who may, in the sight of Allah, Exalted is He, be other than he supposes. In any case, he is not infallible; he may err or be right. Allah, Exalted is He, said: {And they say, "None shall enter Paradise unless he is a Jew or a Christian." Those are their wishful claims. Say, "Bring your proof, if you are truthful"}

"truthful." Thus, Exalted is He, made it incumbent upon anyone who is truthful in his claim to produce proof; and if he does not produce it,

On the basis of proof, he is a liar, one who invalidates the truth, or an ignorant person. Allah, Exalted is He, said: {Here you are, disputing about that of which you have knowledge; why then do you dispute about that of which you have no knowledge? Allah knows, and you do not know.} Thus, He, Exalted is He, permitted disputation only on the basis of knowledge and forbade it without knowledge.

And the Exalted says: {They will say, "Three, the fourth of them being their dog," and they will say, "Five, the sixth of them being their dog," guessing at the unseen; and they will say, "Seven, and the eighth of them being their dog." Say, "My Lord knows best their number. None knows them except a few. So do not argue about them except with an outward argument, and do not ask any of them about them."} Abu Muhammad said: When we found that Allah, Exalted is He, had commanded argumentation and debate in the verses we have mentioned, and had made the acceptance of anything obligatory only on the basis of proof, we were obliged to seek out blameworthy argumentation, as we explained earlier. We found that He said: {We send the messengers only as bearers of glad tidings and warners. But those who disbelieve argue with falsehood in order to refute the truth by it, and they take My signs and the warnings given to them in mockery.} Thus, as you can see, He condemned arguing without proof and arguing about falsehood. By this, Allah refuted the saying of the deluded: "Every deluded person is taught an argument." He made clear that the deluded person is one who is not taught an argument, and that the one who is right is, in truth, the one taught an argument; they are the people of truth. And the Exalted says: {Those who argue about the signs of Allah without any authority having come to them—it is greatly hateful in the sight of Allah and in the sight of those who believe. Thus Allah seals every arrogant tyrant's heart.} These verses together make clear what blameworthy argumentation is.
