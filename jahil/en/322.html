"The arrow passes through the prey." Despite his statement in Al-Fasl fi al-Milal wa al-Ahwa wa al-Nihal (3/144):

"Whoever among the people of Islam exercises interpretation and errs: if the proof has not been established against him and the truth has not been made clear to him, then he is excused and rewarded with one reward for seeking the truth and intending to reach it, and his error is forgiven, since he did not deliberately adopt it, in accordance with the words of Allah, Exalted is He: {There is no blame upon you for what you have done by mistake, but only for what your hearts have intended.} If he is correct, he has two rewards: one for being correct and another for seeking it. But if the proof has been established against him and the truth made clear to him, and he turns away from the truth without opposing Allah, Exalted is He, or His Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him), then he is a transgressor, for his audacity toward Allah, Exalted is He, in persisting in what is forbidden. If he turns away from the truth in opposition to Allah, Exalted is He, and His Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him), then he is an unbeliever, an apostate, whose blood and property are lawful. In these rulings, there is no difference between erring in belief concerning any matter of the Sharia and erring in a legal opinion concerning any matter, as we explained earlier."

Abu Muhammad said: "Here, Allah willing, we shall be brief, and clarify everything on which we have elaborated. Allah, Exalted is He, said: {We would never punish until We had sent a messenger.} And He, Exalted is He, said: {So that I may warn you by it, and whoever it reaches.} And He, Exalted is He, said: {But no, by your Lord, they will not believe until they make you judge concerning what they dispute among themselves, then find no resistance within themselves to what you have decided, and submit wholeheartedly.} These verses clarify this entire subject. It is therefore established that no one is declared an unbeliever until the command of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) reaches him. If it reaches him and he does not believe in it, then he is an unbeliever. But if he believes in him, then adopts whatever belief Allah wills him to adopt, or gives whatever legal opinion or performs whatever act Allah, Exalted is He, wills him to, without the Prophet's ruling to the contrary reaching him in that matter, then he bears no blame at all for what he believed or did until it reaches him.
