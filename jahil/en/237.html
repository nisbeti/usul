And the Exalted said: "Among the people is one who disputes about Allah without knowledge, guidance, or an illuminating Book, turning his neck aside to lead others away from the way of Allah. For him there is disgrace in this world, and on the Day of Resurrection We shall make him taste the punishment of the Burning Fire. That is for what your hands have sent ahead, and Allah is never unjust to His servants."

And indeed, We sent Noah to his people, and he said, "O my people, worship Allah. You have no god other than Him. I fear for you the punishment of a tremendous Day."

"Say, 'To whom belongs the earth and whoever is in it, if you know?' They will say, 'To Allah.' Say, 'Will you not then take heed?' Say, 'Who is the Lord of the seven heavens and the Lord of the Mighty Throne?' They will say, 'To Allah.' Say, 'Will you not then be mindful of Him?' Say, 'In whose hand is the dominion of all things, who protects, while none can protect against Him, if you know?' They will say, 'To Allah.' Say, 'How then are you deluded?'"

In these verses, Allah rebukes them for knowing that Allah is the owner of everything, yet not singling Him out in worship. Instead, they worshipped others without knowledge, following conjecture. This shows that this alone suffices as an argument against an opponent.

"There are three kinds of judges: two judges are in the Fire, and one judge is in Paradise. As for the one in Paradise, he is a man who knew the truth and judged accordingly, so he is in Paradise. A man knew the truth but did not judge by it and acted unjustly in his judgment; he is in the Fire. And a man who did not know the truth and judged among people out of ignorance; he is destined for the Fire."
