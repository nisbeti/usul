Shaykh Abdullah ibn Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab said in Al-Rasa'il wa al-Masa'il, Part One of Volume One (p. 79): "As for one who died while committing shirk out of ignorance, not obstinacy, we leave his affair to Allah, Exalted is He. One should not supplicate for him, ask for mercy for him, or seek forgiveness for him. This is because many scholars say that whoever has received the Qur'an has had the proof established against him, as Allah, Exalted is He, says: {That I may warn you thereby and whoever it reaches}."

Al-Qarafi, Al-Furuq li al-Qarafi = Anwar al-Buruq fi Anwa al-Furuq (2/150):

"Whoever proceeds despite ignorance has sinned, particularly in matters of belief. For the Lawgiver has imposed great strictness regarding the creeds of the foundations of religion, to the extent that if a person exerts all his effort and exhausts his capacity to dispel his ignorance concerning one of the attributes of Allah, Exalted is He, or concerning something that must be believed as part of the foundations of religion, yet that ignorance is not dispelled, then he is sinful and an unbeliever for abandoning that belief, which is part of faith, and will abide in the Fire forever, according to the well-known position."

It is clear from what has preceded that there is disagreement over the question of whether ignorance is an excuse in matters of creed. Therefore, one who does not accept it as an excuse should not be accused of being a Kharijite; otherwise, let them say this of the imams mentioned above, or else they will inevitably fall into contradiction.
