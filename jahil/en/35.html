9 - Ibn Hajar al-Haytami, may Allah have mercy on him, said:

[According to us, if someone lives far from the Muslims, such that his failure to come to their lands to learn is not attributable to negligence, or if he has only recently embraced Islam, he is excused for his ignorance and should be taught what is correct. If he returns to what he said after that, he is an unbeliever. The same is said of one who considers that good or approves of it.] (4).

10 - Shaykh Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab, may Allah have mercy on him.

He said: [This is astonishing: how can you doubt this when I have explained it to you repeatedly? The person against whom proof has not been established is one who has only recently embraced Islam, or who grew up in a remote desert region, or where the matter concerns a subtle issue such as currency exchange or love magic. Such a person is not declared an unbeliever until he has been informed. As for the fundamentals of religion, which Allah has made clear and firmly established in His Book, Allah's proof is the Quran; whoever has received the Quran has received the proof.] (5). His statements in which he grants an excuse on account of ignorance are qualified by these words of his.
