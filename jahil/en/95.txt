Some have rejected the use of the hadith about the man who had himself burned, saying that he repented of this. They cited a weak narration and said: Ibn Abi Hatim said, "My father narrated to us; Muhammad ibn Musaffa narrated to us; Baqiyya narrated to us, from Abu Bakr ibn Abi Maryam, from Atiyya ibn Qays, concerning His words, {But for whoever fears standing before his Lord there will be two gardens}: 'It was revealed concerning the man who said, "Burn me in the fire, so that perhaps I may escape Allah." He repented for a day and a night after saying this, so Allah accepted his repentance and admitted him to Paradise.'" Ibn Kathir, Tafsir.
In this chain is "Abu Bakr ibn Abd Allah ibn Abi Maryam al-Ghassani al-Shami." It is said that his name was Bukayr, and it is also said that it was Abd al-Salam. (He may be attributed to his grandfather.)
Generation: 7; among the senior Successors of the Successors. His rank according to Ibn Hajar: weak; his house was burgled, and he became confused.
His rank according to al-Dhahabi: "They considered him weak; he had knowledge and religious devotion."
Also in the chain is "Baqiyya ibn al-Walid ibn Sa'id ibn Ka'b ibn Hurayz al-Kila'i al-Himyari al-Maytami," Abu Yahmad al-Himsi.
Birth: 110 AH
