The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) was in doubt, since his Lord, Exalted is He, taught him how to debate the People of the Book and the people of disbelief, and commanded him to seek proof and establish the argument against everyone who opposed him. Nor is there any validity to the statement of one who says, "Whenever a man comes who is more skilled in argument than another, should we abandon what we follow?" or words to that effect. Abu Muhammad said: This is a statement in which every atheist on earth is on equal footing with the one who makes it. If this speaker is permitted not to abandon what he found his forebears following, without proof, despite clear proof being presented to him, then the Jew and the Christian must likewise be permitted not to abandon what they found their forebears following in blind imitation, without proof, and not to accept the proof of Islam presented to them and its decisive argument.
