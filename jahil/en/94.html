Ibn Taymiyyah said: ((Declaring unbelief in general terms, like a threat of punishment in general terms, does not entail declaring a particular person an unbeliever until the proof is established against him, the proof whose rejection makes a person an unbeliever.

As is established in the Sahih collections from the Prophet (peace be upon him) about the man who said: "When I die, burn me, then grind me, then scatter me in the sea, for by Allah, if Allah has power over me, He will punish me with a punishment He has not given anyone in all the worlds." Allah said to him: "What made you do what you did?" He said: "Fear of You." So He forgave him.

This man believed that Allah would not be able to gather him if he did that, or he doubted it, and that He would not resurrect him. Each of these two beliefs is unbelief that makes a person an unbeliever once the proof has been established against him. But he was ignorant of that, and the knowledge that would have lifted his ignorance had not reached him, and he had faith in Allah, in His command and prohibition, and in His promise and threat, so he feared His punishment, and Allah forgave him for his fear.

So whoever among the people of faith in Allah, His Messenger and the Last Day, and of righteous deeds, errs in some matters of belief is in no worse a state than this man: Allah forgives his error, or punishes him if he fell short in following the truth, according to his religion. As for declaring a person whose faith is known an unbeliever merely for a mistake in this, it is a grave matter.

It is established in the Sahih from Thabit ibn al-Dahhak, from the Prophet (peace be upon him), that he said: "Cursing a believer is like killing him, and whoever accuses a believer of unbelief, it is like killing him."

For absolute takfeer ( saing that whoever does this thing is disbeliver ) , like an absolute threatening, does not require the accusing of the appointed person of being kafer (disbeliver) until the proof is based on the one who denies it.

As it was proven in the Sahih on the authority of the Prophet, peace be upon him, who said ((one man said that if I died, then burn me and then crush me, then shed me in the sea, if God is able to torture me, he will torture me with a torture he does not torture anyone from the worlds. Then God said to him: What makes you did what u did? he said Your fear, so he forgave him))

This man believed that God could not collect him if he did that. or doubted that, and that he did not resurect him and each of these two beliefs is a disbelief (kofr), a one will be disbeliever if the argument is based on him, but because he was ignorant of that and had no knowledge about that which can makes him away of his ignorance .and had faith in God and his command and forbidding and his promise and his threatening and feared his punishment so God forgave him because of His fear

He whoever has erred in some matters of belief from the people of faith in God and His Messenger and on the other day and have righteous deed is not worse than this man then God will forgive his mistake. or torment him if he neglects to follow the truth according to the extent of his religion. As for saying that an appointed person who known that he is a believer is disbeliever simply because of bmaking a mistake , it is a great(sin).
