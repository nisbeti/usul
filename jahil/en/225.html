He is subject to the proof because of his turning away. There is no doubt that Allah has not charged us with what we cannot do, namely, knowing what lies within people; rather, He has given us signs that make these inner realities clear. Nor is there any doubt that He has not left the religion as a plaything for people, so that anyone may come along and say, "I am interpreting." No: whoever has had the proof established against him is judged accordingly and punished for the act. And Allah knows best.

2. {Indeed, those who dispute concerning the signs of Allah without any authority that has come to them have nothing in their breasts but pride, which they will never attain. So seek refuge in Allah; indeed, He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing.} 56

This verse also clarifies who has the proof established against him. Whoever argues with you without clear evidence, whether following blind imitation or following evidence, this verse indicates that there is pride in his heart. If we return to the definition of pride, we find that it means rejecting and resisting the truth, and looking down on people. This need makes clear that one who speaks without evidence is, in reality, rejecting and resisting the truth, rather than seeking it or preferring it above all else. Thus the proof has been established against him, and the ruling applies to him.
