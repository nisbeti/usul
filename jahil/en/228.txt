Such a person is an unjust wrongdoer with no excuse: he speaks about Allah without knowledge and denies His Sharia without knowledge. It is noteworthy that all the verses in which Allah condemns the polytheists concern those who speak about Him without knowledge or deny His Sharia without knowledge. As for the third category, the one who seeks Allah's pleasure, like Zayd ibn Amr ibn Nufayl, he refrains from affirming anything about Allah that he does not know, and from denying anything of Him that he does not know. Rather, he believes in Him, Exalted is He, with general faith. Such a person is excused for what he does not know, so long as he remains silent and refrains from taking a position. A hadith, deemed sound by some scholars, states that he will be resurrected as a nation unto himself. This matter is under study, Allah willing.
5- "They worship, besides Allah, that for which He has sent down no authority and of which they have no knowledge. The wrongdoers will have no helper."
This verse has the same meaning as the preceding verses, for Allah calls one who has neither proof nor knowledge of what he says a wrongdoer. This shows that anyone who speaks without knowledge or proof is sinful and unjust, and has no excuse.
