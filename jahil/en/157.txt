And the Exalted said, commanding His Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) to say: "Say: I am no unprecedented messenger, and I do not know what will be done with me or with you. I follow only what is revealed to me, and I am only a clear warner." And the Exalted said: "Indeed, We have sent down the Reminder, and indeed, We will surely preserve it." And the Exalted said: "With clear proofs and scriptures. And We sent down to you the Reminder so that you may make clear to people what was sent down to them, and so that they may reflect." Thus it is established that all the words of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) concerning religion are revelation from Allah, Mighty and Majestic, without doubt; and there is no disagreement among any of the people of language and the Sharia that
every revelation sent down from Allah, Exalted is He, is a Reminder sent down. Therefore, all revelation is certainly preserved by Allah, Exalted is He, through His preservation of it. Everything whose preservation Allah has undertaken is guaranteed not to be lost, nor will anything in it ever be distorted in a way that clarification cannot expose as false. For if anything else were possible, the words of Allah, Exalted is He, would be a lie, and His guarantee would be untrustworthy. This would not occur to anyone possessing even a measure of reason. It is therefore necessary that what Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him) brought to us is preserved, with Allah, Exalted is He, undertaking its preservation, and conveyed exactly as it is to everyone who seeks it, continuously until the end of the world. The Exalted said: "Say: What is the greatest testimony? Say: Allah is witness between me and you. And this Quran has been revealed to me so that I may warn you by it and whomever it reaches. Do you truly testify that there are other gods alongside Allah? Say: I do not testify. Say: He is only One God, and I am free of what you associate with Him."
