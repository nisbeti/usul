Then we say to them: Tell us, if all of that is possible in your view, did Allah, Exalted is He, command you to act upon what reliable transmitters have narrated with a chain of transmission going back to the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), or did He not command you to act upon it? One of the two must be the case. If they say, "Allah, Exalted is He, did not command us to do that," they have joined the Mu'tazilites, and their answer to this claim will come, if Allah, Exalted is He, wills. But if they say, "Rather, Allah, Exalted is He, commanded us to act upon that," we say to them: You have thereby claimed that Allah, Exalted is He, commanded you, in your religion, to act upon what He did not command you to act upon: things fabricated by liars and mistaken by those prone to error; and that He commanded you to attribute to Him, Exalted is He, and to His Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him), what never came to you from them and what Allah, Exalted is He, and His Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) never said. This is a definitive proof that He, Mighty and Majestic is He, commanded lying about Him, made it obligatory to act upon falsehood, upon what is not part of the religion, and upon what liars prescribed without His permission.

Allah, Exalted is He—and this is an exceedingly grave matter, which no Muslim would deem permissible to say. Then we ask them about what they claim is possible: that some of the rulings concerning religion which the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) stated, whether imposing an obligation or a prohibition, might have been lost, so that no one knows whether we are still bound to act upon them or whether that obligation has lapsed. One of these two must be the case. If they say, "Rather, we remain bound by them," we say to them: How can we be bound to act upon what we do not know, what has not reached us, and what will never reach us? This is precisely the burden, hardship, and difficulty from which Allah, Exalted is He, has granted us security. But if they say, "Rather, the obligation to act upon them has lapsed," we say to them: You have thus permitted the abrogation of some of the laws of Islam, laws that were established, confirmed, and binding when the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) died. So tell us: who abrogated them?
