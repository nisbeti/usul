What they called fundamentals and what they called subsidiary matters—how could this distinction be valid when they have based rulings upon it that they devised with their own intellects and opinions? Among these is declaring someone an unbeliever for erring in matters of fundamentals, but not in matters of subsidiary rulings. This is among the most false of falsehoods, as we shall explain. Another is affirming subsidiary rulings on the basis of solitary reports, but not fundamentals, and so on. Any division that is not supported by the Book, the Sunnah, and the principles of the Sharia is a false division that must be discarded.
This division is one of the foundations of the people's misguidance. They distinguished between what they called fundamentals and what they called subsidiary matters, and stripped the subsidiary matters of any specific ruling of Allah concerning them. Rather, they held that Allah's ruling in them varies according to the differing opinions of the jurists. As for what they called fundamentals, they held that whoever errs in them is an unbeliever or a sinner. They claimed consensus on this distinction, yet what they presented as consensus is not recorded from any of the imams of the Muslims, nor from any of the Companions or Successors. This is the habit of the people of speculative theology: they report consensus on something that none of the imams of the Muslims said; indeed, the imams of Islam held the opposite view. Imam Ahmad said, "Whoever claims consensus has lied." This is the claim of al-Asamm, Ibn Ulayya, and those like them: they seek to invalidate the Sunnahs of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) by means of the consensus they claim.
It is known with certainty from the textual proofs and the consensus of the Companions and Successors, and is explicitly stated by the four imams, that jurists who disagree over legal rulings are not all alike; rather, among them are those who are correct and those who are mistaken. Thus, discourse on what they called fundamentals and what they called subsidiary matters divides into that which accords with the truth as it actually is and that which does not.
