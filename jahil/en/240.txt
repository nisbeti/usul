{We relate to you their story in truth. They were young men who believed in their Lord, and We increased them in guidance.} Thus Allah, Mighty and Majestic, praised them for rejecting the claim of their people, since their people had not established clear proof for what they said. And He, Exalted is He, affirmed their statement that whoever makes a claim without evidence is fabricating a lie against Allah, Mighty and Majestic.

And He, Exalted is He, said: {Who is more unjust than one who is reminded of the signs of his Lord and then turns away from them? Indeed, We will take retribution on the criminals.} No one is more unjust than one against whom proof has been established from the Book of Allah, Exalted is He, and from the words of His Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him), yet turns away from it, though it is the decisive proof and the clear argument. And Allah, Exalted is He, said: {Those who consume usury will not stand except as one stands who is being beaten by Satan into madness. That is because they say, "Trade is just like usury." But Allah has permitted trade and forbidden usury. Whoever receives an admonition from his Lord and desists may keep what is past, and his affair rests with Allah. But whoever returns to it—those are the inhabitants of the Fire; they will abide therein forever.} And He, Exalted is He, said: {Rather, those who do wrong follow their desires without knowledge. So who can guide one whom Allah has led astray? They will have no helpers.} Thus He, Exalted is He, has informed us, as you hear, that whoever follows a statement that accords with his view without knowing that it is sound is unjust, and that whoever does not return to the truth he hears is among the people of the Fire. And He, Exalted is He, said: {If they do not respond to you, then know that they follow only their desires. And who is more astray than one who follows his desire without guidance from Allah? Indeed, Allah does not guide the wrongdoing people.} Allah, Exalted is He, condemned a person for denying what he does not know, saying: {Rather, they denied what they had not encompassed in knowledge, and whose interpretation had not yet come to them. Thus those before them denied the truth. So see what was the end of the wrongdoers.} All that we have mentioned thus establishes the duty to refrain from what we do not know and to return to what proof requires once it has been established. And He, Exalted is He, said: {Who is more unjust than one who fabricates a lie against Allah or denies the truth when it comes to him? Is there not a dwelling in Hell for the disbelievers?} Abu Muhammad said: This verse suffices to establish that no one should affirm anything unless proof has been established for it, and that no one should do anything when proof has been established against it.
