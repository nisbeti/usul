(Abu Bakr's position toward Umar in the incident of those who withheld zakat)

It has been reported that those who declare the person who excuses someone acting on an interpretation to be an unbeliever say that those who withheld zakat were unbelievers, and that Abu Bakr did not excuse them on the basis of their interpretation. They said: "The Companions unanimously agreed that those who withheld zakat were unbelievers, as related by Imam Abu Ubayd, Abu Bakr al-Jassas, Qadi Abu Ya'la, Hafiz Ibn Abd al-Barr, Abu al-Faraj al-Maqdisi, and Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyya; they did not excuse them on the basis of interpretation or ignorance. No informed person can doubt that they were ignorant and acting on an interpretation, given how recently they had entered Islam and the religion." End quote.

But if their claim is correct—that those who withheld zakat were unbelievers and were acting on an interpretation, yet Abu Bakr did not excuse them on that basis—then what is your position on Umar, who excuses those acting on interpretations, in this case? Umar excused those who withheld zakat and initially objected to fighting them.
