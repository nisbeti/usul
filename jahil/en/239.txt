Ibn Hazm said regarding the manner of disputation and establishing proof:
"Al-Ihkam fi Usul al-Ahkam - Ibn Hazm" (1/21):
"Allah, Exalted is He, has commanded us explicitly in the Quran to follow the way of Abraham (peace be upon him), and Allah has informed us that disputation and debate were part of Abraham's way: at one time with the king, and at another with his people, using reasoning, as Allah has informed us. Thus, it is incumbent upon us to follow the practice of debate, so that we may turn the people of falsehood toward the truth, and to seek what is correct through reasoning concerning matters over which people disagree. Allah, Mighty and Majestic, said: {Indeed, the people most worthy of Abraham are those who followed him, and this Prophet and those who believe; and Allah is the Guardian of the believers.} We follow Abraham (peace be upon him) in disputation and debate, and so we are the people most worthy of him; all other people are commanded to do likewise. Allah, Exalted is He, said: {Say, 'Allah has spoken the truth, so follow the way of Abraham, inclining toward truth; he was not one of the polytheists.'} Debate, as we have mentioned, is part of his way. Whoever forbids debate and proof should know that he is disobeying Allah, Mighty and Majestic, and opposing the way of Abraham and Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon them). Allah, Mighty and Majestic, said, praising the Companions of the Cave..."
