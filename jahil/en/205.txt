When the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) failed to explain this to the free woman and the slave woman, the free man and the female slave, anyone who permits this has said that the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) did not convey the message as he was commanded. This is outright disbelief on the part of anyone who permits it. It is therefore necessarily the case that ignorance of all this does no harm at all. Discussion of these matters is only required when people engage in them; then it becomes necessary to explain the truth from the Quran and the Sunnah, in accordance with the words of Allah, Mighty and Majestic: {Be steadfast for Allah, bearing witness with justice}, and the words of Allah, Mighty and Majestic: {You shall surely make it clear to the people and not conceal it}. Whoever turns away after the truth has been explained is therefore a disbeliever, because he has neither accepted the judgment of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) nor submitted to what he decreed. It is authentically reported from the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) that a man who had never done any good deed said to his family when death came upon him, "When I die, burn me, then scatter my ashes on a windy day, half in the sea and half on land. For by Allah, if Allah, Exalted is He, has power over me, He will punish me with a punishment unlike any He has inflicted on anyone of His creation." Allah, Mighty and Majestic, gathered his ashes and brought him back to life, then asked him, "What made you do that?" He replied, "Fear of You, my Lord." And Allah, Exalted is He, forgave him for this statement.
Abu Muhammad said: This was a man who remained ignorant until he died that Allah, Mighty and Majestic, is able to gather his ashes and bring him back to life. Yet He forgave him because of his affirmation, his fear, and his ignorance. Some who distort words from their proper meanings have said that "if Allah has power over me" means only "if Allah constricts me," as in His words, Exalted is He: {But when He tests him and restricts his provision}. Abu Muhammad said: This is a false interpretation, which is impossible, because its meaning would then be, "If Allah constricts me, He will surely constrict me." Moreover, if that were the meaning, there would have been no point in his commanding them to burn him.
