A statement without evidence is a fabrication of lies against Allah, Mighty and Majestic.

And He, Exalted is He, said: {Rather, those who do wrong follow their desires without knowledge. So who can guide those whom Allah has led astray? They will have no helpers.} Thus, as you hear, He, Exalted is He, declared that whoever follows a statement and agrees with it without knowing that it is sound is a wrongdoer, and that whoever does not return to the truth he hears is among the people of the Fire. And He, Exalted is He, said: {If they do not respond to you, then know that they only follow their desires. And who is more astray than one who follows his desire without guidance from Allah? Indeed, Allah does not guide the wrongdoing people.}

Allah, Exalted is He, condemned a person for denying what he does not know, saying: {Rather, they denied what they had not encompassed in knowledge, and whose interpretation had not yet come to them. Thus did those before them deny; so see what was the end of the wrongdoers.} Thus, all that we have mentioned establishes the duty to refrain from what we do not know and to return to what the proof requires once it has been established. Allah, Exalted is He, also said: {And who is more unjust than one who invents a lie about Allah or denies the truth when it has come to him? Is there not in Hell a dwelling for the disbelievers?} Abu Muhammad said: This verse is sufficient to establish that no one should affirm anything for which no proof has been established, nor do anything that the proof has established. Who is more unjust than one who knows what we have mentioned, yet follows a whisper arising within himself, or a report for which no proof has been established that it must be believed, or blindly follows a person like himself, who may, in the sight of Allah, Exalted is He, be mistaken in what he supposes? In any case, that person is not infallible: he may err or be right. Allah, Exalted is He, also said: {And they said, "None will enter Paradise unless he is a Jew or a Christian." Those are their wishes. Say, "Produce your proof, if you are truthful."} Thus, Allah, Exalted is He, required that whoever is truthful in his claim produce proof; and if he does not produce it,

On the basis of proof, he is a liar and a falsifier, or an ignoramus. Allah, Exalted is He, said: {Here you are, disputing about that of which you have knowledge; why then do you dispute about that of which you have no knowledge? Allah knows, and you do not know.} Thus, He, Exalted is He, permitted disputation only on the basis of knowledge and prohibited it without knowledge. } ))

From the foregoing, it is clear that there is a difference between a believer whose faith is general and who suspends judgment about what he does not know, and one who fabricates lies against Allah and speaks without knowledge. The ruling on the latter is different. There is no room here for the objection that a person does not have enough time to learn all the sciences, because we say to him: Suspend judgment, do not fabricate lies against Allah, and say, "I believe in everything that has come from Allah"; otherwise, you will be punished as a fabricator. Allah called those who brought three upright, just witnesses to a crime of adultery, but were unable to bring a fourth, liars. He, Exalted is He, said: ((Why did they not produce four witnesses against it? Since they did not produce the witnesses, then in the sight of Allah they are the liars.)) Their backs are thus made liable to be flogged; indeed, the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) flogged Mistah al-Badri, whom Allah had forgiven, for this very reason: slandering a woman—any woman, even if she were the most immoral of the Muslims, so long as she kept her chastity and had not committed adultery.
