And His saying: "If you ask them, 'Who created the heavens and the earth?' they will surely say, 'Allah.'" Once their acknowledgment was established, He rebuked them, denouncing them, saying: "Say, 'Praise be to Allah.' But most of them do not know." And His saying, Exalted is He: "Is Allah better, or what they associate with Him? Or who created the heavens and the earth and sent down water for you from the sky, with which We caused gardens of delight to grow? You could not have caused their trees to grow." There is no doubt that the only answer they could possibly give is that the One capable of creating the heavens and the earth, and all that He mentions alongside them, is better than an inanimate object incapable of doing anything. Once their acknowledgment was thus established, He rebuked them, denouncing their associating partners with Him, saying: "Is there a god alongside Allah? Rather, they are a people who deviate." There are very many verses of this kind. For this reason, we have stated elsewhere that all questions concerning the oneness of lordship (tawhid al-rububiyya) are interrogative questions intended to elicit acknowledgment; once they acknowledge it, they are then rebuked and censured on the basis of that acknowledgment, because one who acknowledges lordship must necessarily acknowledge divinity (uluhiyya), as in His saying, Exalted is He: "Is there any doubt about Allah?" And His saying: "Say, 'Shall I seek a lord other than Allah?'" End quote. They likewise said that Allah rebuked the polytheists for failing to acknowledge divinity despite acknowledging lordship.

But this group differed among themselves. One faction declared those who committed polytheism to be unbelievers and said that they are punished in this world by being fought, and in the Hereafter they will remain forever in the Fire of Hell, since proof was established against them by reason and innate disposition, even if no messenger came to them or a messenger's message did not reach them. This is based on the aforementioned punishment of Amr ibn Luhayy and the Prophet's father (peace and blessings be upon him), despite His saying, Exalted is He: "No warner had come to them before you." The other faction said: We apply the terms polytheism and unbelief to them in this world, just as Allah applied them to these ignorant people to whom no messenger came; but punishment in this world and the Hereafter occurs only after messengers have been sent, and after the test on the Day of Resurrection, as in the hadith of al-Aswad ibn Sari.
