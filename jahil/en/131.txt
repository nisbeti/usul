He says: "As for those who are not of the people of Islam, whether Christians, Jews, Magians, or adherents of other religions, or the esotericists who claim that a human being is divine, or that someone became a prophet after the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him), they are not excused on the basis of interpretation at all. Rather, they are unbelievers and polytheists in every case." End quote from al-Durra fi ma yajibu i'tiqaduhu, p. 441.
It says in al-Fasl fi al-milal wa al-ahwa wa al-nihal (3/139):
"Abu Muhammad said: As for one against whom proof of the truth has not been established, whatever the matter may be, he is not an unbeliever unless there is a text declaring him an unbeliever, in which case one must stop at that—such as a person in the farthest reaches of the Zanj to whom only mention of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) has reached, and who refrains from seeking further information about him: he is an unbeliever."

And in al-Fasl fi al-milal wa al-ahwa wa al-nihal (3/142):
"One of their spokesmen said: 'If you excuse those who exercise independent reasoning when they err, then excuse the Jews, Christians, Magians, and adherents of all other religions, for they too exercise independent reasoning and seek what is good.' Our answer—and success is from Allah, Exalted is He—is that we did not excuse those whom we excused on the basis of our own opinions, nor declare those whom we declared unbelievers on the basis of our own conjecture. This is a prerogative that Allah, Mighty and Majestic, has granted to no one besides Himself. No one admits anyone to Paradise or Hell; rather, Allah, Exalted is He, admits to them whomever He wills. Thus, we call no one a believer except one whom Allah, Exalted is He, has so called, all of this being conveyed through the words of His Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him)..."
