I say: This distinction is more corrupt than the first, for most subsidiary rulings may be observed as acts of worship only in accordance with what was prescribed through the tongue of each prophet. It is not permissible to worship by prostrating to idols, permitting indecencies, killing people, wrongfully taking property, violating people's honor, bearing false witness, and the like. Although those who deny that things are intrinsically good or bad deem it permissible to worship in these ways and say that it is possible for revealed laws from Allah, Exalted is He, to prescribe them, their claim is among the most false of falsehoods. We have explained its corruption in more than sixty ways elsewhere in this book, and it is something whose invalidity is known necessarily.
He said: It has also been said that the foundational matter is one that may be known without the prior advent of revealed law, while the subsidiary matter is otherwise. This distinction, too, is utterly corrupt, for most of the issues they call foundational were not known until after the advent of revealed law, such as that a command entails obligation and a prohibition entails forbiddance, that analogy is authoritative, and that consensus is authoritative. Indeed, most issues of the foundations of religion were known only through transmitted revelation. The permissibility of seeing the Lord, Blessed and Exalted is He, on the Day of Resurrection, and His establishing Himself over His Throne, are unlike the question of His being, in His essence, above created things, for that is innate and self-evident. Most issues concerning the Resurrection and its details are not known before the advent of revealed law, including the issues of the punishment and bliss of the grave, the questioning by the two angels, and other foundational issues that are not known before the advent of revealed law.
Qadi Abu Bakr ibn al-Baqillani said: Any issue concerning which disagreement is forbidden once revealed law has been established, and whose contrary belief makes a person ignorant, is a foundational issue, whether rational or revealed. A subsidiary issue is one concerning which disagreement is not forbidden, or in which the one who errs does not incur sin.
