"The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) was asked about the people of a household among the polytheists who were attacked at night, with some of their children and women being struck. He said, 'They are of them.' This is an authentic hadith.

When the words of Allah, Exalted is He, were revealed: {Indeed, you will die, and indeed, they will die} [al-Zumar: 30] {Then, on the Day of Resurrection, you will dispute before your Lord} [al-Zumar: 31], the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) was asked, 'Messenger of Allah, will what happened between us in this world be brought up again, even with regard to particular sins?' He replied, 'Yes, it will surely be brought up again until you give everyone who is entitled to a right their due.' Al-Zubayr said, 'By Allah, this is indeed a grave matter.' The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) was also asked about medicines and incantations, whether they avert anything decreed by destiny. He replied, 'They are part of destiny.'"

"The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) was asked about those of the children of the polytheists who die. He replied, 'Allah knows best what they would have done.'"

Some of the Companions interpreted the word 'wrongdoing' in the words of Allah, Exalted is He, {and have not mixed their faith with wrongdoing} to mean every kind of wrongdoing. This was a matter of belief, and it troubled them greatly, so they asked, 'Which of us has not wronged himself?' The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) replied, 'That is not what it means; it means only polytheism. Have you not heard what Luqman said to his son: {My son, do not associate anything with Allah. Indeed, associating others with Allah is a tremendous wrongdoing} [Luqman: 13]?' He did not declare them unbelievers for this interpretation or belief of theirs.
