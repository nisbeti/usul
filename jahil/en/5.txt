Therefore, according to them, one who is ignorant of an attribute is an unbeliever if that attribute is known by innate disposition (fitra). They did not set a limit on what is known by innate disposition as opposed to what is not, and this led to their differing over whether one who is ignorant of some attributes is to be declared an unbeliever. For these people, the innate disposition upon which people were created encompasses, more or less, the religion of Islam and its branches. They hold that Allah fashioned the intellect to deem monotheism good and polytheism bad. I say "its forms" because some have said that only monotheism itself is established by innate disposition, meaning that Allah alone has the right to be worshipped—that He alone is to be obeyed. But, in their view, the forms of monotheism and the forms of polytheism are not established by reason, but by transmitted evidence. For example, prostrating to someone other than Allah is not, in their view, polytheism unless there is a textual proof, since prostration to Adam and Joseph is established, yet it was neither polytheism nor unbelief. This group held that proof is established against people by innate disposition, while some of them restricted what is known by innate disposition to the matter of monotheism and polytheism. As for other practical matters, and likewise some less apparent matters of unbelief, these are not established by innate disposition in their view, so one who is ignorant of them is excused. Among the proofs they cite is the hadith that every newborn is born upon the innate disposition, and in one narration, "upon this religion." Thus, they say, proof was established against people in this way. They also said that Allah, Exalted is He, judged the Prophet's father and mother, Ibn Jud'an, and Amr ibn Luhayy to be unbelievers, even though they lived during an interval between messengers, in light of His saying, "so that you may warn a people whose forefathers were not warned, and so they are heedless," and His saying, "We sent no warner to them before you." This indicates that they were held accountable on the basis of innate disposition or reason. There is no difference between an original polytheist and someone who became Muslim and then committed polytheism: neither is excused. Allah, Exalted is He, also said, "Are your unbelievers better than those, or have you been granted immunity in the scriptures?" There is likewise no difference in the ruling between one unbeliever and another. They also said that reason recognizes the repugnance of things before the advent of the revealed law.
