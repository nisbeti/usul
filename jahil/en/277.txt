The second view: Fighting in their ranks is disliked. This is the position of the Hanafis, the Shafi'is, and others.
Muhammad ibn al-Hasan said: "If the rule of the people of polytheism prevails, Muslims should not fight alongside the people of polytheism, unless they fear for their own lives, in which case they defend themselves."(42) Al-Siyar al-Saghir
It appears in "Ikhtilaf al-Ulama by al-Tahawi, abridged by al-Jassas" (3/428): "On seeking assistance from the polytheists"
Our companions said: There is no harm in seeking the assistance of polytheists to fight polytheists if the rule of Islam prevails and is applied to them. Seeking their assistance is only disliked when the rule of polytheism is manifest. This is the view of al-Shafi'i.
Al-Awza'i said: "I do not disapprove of seeking their help."
Malik said: "I do not think they should seek their help unless they are servants."
Abu Ja'far said: Allah, Glorified is He, said: {Do not take as confidants anyone other than yourselves}
And He said: {And I would not take the misguided as helpers.} Malik ibn Anas narrated from al-Fudayl ibn Abi Abd Allah, from Abd Allah ibn Niyar al-Aslami, from Urwa ibn al-Zubayr, from Aisha, who said: The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) set out before Badr. When he was at Harrat al-Wabara, a man came to him and said, "I have come to follow you and fight alongside you." The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said, "Do you believe in Allah and His Messenger?" He replied, "No." He said, "Then go back, for we will not seek help from a polytheist." The man came back to him three times, and the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said the same to him each time. The man said that he went back, then caught up with him at al-Bayda. The Prophet said to him what he had said the first time: "Do you believe in Allah and His Messenger?" He replied, "Yes." The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said, "Then set out."
Abu Ja'far said: This indicates that he was one of the idol worshippers, because he called him to believe in Allah, whereas the People of the Book believe in Allah.
