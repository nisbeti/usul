Some of them sought protection by prostrating, but the killing among them was swift. This reached the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him), and he ordered that half the blood money be paid for them. He said, "I disavow every Muslim who lives among the polytheists." They said, "O Messenger of Allah, their fires should not be visible to one another." Here, the Companions assumed that these people had remained disbelievers, based on their interpretation, so the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) paid blood money for them and did not declare those who had killed them disbelievers.

Another example is the hadith, "None of you should pray the afternoon prayer except at Banu Qurayza." It appears in Sahih al-Bukhari (5/112):

Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Asma narrated to us: Juwayriya ibn Asma narrated to us, from Nafi, from Ibn Umar (may Allah be pleased with them both), who said: The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said on the day of the Confederates, "None of you should pray the afternoon prayer except at Banu Qurayza." Some of them caught the afternoon prayer while on the way. Some said, "We will not pray until we reach it," while others said, "Rather, we will pray; he did not mean for us to do that." This was mentioned to the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him), and he did not rebuke any of them.

The Companions differed on a matter of creed, namely whether the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) saw Allah. Most of the Companions held that the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) did not see Allah, Exalted is He, with his eyes on the Night Journey and Ascension.

It is authentically reported from Aisha (may Allah be pleased with her) that she said, "Whoever tells you that Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him) saw his Lord has lied," and she would say, "Sight cannot perceive Him..." Narrated by al-Bukhari (Tawhid/6832).
