(1) It was part of Allah's decree that He referred the matter to men to judge, though, had He willed, He could have judged it Himself. Thus, the judgment of men was permissible.
"I adjure you by Allah: Is the judgment of men to restore harmony and spare their blood better, or judgment concerning a hare?"
They said, "Yes; that is better."
"And concerning a woman and her husband: {If you fear a breach between them, appoint an arbiter from his family and an arbiter from her family} (2). I adjure you by Allah: Is the judgment of men to restore harmony between them better than their judgment concerning a woman's private parts?! Have you withdrawn from this?"
They said, "Yes."
I said, "As for your claim that he fought but neither took captives nor seized spoils: Would you take your mother Aisha captive, making lawful with her what you make lawful with other women, when she is your mother?! If you say, 'We make lawful with her what we make lawful with other women,' then you have disbelieved. And if you say, 'She is not our mother,' then you have disbelieved, for Allah, Exalted is He, says: {The Prophet has a greater claim on the believers than they have on themselves, and his wives are their mothers} (3). So you are caught between two errors; find a way out of this. Have you withdrawn from this?"
They said, "Yes."
"As for his erasing the title 'Commander of the Faithful,' I shall give you something you will accept. On the day of al-Hudaybiyya, the Prophet of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) made a truce with the polytheists and said to Ali:
'Write, Ali: This is what Muhammad, the Messenger of Allah, has made a truce upon.' They said, 'If we knew you were the Messenger of Allah, we would not have fought you!'
