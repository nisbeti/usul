Through two chains of transmission from Ikrima ibn Ammar:

1 - Abd al-Rahman ibn Mahdi, from him.

2 - Umar ibn Yunus al-Yamami, from him.

The hadith scholar Ahmad Shakir graded its chain of transmission authentic in his edition of "al-Musnad" (5/67/3187), and the hadith scholar al-Albani graded its chain good in "Sahih Sunan Abi Dawud," no. (3406).

There are several points in this hadith.

First: What is apparent from it is that Ali (may Allah be pleased with him) did not hold the Kharijites to be unbelievers. Otherwise, if he had believed them to be unbelievers, how could he have refrained from carrying out the prescribed punishment for apostasy against them? This is not something one would think of him.

Second: Ibn Abbas did not hold the Kharijites to be unbelievers. This report states that clearly, where he said: "I said: 'As for your claim, "He fought but took no captives and seized no spoils"—would you then take your mother Aisha captive, deeming lawful with her what you deem lawful with other women, when she is your mother?!'" This clearly shows that he regarded them as believers, since Aisha is only the Mother of the Believers.
