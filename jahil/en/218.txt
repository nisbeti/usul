Among the Companions, concerning the hypocrites, Allah did not declare the group that interpreted the matter to be unbelievers, in His saying, {What is the matter with you that you have become two groups concerning the hypocrites, when Allah has cast them back because of what they have earned? Do you wish to guide those whom Allah has led astray? Whoever Allah leads astray, you will never find a way for him (88).} It says in al-Tahrir wa al-Tanwir (5/149):
The interrogative is for expressing astonishment and reproach. The definite article in "the hypocrites" is for reference to those already known, and "two groups" is an adverbial state referring to the pronoun governed by the preposition "for"; thus it qualifies the element governing it, namely the reproach. It is therefore clear that the object of reproach is their division. "Concerning the hypocrites" is connected to "two groups," which is interpreted to mean "divided"; its meaning is "concerning the matter of the hypocrites," since a ruling does not pertain to the persons of the hypocrites themselves.
The word fi'a means a group. Its pattern is fi'la, derived from fay' (return), because members of a group turn to one another in their affairs. Its original form was fay', but the ya was omitted from its middle because of frequent use, and the ta was added in its place.
It is understood that division into two groups can only mean division over one of two possible positions. The context is a discussion of faith and unbelief; that is, "What is the matter with you that you are divided between those who declare them unbelievers and those who excuse them, and that you apply the rulings of faith or unbelief to them?" It is said that this verse was revealed concerning those who withdrew on the day of Uhud: Abd Allah ibn Ubayy and his followers. The Muslims differed over whether to describe them as believers or unbelievers because of what they had done. In Sahih al-Bukhari, Zayd ibn Thabit reported: "Some people returned from Uhud, among the Companions of the Prophet, and the people split into two groups concerning them: one group said, 'Kill them,' and another said, 'No.' Then the verse 'What is the matter with you that you have become two groups concerning the hypocrites' was revealed."
