Seeking knowledge only ((Here is a selection of statements by scholars explaining the criterion for being able to learn:

Some of them will mention circumstances in which ignorance is excused and those in which it is not, as examples of being able or unable to learn. These are the circumstances I mentioned above.

1 - Abu Muhammad ibn Hazm, may Allah have mercy on him

He said: "I have seen people who hold that the religious laws do not bind someone who is ignorant of them, or someone to whom they have not been conveyed."

Abu Muhammad said: "This is false. Rather, they do bind him, because the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) was sent to all human beings, all jinn, and everyone not yet born, once they reach maturity after birth."

(Ability to learn) Abu Muhammad said: "Allah Most High said, commanding him to say: {I am the Messenger of Allah to you all} (al-A'raf: 158). This is a general statement from which no one may be excluded. And He Most High said: {Does man think that he will be left neglected?} (al-Qiyama: 36). Thus, Exalted is He, denied that anyone could be left neglected. The neglected person is one who is left without being commanded or forbidden. He, Mighty and Majestic, denied that this could be so. But a person is excused for his ignorance and for being unable to attain knowledge alone. As for someone to whom news of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) has reached, wherever he may be, even in the farthest reaches of the earth, it is incumbent upon him to seek him out. Once his warning has reached him, it is incumbent upon him to believe in him and follow him, seek the religion that is binding upon him, and leave his homeland for that purpose. Otherwise, he has incurred disbelief, everlasting residence in the Fire, and punishment, as stated explicitly in the Quran.
