He then ordered that man to be sought out, and he was brought to him, until I saw him matching the description the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) had given of him: "Among them is a black man, one of whose upper arms has no bone in it; it is like a woman's breast, quivering like a woman's breast." Then, when Ali saw this man killed, he said, "Indeed, my close friend told me that they are three brothers from among the jinn. This is the eldest of them; the second has a large following, and the third is weak." Shu'ayb deemed its chain of transmission good, and Ahmad Shakir authenticated it. Thus, if we consider this hadith authentic, one of the leaders of these jinn who took human form was among them. Then: "the worst of creation and creatures"—al-Albani authenticated it (and it is clear that they are unbelievers). Then: "The worst of those killed beneath the sky are they, three times over; and the best of those killed beneath the sky is the one they killed." Shu'ayb authenticated it. By tracing the hadiths and the characteristics reported concerning the Kharijites, we find that, alongside the characteristics mentioned, they include rulings. These rulings are that they are "the worst of creation" and "wherever you find them, kill them." Abu Umama, one of the Companions who narrated the hadith, said that they "disbelieved after their faith." These are three rulings concerning this world; then, in the Hereafter, they are "the dogs of Hell." We then ask whether these hadiths concern specific people—that is, people whom the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) knew through revelation would deliberately leave the religion, following their desires without interpretation, and whom Allah informed him about by their characteristics.
