Al-Sha'bi, from Alqama, from Salama ibn Yazid al-Ju'fi, who said: "My brother and I went to the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him). We said, 'Messenger of Allah, our mother, Mulayka, used to maintain ties of kinship, honor guests, and do such-and-such. She died in the pre-Islamic era. Will that benefit her in any way?' He said, 'No.' We said, 'She also buried a sister of ours alive in the pre-Islamic era. Will that benefit her in any way?' He said, 'The woman who buries a child alive and the child who is buried alive are in the Fire, unless the woman who buried the child alive lives to see Islam, in which case Allah will forgive her.'"

This is an authentic hadith; its narrators are among the narrators of the Sahih collections. These people were ignorant of the ruling concerning the unbeliever in the Hereafter, which is a matter of belief, yet he did not declare them unbelievers. A man was ignorant of the fate of his unbelieving father, who had died in the pre-Islamic era, and asked the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) about him, "Where is my father?" He did not declare him an unbeliever; rather, he told him that his father was in the Fire. This is a matter of belief, and the hadith is in Sahih Muslim. A man was ignorant of the nature of jihad. Abu Musa reported: The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) was asked about a man who fights out of courage, one who fights out of zeal, and one who fights to be seen by others: "Which of these is in the path of Allah?" The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) replied, "Whoever fights so that the word of Allah may be supreme is in the path of Allah." This is a matter of belief, yet he did not declare him an unbeliever. A woman was ignorant that she had to perform ghusl after experiencing a wet dream, yet he did not declare her an unbeliever; the hadith is in al-Bukhari. The Companions were ignorant that the spoils had to be divided before the animals were slaughtered, so they slaughtered the sheep before they were divided, yet he did not declare them unbelievers; the hadith is in al-Bukhari. The Companions were ignorant of where people would be when the earth was changed into another earth, and the heavens likewise, so he explained to them that they would be in darkness, before the Bridge.
