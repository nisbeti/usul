On these two grounds, people differed over declaring the proponents of figurative interpretation to be unbelievers. Once you understand this, the reason for their disagreement will become clear to you, and the correct position is to refrain from declaring them unbelievers.

And refraining from definitively condemning them to perdition, while applying the rulings of Islam to them in matters of retaliation, inheritance, marriage, blood money, funeral prayers, burial in Muslim cemeteries, and all their other dealings. But they are to be dealt with sternly through painful discipline, severe rebuke, and ostracism, until they abandon their innovation. This was the practice of the earliest generation regarding them. In the time of the Companions and afterward, among the Successors, there arose people who held these views concerning predestination, as well as the Khawarij and the Mu'tazila. Yet they did not disinter anyone's grave or deprive anyone of an inheritance; rather, they ostracized them and disciplined them with beatings, exile, and execution, according to their circumstances, because they were disobedient, misguided sinners who committed major sins. (I say: See here the account of Ali's dealings with the Khawarij. Al-Ash'ari transmitted the statement, "All the Khawarij say that the Quran was created." [Maqalat al-Islamiyyin: 108])

Among the scholars who have investigated the matter and the Sunnis who have not declared them unbelievers, there is disagreement, contrary to those who held otherwise. And Allah is the One who grants success in attaining what is correct.

Qadi Abu Bakr said: "As for questions concerning promise and threat, the vision of Allah, the createdness of the Qur'an, the creation of acts, the permanence of accidents, generation, and similar subtle matters, the prohibition against declaring those who interpret them to be unbelievers is all the clearer, since ignorance of any of them does not constitute ignorance of Allah, Exalted is He, nor have the Muslims unanimously agreed to declare anyone who is ignorant of any of them an unbeliever. In the preceding section, we have already presented the discussion and the nature of the disagreement on this matter, which makes it unnecessary to repeat it, by the help of Allah, Exalted is He." He said in al-Shifa bi-Ta'rif Huquq al-Mustafa (2/272).
