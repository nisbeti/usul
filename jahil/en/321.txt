))The hadith of Abu Umama has already been mentioned. In a narration by Ahmad from Abu Ghalib, he said: When the heads of the Azariqa were brought and set up on the steps of Damascus, Abu Umama (may Allah be pleased with him) came. When he saw them, his eyes filled with tears, and he said: "Dogs of the Fire" (three times). "These are the worst of those killed beneath the sky, and the best of those killed beneath the sky are those whom these people killed." He said: I asked, "Why are your eyes filled with tears?" He replied, "Out of compassion for them; they were among the people of Islam." We asked, "Did you say that these are dogs of the Fire based on your own opinion, or is it something you heard from the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him)?" He said, "I would be bold indeed!" Rather, I heard it from the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) more than once, twice, or three times." He said: Then he counted them many times.))
Here, the Companions Abdullah ibn Abi Awfa and Abu Umama report that these particular people, the Azariqa, are dogs of the people of the Fire. It is authentically reported concerning the Khawarij who came before those fought by Ali that they would leave the religion. These hadiths, then, concerned specific people. For this reason, Ibn Hazm said in al-Muhalla bi al-Athar (12/173):
