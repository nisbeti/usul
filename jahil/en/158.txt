Falsehood may be mixed into it so thoroughly that no one can distinguish it with certainty. For if that were possible, the Reminder would not be preserved, and the words of Allah, Exalted is He, "Indeed, it is We who sent down the Reminder, and indeed, We will preserve it," would be a lie and a broken promise. No Muslim would say this. If someone says that Allah meant by this the Quran alone, and that it is the Quran whose preservation He guaranteed, but not the rest of the revelation that is not Quran, we say to him—and Allah is the source of success—this is a baseless claim, unsupported by proof, and an unjustified restriction of the meaning of the Reminder. Anything of this kind is false, in light of Allah's words: "And they say, 'None will enter Paradise unless he is a Jew or a Christian.' Those are their wishes. Say, 'Produce your proof, if you are truthful.'" It is thus established that he has no proof for his claim, and so he is not truthful in it. The word "Reminder" applies to everything Allah sent down to His Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him), whether Quran or Sunnah—revelation by which the Quran is explained. Moreover, Allah, Exalted is He, says: "With clear proofs and scriptures. And We sent down to you the Reminder so that you may make clear to people what was sent down to them, and so that they may reflect." It is thus established that he (peace and blessings be upon him) was commanded to explain the Quran to people. The Quran contains many matters stated in general terms, such as prayer, zakat, pilgrimage, and other things whose details of what Allah, Exalted is He, has made obligatory upon us are not known from its wording alone; rather, they are known through the explanation of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him). If his explanation of those general matters were not preserved and guaranteed to be free of what does not belong to it, then the benefit of the Quran's text would be lost, and most of the religious duties it makes obligatory upon us would be invalidated. If we did not know what Allah, Exalted is He, truly intended by them, then what would remain would be the error of the one who erred or the deliberate lie of the liar—and Allah forbid this.
