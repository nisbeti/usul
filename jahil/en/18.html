This is speaking about Allah without knowledge, and it is forbidden; its perpetrator has no excuse. The blind following of those who cited their forefathers did not avail them at all.

Allah, Exalted is He, says: {Thus We did not send before you any warner to a town without its affluent people saying, "We found our fathers following a way, and we are following in their footsteps."} (23)

Thus, there is a difference between someone who has embraced faith in general terms and then does not invent anything—whether deliberately or by blind following—that contradicts that faith, thereby speaking about Allah without knowledge; and someone who remains silent about that and says, "I believe in everything the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) brought, and I do not speak about Allah's religion without knowledge." These are two different things.

Ibn Hazm said: ("Al-Ihkam fi Usul al-Ahkam by Ibn Hazm" (6/12):

"Among the things that utterly invalidate their position are the words of Allah, Exalted is He: {And do not say, as to what your tongues falsely describe, 'This is lawful and this is forbidden,' in order to fabricate lies against Allah. Indeed, those who fabricate lies against Allah will not prosper.} And His words, Exalted is He: {Say, 'Have you considered the provision Allah has sent down for you, of which you have made some forbidden and some lawful?' Say, 'Has Allah given you permission, or are you fabricating lies against Allah?'} These two verses establish that anyone who declares lawful or forbidden something without permission from Allah, Exalted is He, to forbid or permit it, has fabricated a lie against Allah. We are certain that Allah, Exalted is He, has made lawful for us everything He created on earth, except what He explicitly specified as forbidden, in accordance with His words, Exalted is He: {And why should you not eat of that over which Allah's name has been mentioned, when He has clearly explained to you what He has forbidden you, except when you are compelled to do so? Indeed, many lead others astray through their desires, without knowledge. Your Lord knows best those who transgress.} These two clear texts thus invalidate the forbidding of anything by anyone on the pretext of precaution or fear."
