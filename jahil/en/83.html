Their spokesman said: "If you excuse those who exercise independent reasoning when they err, then excuse the Jews, Christians, Magians, and all other religious communities, for they too exercise independent reasoning, seeking what is good." Our answer—and Allah Most High is the source of success—is that we did not excuse those whom we excused on the basis of our own opinions, nor declare those whom we declared unbelievers to be so on the basis of our conjecture. This is a prerogative that Allah, Mighty and Majestic, has granted to no one besides Himself. No one enters Paradise or Hell; rather, Allah Most High admits to them whomever He wills. We therefore call no one a believer except one whom Allah Most High has so called—all of this being conveyed through the words of His Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him). No two people on earth disagree—not merely among the Muslims, but among people of every religious community—that the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) unequivocally declared unbelievers the adherents of every religion other than Islam, whose followers disavowed every religious community except the one he (peace be upon him) brought. So we stopped at that, and no two people disagree either that he (peace be upon him) unequivocally called everyone who followed him, believed everything he brought, and disavowed every religion besides it, a believer. So we stopped at that as well, and there is nothing more to add. Thus, if there is an explicit text declaring someone outside Islam after the designation of Islam has applied to him, we declare him outside it, whether or not there is consensus on his exclusion. Likewise, if the Muslims unanimously agree that someone is outside Islam, it is obligatory to follow that consensus. As for someone whose exclusion from Islam after Islam has applied to him is established neither by an explicit text nor by consensus, it is not permissible to remove him from what has been established with certainty as applying to him. Allah Most High has explicitly stated what we have said, saying: {Whoever seeks a religion other than Islam, it will never be accepted from him, and in the Hereafter he will be among the losers.} And He Most High said: {They wish to make a distinction between Allah and His messengers, and say, "We believe in some and disbelieve in others," and wish to adopt a way in between.}
