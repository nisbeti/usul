Ibn Mandah, may Allah have mercy on him, said in his book al-Tawhid, 1/314: "Chapter: Citing the evidence that one who exercises independent reasoning but errs in knowing Allah, Mighty and Majestic is He, and His oneness is like one who stubbornly resists." Then he said: "Allah Most High said, informing us of their misguidance and stubborn resistance: {Say, 'Shall We inform you of those who are the greatest losers in their deeds, those whose efforts in the life of this world have gone astray, while they think that they are doing good?'}"

Al-Qurtubi said in his Tafsir, 7/319, regarding the verse of the covenant, at its end: "There is no excuse for the imitator in matters of divine oneness." End quote.

Abu al-Wafa Ibn Aqil, may Allah have mercy on him, said that whoever calls upon the occupant of a shrine and slips written petitions into graves has committed major shirk. The imams of the da'wa have often quoted him on this matter approvingly. Shaykh Muhammad said in Tarikh Najd, p. 266: "Ibn Aqil stated that they are unbelievers on account of this act"—meaning calling upon the occupant of a shrine and slipping written petitions into graves. Shaykh Aba Butayn said: "Ibn Aqil's statement has already been cited, in which he unequivocally declared unbelievers those whom he described as ignorant of the excesses they committed regarding graves. Ibn al-Qayyim quoted him approvingly." Al-Durar, 10/386.

Ibn Farhun said in Tabsirat al-Hukkam, in the chapter on apostasy: "A question: Whoever worships the sun, the moon, a stone, or anything else is to be executed and not asked to repent."

The sons of Shaykh Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab and Hamad ibn Nasir said in Al-Durar, 10/136–138, when asked: "If a person who believes in Allah and His Messenger says or does something that constitutes unbelief, out of ignorance of that fact, do you refrain from declaring him an unbeliever until the proof of the message has been established against him? And if someone in this state was killed before this da'wa appeared, is that an issue or not?"
