-Among the proofs is Khalid's killing of Banu Jadhima, thinking that they remained in disbelief—the hadith is as follows.

Mahmud narrated to me: Abd al-Razzaq narrated to us: Ma'mar informed us. And Nu'aym narrated to me: Abdullah informed us: Ma'mar informed us, from al-Zuhri, from Salim, from his father, who said: The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) sent Khalid ibn al-Walid to Banu Jadhimah and called them to Islam. They could not properly say, "We have submitted," so they kept saying, "We have become Sabaeans, we have become Sabaeans." Khalid began killing some of them and taking others captive, and he assigned each of us a captive. Then, one day, Khalid ordered each of us to kill his captive. I said, "By Allah, I will not kill my captive, and none of my companions will kill his captive," until we came to the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) and told him about it. The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) raised his hands and said, "O Allah, I disavow before You what Khalid has done," twice.

[Sh: (Banu Jadhima) was one of the Arab tribes. (We have left our religion) We have moved from one religion to another. They intended to enter Islam, but Khalid (may Allah be pleased with him) thought that they had not submitted, since they had not said, "We have embraced Islam." (I disavow myself before You) I apologize. (For what Khalid did) in killing and taking these people captive.] Here Khalid declared unbelievers those who did not deserve to be declared unbelievers, acting on his own interpretation. [6766]
