Aisha said: "We administered the ladud to him during his illness, and he began gesturing to us, 'Do not administer the ladud to me.' We said, 'The sick person's aversion to medicine.' When he came to, he said, 'Did I not forbid you to administer the ladud to me?' We said, 'The sick person's aversion to medicine.' He said, 'Let no one in the house remain without being given the ladud while I watch, except al-Abbas, for he was not present with you.'"
This shows that someone who offers a far-fetched interpretation is not excused, because the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) punished them. The ladud is medicine placed in one side of the mouth, or administered by rubbing it onto the palate, inserting a finger into the patient's mouth and applying the medicine to it. The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) explicitly forbade them from doing this by gesturing, but they assumed that he did not mean to forbid it, and merely disliked the medicine. This was a far-fetched assumption. The hadith also shows that a prohibition indicates unlawfulness, and that a far-fetched interpretation is not accepted.
"Sahih al-Bukhari" (1/31):
Abdullah ibn Amr reported: The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) fell behind us during a journey we had undertaken. He caught up with us when we had delayed the prayer—the Asr prayer—and we were performing ablution. We began wiping our feet, so he called out at the top of his voice, "Woe to the heels from the Fire!" twice or three times.
In this hadith, the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) did not excuse them, because the matter of ablution was well known. And Allah knows best.
Contradiction
By this heading, I mean that a person's contradiction indicates that he follows his own desires and is not excused. For example, a man says that a mursal hadith is sound. Then you want to establish proof against him, so you bring him a mursal hadith with a sound chain of transmission. He says to you, "This is mursal; I do not accept it because it is weak." Such a person is contradictory and is not excused, and proof has thereby been established against him.
