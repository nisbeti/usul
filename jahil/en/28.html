We found that He said: {We send the messengers only as bearers of glad tidings and warners. But those who disbelieve dispute with falsehood in order to refute the truth by it, and they take My signs and the warnings given to them in mockery.} Thus, as you see, Exalted is He condemned disputation without proof and disputation in support of falsehood. By this, Exalted is He refuted the claim of the deluded: "Every person who is led astray is furnished with an argument." Exalted is He made clear that the deluded person is one who is not furnished with an argument, whereas the one who is right is, in truth, furnished with an argument; they are the people of truth. And Exalted is He said: {Those who dispute concerning the signs of Allah without any authority having come to them—it is greatly hateful in the sight of Allah and in the sight of those who believe. Thus does Allah seal the heart of every arrogant tyrant.} These verses together clarify both blameworthy disputation and praiseworthy, obligatory disputation. The obligatory kind is that in which the person disputing seeks to make the truth manifest. The blameworthy kind has two forms, as stated in the verses we have cited: first, disputing without knowledge; and second, disputing in support of falsehood through sophistry and deception after the truth has become clear to him. This makes clear that the truth is one, and that nothing is valid except what is established by rational proof. These are the blameworthy people of whom Allah, Exalted is He, said: {Have you not seen those who dispute concerning the signs of Allah? How are they turned away?} And His saying, Exalted is He: {Among the people are those who dispute concerning Allah without knowledge and follow every rebellious devil.} And His saying, Exalted is He: {Among the people are those who dispute concerning Allah without knowledge, guidance, or an illuminating Book, turning his neck aside to lead others astray from the path of Allah. For him there is disgrace in this world, and on the Day of Resurrection We shall make him taste the punishment of the Blaze.}

Ibn Taymiyya (may Allah have mercy on him) said: "The ruling of a divine address is not established with respect to those legally responsible until they are able to know it; this is the soundest of the opinions." (3).
