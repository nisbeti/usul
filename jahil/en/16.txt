As for failure to learn in itself, it does not entail denial, since it may be due to inattention, never having turned one's thoughts to the question, or culpable negligence that does not indicate hatred or arrogance—as is the case with many Muslims regarding many details. If it were said that the criterion is the ability to learn, then the masses would have to be declared unbelievers merely because they are ignorant despite being able to acquire knowledge. Indeed, the Companions' knowledge during the Prophet's lifetime did not encompass every detail; otherwise, they too would have to be declared unbelievers, even though Allah has declared them upright and is pleased with them. Likewise, one who enters Islam—with a general understanding of the two testimonies of faith and a readiness to submit to the details—would be an unbeliever until he learned those details. He would remain an unbeliever for a period until he learned them, because he could learn them immediately after uttering the two testimonies, without any interval. And if he learned them over a period of time, he would be an unbeliever because he could have learned them in a shorter period but did not! End quote. However, their words apply to someone who has embraced faith in general and uttered the two testimonies, then remained ignorant of the details and refrained from speaking about them until he learned them, without speaking about what he did not know. For example, Zayd ibn Amr ibn Nufayl believed in Allah in general and that He alone deserves worship, and stopped there. He did not speak about Allah without knowledge, nor did he falsely attribute anything to Allah by entering into polytheistic rites without knowledge, for by doing so he would falsely attribute to Allah an act of worship that He had not prescribed. In such a case, he would outwardly appear to be excused. The discussion, rather, concerns someone who turns away from learning, then follows conjecture in attributing to Allah what he does not attribute to Himself, through various forms of polytheistic worship or other kinds of unbelief, or denies something of what Allah has revealed without knowing it, following conjecture or desire.
