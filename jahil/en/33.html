It follows the apparent meaning of the ruling: the children and the insane among the unbelievers are unbelievers in the rulings of this world, and are treated like their guardians. With this distinction, the difficulty in the matter is resolved.] (1).

Al-Qarafi, may Allah have mercy on him, said: ["The ninety-fourth distinction: between the principle of what ignorance is not an excuse for and the principle of what ignorance is an excuse for." Know that the Lawgiver has been lenient regarding certain forms of ignorance in the Sharia, pardoning those who commit them, while in other cases He has taken people to account for their ignorance and has not pardoned those who commit them. The criterion for the forms of ignorance that are pardoned is that they are ones that it is ordinarily impossible to guard against. As for what it is possible to guard against without hardship, it is not pardoned.] (2).

Al-Qarafi also said: [The principle of the Sharia indicates that any ignorance which a legally responsible person can dispel is no proof for the ignorant person. For Allah, Exalted is He, sent His messengers to His creation with His messages and made it obligatory upon all of them to learn them and then act upon them. Thus, knowledge and acting upon it are obligations. (Punishment is for the act, not merely for negligence.) Whoever neglects learning and acting, and remains ignorant, has committed two acts of disobedience by neglecting two obligations. If he learns but does not act, he has committed one act of disobedience by neglecting to act. And whoever learns and acts has attained salvation.] (3). The commentator said: [Because the principle of the Sharia indicates that any ignorance a legally responsible person can dispel is no proof for the ignorant person, especially when a long time has passed and the days have continued, for what is not known today will be known tomorrow.] (4). His words, ["especially when a long time has passed"], are supported by the words of Allah, Exalted is He: (Did We not grant you a life long enough for whoever would take heed to take heed, and did the warner not come to you? So taste the punishment, for the wrongdoers will have no helper.) Fatir: 37.
