I asked him, 'What do you say about someone who says that the Quran is created?' and I related the man's words to him.

(58) Ahmad ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Khidr ibn Masrur. Al-Khatib said: He was trustworthy and devout, sound in belief, and staunch in adherence to the Sunnah. Tarikh Baghdad 4/237
(59) Ahmad ibn Sulayman ibn al-Hasan, the jurist and hadith memorizer. Al-Khatib said: He was truthful and knowledgeable, and compiled a large book on the Sunan.
Tarikh Baghdad 4/189-192
(60) I found no biographical entry for him.
(61) I found no biographical entry for him.
(62) Yahya ibn Khalaf Abu Muhammad al-Tartusi. Al-Dhahabi said: He is unknown. He related an objectionable statement from Malik: Someone asked him, "What do you say about the Quran, regarding one who says it is created?" He replied, "A heretic. Seize him and kill him." He said, "I am only relating what was said." Malik replied, "I have heard it only from you." God forbid that Malik should order the killing of someone who relates unbelief or judge him to be a heretic.
Al-Dhahabi also said: He is not trustworthy; he attributed to Malik something unacceptable.
Dhayl Diwan al-Duafa wa al-Matrukin (533); al-Mughni fi al-Duafa (6957); Mizan al-Itidal (4/372)
