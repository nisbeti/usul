Likewise, He related that Hud said to his people: (Worship Allah; you have no god other than Him. You are only fabricators.) He thus called them fabricators before issuing a ruling that they would contravene, because they had set up another god alongside Allah. Likewise, His saying, Exalted is He: (Say, "Allah does not command indecency. Do you say about Allah what you do not know?" (28)) shows that indecency is known by reason. Otherwise, if indecency meant only what Allah has forbidden—since indecency could be known only through textual revelation, not reason—the verse would mean, "Allah does not command what He has forbidden," and this is false. This shows that abominations, the greatest of which is associating partners with Allah, are known by innate disposition and reason before a prohibition comes in revelation, so no one who is ignorant of them has an excuse. The Noble Quran frequently argues from the disbelievers' acknowledgment of His lordship, Exalted and Glorified is He, to the obligation of singling Him out in worship. Thus, when addressing them about the oneness of lordship, He asks them a question that elicits their affirmation. Once they acknowledge His lordship, He uses it as proof against them that He alone deserves to be worshipped, and rebukes them, condemning their association of others with Him despite their acknowledgment that He alone is the Lord. For whoever acknowledges that Allah, Exalted is He, alone is the Lord must also acknowledge that He alone deserves to be worshipped. Examples of this include His saying, Exalted is He: (Say, "Who provides for you from the heaven and the earth, or who controls hearing and sight?") up to His saying: (They will say, "Allah.") When they acknowledged His lordship, He rebuked them, condemning their association of others with Him, saying: (Then say, "Will you not be mindful of Allah?") Another example is His saying, Exalted is He: (Say, "To whom belong the earth and all who are in it, if you know?" They will say, "To Allah.") When they acknowledged this, He rebuked them, condemning their association of others with Him, saying: (Say, "Will you not then reflect?") Then He said: (Say, "Who is the Lord of the seven heavens and the Lord of the Mighty Throne?" They will say, "Allah.") When they acknowledged this, He rebuked them, condemning their association of others with Him, saying: (Say, "Will you not be mindful of Allah?") Then He said: (Say, "In whose hand is the dominion of all things, and who protects, while none can protect against Him, if you know?" They will say, "Allah.") When they acknowledged this, He rebuked them, condemning their association of others with Him, saying: (How, then, are you deluded?) Another example is His saying, Exalted is He: (Say, "Who is the Lord of the heavens and the earth?" Say, "Allah.") When their acknowledgment was established, He rebuked them, condemning their association of others with Him, saying: (Say, "Have you then taken protectors besides Him, who have no power to benefit or harm themselves?")

And His saying, Exalted is He: (If you ask them, "Who created the heavens and the earth and subjected the sun and the moon...")
