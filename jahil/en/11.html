Likewise, fighting and jihad against the polytheists may take place only after the proof has been established, though before that they are polytheists." Thus, as you can see, they distinguished between merely applying the name and the rulings concerning punishment in the Hereafter and worldly punishment by killing. Another group said that the name entails punishment in the Hereafter, but not worldly punishment by killing and captivity. They said that as long as the name applies, its rulings in the Hereafter apply: they will be punished in the Hereafter. As for the words of the Exalted, "We would never punish until We had sent a messenger," what is meant is their punishment in this world. Al-Shawkani transmitted this view from the majority. As for the Hereafter, they will be punished in the Fire, because the Prophet's father was punished, despite the words of the Exalted, "No warner had come to them before you."

Another group distinguished between someone who was originally a polytheist and someone who entered Islam and then committed polytheism. They said: an original polytheist is called a polytheist and is punished in this world by being treated as one; as for the Hereafter, his case depends on whether a messenger came to him or not. If no messenger came to him, he will be tested on the Day of Resurrection—as stated in the hadith—with a fire into which he will be commanded to enter. Whoever enters it will find it cool and safe, and whoever does not enter it will be dragged into it." This is based on the hadith, "No one from this community, whether Jew or Christian, hears of me and then fails to believe in me without being among the people of the Fire."
