Comment: It is astonishing that they cite Ibn Taymiyya here, since, as noted above, Ibn Taymiyya did not declare the Ash'aris individually to be unbelievers, nor did he transmit such a view from the Salaf, as they do when declaring individuals unbelievers. Rather, he transmitted a general, unrestricted ruling from the Salaf here. Ibn Taymiyya also said: "An unrestricted declaration of unbelief, like an unrestricted threat, does not entail declaring a particular person an unbeliever until the proof has been established against him, the rejection of which constitutes unbelief."
