The one ignorant of it is excused on account of his ignorance, for knowledge of this cannot be attained through reason, reflection, or thought.

This includes what Allah, Exalted is He, has informed us: that He is All-Hearing, All-Seeing, and that He has two hands, as He says: {Rather, both His hands are outstretched}; and that He has a right hand, as He says: {And the heavens will be folded in His right hand}; and that He has a face, as He says: {Everything will perish except His face}, and: {And there will remain the face of your Lord, Possessor of majesty and honor}; and that He has a foot, as the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: "Until the Lord places His foot in it"—meaning Hell.

And that He laughs at His believing servant, as the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said of the man who was killed in the path of Allah: "He met Allah, Mighty and Majestic, while He was laughing at him."

And that He descends every night to the lowest heaven, according to the report of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him).

And that He is not one-eyed, as the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said when mentioning the Dajjal: "He is one-eyed, but your Lord is not one-eyed." And that the believers will see their Lord on the Day of Resurrection with their eyes, just as they see the sun when no cloud veils it, and just as they see the full moon, according to the words of the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him). And that He has fingers, as the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said: "There is no heart except that it is between two of the fingers of the Most Merciful."

These meanings that have been described, and others like them, are among the things with which Allah, Mighty and Majestic, has described Himself, or with which His Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) has described Him. Their true nature cannot be grasped through thought or reflection. We do not declare anyone an unbeliever for being ignorant of them unless they have been conveyed to him.
