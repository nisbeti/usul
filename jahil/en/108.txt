(Ali, may Allah be pleased with him, dealing with the Kharijites and excusing them on account of their interpretation.) This is alongside al-Ash'ari's explicit statement: "All the Kharijites say that the Quran was created" (Maqalat al-Islamiyyin, p. 108).
I cite this statement here even though I consider the Kharijites disbelievers, because of the Prophet's statement about them: "They are the worst of creation; they leave the religion and then do not return to it; they are the dogs of Hell." But I present what is reported from Ali here to show that he excused these people on account of their interpretation. This is evidence that his position was to excuse on the basis of interpretation, and he was a rightly guided caliph and one of the leading figures among the early generations. We also mentioned Ibn Abbas excusing them when he said to them, "Isn't your mother Aisha?" This indicates that he regarded them as believers, since Aisha is the Mother of the Believers, so there is no need to repeat it here. Likewise, it is not reported that any of the Companions declared Ali a disbeliever over this matter. This shows that the position of everyone who learned of Ali's action, whether through a report or directly, was not to declare the one who excuses someone acting on an interpretation a disbeliever. Here are the texts:
Ibn Abi Shayba recorded in his Musannaf (5/332), from Tariq ibn Shihab, who said: I was with Ali when he was asked about the people of al-Nahr: "Are they polytheists?" He replied, "They fled from polytheism." He was asked, "Are they hypocrites, then?" He replied, "The hypocrites remember Allah only a little." He was asked, "What are they, then?" He replied, "People who have rebelled against us." Its editor said: "Its chain of transmission is hasan." And Allah, Exalted is He, knows best.
