The sending of these people mentioned above is well known through mass transmission, from both disbelievers and believers. None of the scholars or Muslims doubts it, nor that they were sent only for the reason we have stated. It is impossible and false to suppose that the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) sent them to people who would not be bound by the proof established through their conveying the message, who would not be obliged to accept what they taught them of the Quran and the rulings of religion, what they gave them as legal opinions, or to submit to what they reported to them in all these matters from the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him). For if that were so, his sending them would have been pointless, and he would have been saying to the Muslims: "I have sent you people whom you are not obliged to accept when they convey to you what I have said, and whom you are required to disregard when they transmit something from me or tell you what I have said." Whoever holds this view has departed from Islam. The same applies to someone who grows up in a village or city where there is only one Quran reciter, one hadith scholar, or one mufti. We say to those who disagree with us: What do you say? Is someone obliged, when he studies the Quran with that reciter, to believe what he teaches him and affirm that it is the word of Allah, Exalted is He, and remain firm in that belief? Or must he doubt and not affirm that it is the word of Allah, Mighty and Majestic? If they say that he is obliged to affirm that it is the word of Allah, Exalted is He, we say: You are right. So what difference is there between their transmission of the Quran and their transmission of the other Sunnahs, when both are from Allah, Exalted is He, and acceptance of both is obligatory? But if they say that he must doubt it until he meets a large number of people, they have committed a grave offense against religion. We then ask them about someone who meets two, three, or four people in this way: they must set a limit.
