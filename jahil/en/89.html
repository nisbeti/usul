"Is he a disbeliever?" This is an explicit statement from Ahmad that the one who excuses him is not to be declared a disbeliever. 2/

Al-Marwadhi related concerning a group in Tarsus who declare anyone who does not declare others unbelievers to be an unbeliever. He said: "I have heard nothing about this." [Al-Riwayatayn wa-al-Wajhayn, Masa'il al-Aqida (p. 111), and al-Furu (6/568)]

Al-Bukhari said (20): "I have examined the words of the Jews, Christians, and Magians, and I have seen none more astray in their disbelief than they are. I regard as ignorant anyone who does not declare them disbelievers, except one who does not know of their disbelief."(21)

Abu al-Shaykh al-Asbahani(22) said: "Whoever doubts the disbelief of one who says that the Quran is created, after knowing this and hearing it from scholars who are approved, is like him." End quote. [Al-Hujja fi Bayan al-Mahajja, 1/240]
