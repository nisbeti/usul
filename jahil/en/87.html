As for one who calls people to enjoining what is right, forbidding what is wrong, making the Quran and the Sunnah manifest, and ruling with justice, he is not a rebel; rather, the rebel is the one who opposes him. And with Allah, Exalted is He, lies success." Here Ibn Hazm mentioned the matters for which the Kharijites declared others unbelievers and by which they opposed the definitive consensus. Among them he mentioned the invalidation of stoning, and he did not excuse those who held this view on the basis of an interpretation.

It says in al-Tanbih wa al-Radd ala Ahl al-Ahwa wa al-Bida (p. 185):

Hassan ibn Farukh said: "Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz (may Allah be pleased with him) asked me what the Azariqa say, so I told him. He said, 'What do they say about stoning?' I replied, 'They declare it unbelief.' He said, 'Allah is greatest! They have disbelieved in Allah and His Messenger.'" End quote.

It says in al-Fasl fi al-Milal wa al-Ahwa wa al-Nihal (4/144):

The rest of the Azariqa, who are the followers of Nafi ibn al-Azraq, held that stoning a married adulterer should be abolished. They cut off a thief's hand at the shoulder, and required menstruating women to pray and fast during their menstruation. Some of them said, "No; rather, she makes up the prayers when she becomes pure, just as she makes up the fasts." They deemed the blood of children who were not in their army lawful, and also killed women who were not in their army. The Azariqa disavowed anyone who stayed behind from going out to fight, whether due to weakness or for any other reason. They declared anyone who opposed this view an unbeliever after the death of the first among them to hold it, but did not declare him an unbeliever for opposing it during his lifetime. They said that they should test everyone they encountered who was not from their army, and kill him if he said, "I am a Muslim"; but they forbade killing anyone affiliated with the Jews, Christians, or Magians. It was concerning this that the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) testified that they would depart from the religion as an arrow passes through its prey, when he said that they would kill the people of Islam and leave the idolaters alone. This is among the signs of his prophethood (peace and blessings be upon him), for he foretold this, and it is one of the particulars of the unseen; thus it came to pass exactly as he said." End quote.
