So consider, may Allah have mercy on you: despite the repugnance of the Jahmiyya's doctrine, he did not declare their specific adherents to be unbelievers; rather, he prayed for them. Nor did he support the people of polytheism and the Crusaders against them. Instead, he rejoiced at the conquest of Amorium and openly expressed his joy at it. Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyya says in his response to Ibn al-Bakri: "For this reason, the people of knowledge and the Sunnah do not declare those who oppose them to be unbelievers, even if those opponents declare them unbelievers, because declaring someone an unbeliever is a legal ruling, and a person may not retaliate in kind. If someone lies about you and commits adultery with your wife, you may not lie about him or commit adultery with his wife, because adultery and lying are forbidden as a right owed to Allah, Exalted is He. Likewise, declaring someone an unbeliever is a right owed to Allah, Exalted is He, so no one is declared an unbeliever except one whom Allah and His Messenger have declared an unbeliever." End quote, p. 257.
