3—"Whoever invokes another god alongside Allah, for which he has no proof, his reckoning is only with his Lord. Indeed, the disbelievers will not succeed." 117

This verse clearly shows that anyone who associates another with Allah by invoking someone other than Him must produce proof for his action. Since he has produced no proof, the argument has thereby been established against him, and Allah will call him to account for it. He will be among the disbelievers who do not succeed. This verse demonstrates the gravity of associating partners with Allah.
