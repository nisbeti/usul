Al-Fakhr al-Razi said: "In his discussion of the Kharijite sects:

The other sects among them agree that a servant becomes an unbeliever through sin. They declare Uthman and Ali, may Allah be pleased with them, as well as Talha, al-Zubayr, and Aisha, unbelievers, while holding Abu Bakr and Umar in high regard." End quote.

Al-Ashari said in Maqalat al-Islamiyyin, ed. Zarzur (1/84):

"The general position of the Kharijites:

The Kharijites unanimously agreed that Ali ibn Abi Talib, may Allah be pleased with him, was an unbeliever if he ruled; but they differed over whether his unbelief constituted associating partners with Allah.

They unanimously agreed that every major sin is unbelief, except the Najdat, who do not hold this view.

They unanimously agreed that Allah, Exalted is He, punishes those who commit major sins with everlasting punishment, except the Najdat, the followers of Najda."

"It is stated in Fath al-Bari that Shaykh Taqi al-Din al-Subki said in his fatwas: 'Those who declared the Kharijites and the extremist Rafidites unbelievers argued that they did so because they declared the leading Companions unbelievers, which entails accusing the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) of falsehood in his testimony that they would enter Paradise. He said: In my view, this is a sound argument.'"
