The consensus was transmitted. But did al-Lalaka'i transmit a consensus in the sense they understood it—that the Salaf declared anyone who did this an unbeliever without qualification, even if acting on an interpretation? Let us examine his words. In Sharh Usul I'tiqad Ahl al-Sunna wa al-Jama'a (1/197), it says:
"We were informed by Muhammad ibn al-Muzaffar al-Muqri, who said: Al-Husayn ibn Muhammad ibn Habash al-Muqri narrated to us, who said: Abu Muhammad Abd al-Rahman ibn Abi Hatim narrated to us, who said: I asked my father and Abu Zur'a about the doctrines of the people of the Sunnah concerning the fundamentals of religion, what they had found the scholars in all the regions adhering to, and what they themselves believed regarding these matters. They replied: 'We found the scholars in all the regions—Hijaz, Iraq, Syria, and Yemen—adhering to the following doctrine: faith is speech and action; it increases and decreases. The Quran is the speech of Allah, uncreated in all its aspects. Both the good and the evil of divine decree are from Allah, Mighty and Majestic. The best of this community after its Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) is Abu Bakr al-Siddiq, then Umar ibn al-Khattab, then Uthman ibn Affan, then Ali ibn Abi Talib (peace be upon them). They are the rightly guided caliphs. The ten whom the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) named and testified would enter Paradise are as the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) testified, and his word is true. We pray for mercy upon all the Companions of Muhammad and refrain from discussing what occurred between them. Allah, Mighty and Majestic, is above His Throne, distinct from His creation, as He described Himself in His Book and through the words of His Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him), without asking how; His knowledge encompasses all things. {There is nothing like Him, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing} [al-Shura: 11]. And He, Blessed and Exalted, will be seen in the Hereafter: the people of Paradise will see Him with their eyes and hear His speech, as and however He wills. Paradise is true and the Fire is true; both are created and will never perish. Paradise is the reward for His devoted servants, and the Fire is the punishment for those who disobey Him, except those upon whom Allah, Mighty and Majestic, has mercy. The Bridge is true, and the Balance is true; it has two pans, on which the deeds of the servants will be weighed"
