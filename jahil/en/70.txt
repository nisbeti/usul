It was also related from Sahnun that he held the same view regarding one who says that Allah has no speech: he is an unbeliever. Reports from Malik differ. In a report from the Syrians, Abu Mushir and Marwan ibn Muhammad al-Tatari, he stated categorically: "Unbelief applies to them." He was consulted about marrying a Qadari and said, "Do not marry him." Allah, Exalted is He, says: {A believing slave is better than a polytheist}. It was also related from him (perhaps: from him) that all the people of heretical doctrines are unbelievers. He said that whoever describes any aspect of Allah's essence, or points to some part of his own body—a hand, hearing, or sight—should have that part cut off, because he has likened Allah to himself. Regarding one who says that the Quran is created, he said: "He is an unbeliever, so kill him." He also said, in a report from Ibn Nafi, that he should be flogged, beaten severely, and imprisoned until he repents. In a report from Bishr ibn Bakr al-Tinnisi, Malik said that he should be killed and his repentance not accepted. Qadi Abu Abd Allah al-Barnakani and Qadi Abu Abd Allah al-Tustari, among the leading Iraqi scholars, said that Malik's response varied: the one who is firmly convinced and calls others to the doctrine is to be killed. On this same point, his statements differed regarding repeating the prayer performed behind them. Ibn al-Mundhir related from al-Shafi'i that a Qadari is not asked to repent. Most of the statements of the early scholars declare them unbelievers. Among those who held this view were al-Layth, Ibn Uyayna, and Ibn Lahi'a; this was related from them concerning one who says that the Quran is created. The same view was held by Ibn al-Mubarak, al-Awdi, Waki, Hafs ibn Ghiyath, Abu Ishaq al-Fazari, and Ali ibn Asim, among others. It is the view of most hadith scholars, jurists, and theologians concerning them, the Kharijites, the Qadaris, those who follow deviant doctrines, and the proponents of interpretive innovations. This was also the view of Ahmad ibn Hanbal. They said the same of those who suspend judgment and those who doubt these foundational doctrines.
