"Our people have taken gods besides Him. Why do they not produce clear proof for them? Who is more unjust than one who fabricates a lie against Allah?" Thus Allah, Mighty and Majestic, praised them for rejecting what their people said, since their people had no clear proof for their claim, and He affirmed as true their statement that whoever claims...

A statement without evidence is a lie fabricated against Allah, Mighty and Majestic.

Allah, Exalted is He, says: {And who is more unjust than one who is reminded of the signs of his Lord, then turns away from them? Indeed, We will take retribution from the criminals.} No one is more unjust than one against whom proof has been established from the Book of Allah, Exalted is He, and the words of His Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him), yet turns away from it, though it is the decisive proof and the clear argument. Allah, Exalted is He, says: {Those who consume usury will not stand except as one stands who is being beaten by Satan into madness. That is because they say, "Trade is just like usury." But Allah has permitted trade and forbidden usury. So whoever receives an admonition from his Lord and desists may keep what is past, and his affair rests with Allah. But whoever returns to it—those are the companions of the Fire; they will abide therein forever.} And He, Exalted is He, says: {Rather, those who do wrong follow their desires without knowledge. So who can guide one whom Allah has led astray? They will have no helpers.} Thus, as you hear, He, Exalted is He, has informed us that whoever follows a statement that accords with his view without knowing that it is sound is an unjust person, and that whoever does not return to the truth he hears is among the people of the Fire. Allah, Exalted is He, says: {If they do not respond to you, then know that they only follow their desires. And who is more astray than one who follows his desire without guidance from Allah? Indeed, Allah does not guide the wrongdoing people.} Allah, Exalted is He, condemned a person for denying what he does not know, saying: {Rather, they have denied what they have not comprehended...}
