Are solitary reports all true if they are transmitted specifically by reliable narrators, or are they all false, or do they contain both truth and falsehood? If they say that they contain both truth and falsehood—and this is their position—we say to them: Is it permissible for a law that Allah, Exalted is He, revealed to His Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) so that he might explain it to His servants to be corrupted, until it becomes mixed with a lie fabricated by a wicked person and attributed to the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him), or with an error made by someone who errs, so that the truth enjoined upon us becomes mixed with fabricated falsehood in such a way that the truth can never be distinguished from falsehood by anyone? Are all the Islamic laws preserved and binding upon us, or are they not preserved, and are not all of them binding upon us, but rather many of them ceased to apply after the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him)? Has Allah, Exalted is He, established proof against us regarding the laws He prescribed, in that they are clear to us and distinguishable from what He did not command us to do? Or has Allah, Exalted is He, not established proof against us in religion, because much of it is mixed with lies and can never be distinguished from them? If they allow the laws of religion that Allah, Exalted is He, revealed to His Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) to be mixed with what is not in
the religion, and they said, "No proof from Allah, Exalted is He, has been established against us concerning what He commanded us to do," they would fall into the same argument for the corruption of the Sharia, the disappearance of Islam, and the invalidation of Allah's guarantee to preserve the Reminder as others have fallen into, letter for letter, exactly alike. They would be bound to have abandoned much of the sound religion, just as others are bound to be acting on what is not part of the religion, and that the Prophet's (peace and blessings be upon him) clarification has been nullified, and that Allah's proof through him has not been established against us, exactly alike. This is self-evident.
