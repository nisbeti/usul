Thus, everything we have mentioned makes it clear that one must refrain from what one does not know and return to what the proof requires once it has been established. Allah, Exalted is He, said: {Who is more unjust than one who fabricates a lie against Allah or denies the truth when it comes to him? Is there not in Hell a dwelling for the disbelievers?} Abu Muhammad said: This verse is sufficient to establish that no one should affirm anything for which no proof has been established, nor do anything for which proof has been established. Who is more unjust than one who knows what we have mentioned, yet follows a whisper arising within himself, or a report for which no proof has established that it must be believed, or imitates a person like himself, who may, in the sight of Allah, be mistaken in what he supposes? In any case, he is protected from error, yet he may be right or wrong. Allah, Exalted is He, said: {And they say, "None shall enter Paradise unless he is a Jew or a Christian." Those are their wishful fancies. Say, "Bring your proof, if you are truthful."} Thus Allah required that whoever is truthful in his claim must produce proof; and if he does not produce it,
By proof, he is a liar and a falsifier, or an ignorant person. Allah, Exalted is He, said: {Here you are, disputing about that of which you have knowledge; why then do you dispute about that of which you have no knowledge? Allah knows, and you do not know.} Thus, He, Exalted is He, permitted disputation only on the basis of knowledge and prohibited it without knowledge.

Say, "My Lord knows best their number. None knows them except a few."
