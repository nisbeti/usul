And is it possible, in your view, for a ruling founded on a lie or an erroneous supposition to have been accepted and passed into effect, and become so thoroughly mixed with the rulings of the Sharia that no one among the people of Islam anywhere in the world could ever distinguish it, or is neither of these two possibilities possible in your view?

If they say, "They can never occur; rather, we have secured against that," then they have adopted our position and affirmed that every report transmitted by a trustworthy narrator from another trustworthy narrator, with an unbroken chain back to the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), concerning religion, is true and was indeed said by him, and that it yields certain knowledge. We affirm its soundness and hold that a fabricated report, or one mistakenly attributed to him, which the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) never said, can never become mixed with it in such a way that falsehood can never be distinguished from truth. But if they say, "Rather, all of that is possible," then they have judged that the religion of Islam has been corrupted and most of it invalidated, and that what Allah, Exalted is He, commanded has become mixed with what He did not command in a way that no one can ever distinguish; and that they can never know what Allah, Exalted is He, commanded them to do, as distinct from what He did not command them to do, nor what liars and frivolous people have fabricated, as distinct from what the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) brought, except through conjecture, which is the most false of speech and avails nothing against the truth.
