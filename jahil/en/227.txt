By Allah, with their strongest oaths, they swear that they are with you. Their deeds have come to nothing, and they have become losers.

These two verses show that their companions fell into disbelief and were not excused by claiming worldly interests as a reason for committing disbelief. Thus, anyone who justifies his disbelief by citing a worldly interest that he supposes to be valid, without evidence that the Sharia recognizes it as a barrier to declaring someone a disbeliever (such as coercion, for example), will not have that justification accepted, nor will it prevent the ruling from being applied to him.

4. "Or do they say, 'He has fabricated it'? Say, 'Then bring a surah like it, and call upon whoever you can besides Allah, if you are truthful.' Rather, they have denied what they have not comprehended in knowledge, and whose interpretation has not yet come to them. Thus did those before them deny. So see what was the end of the wrongdoers."

The discussion of this verse takes another angle: sometimes you present someone with sound proof from the Book or the Sunnah, and he finds no evidence to counter what you have said. As a result of following his own desires, he resorts to rejecting this proof and calling you a liar. Allah, Exalted is He, makes clear that whoever does this is a wrongdoer, and that we must not reject what we have not comprehended.
