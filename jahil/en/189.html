How great the difference between the two! For if one reporting from the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) were assumed to have lied deliberately or by mistake, and nothing appeared to indicate his falsehood, that would necessarily entail leading people astray. The discussion concerns a report that the community received with acceptance and acted upon, and by which it affirmed the attributes and acts of the Lord. For reports that must be accepted according to the Sharia cannot be false in reality, especially when the entire community has accepted them. The same must be said of every proof that must be followed according to the Sharia: it can only be true, and what it indicates must therefore be established in reality.

This applies to what is reported concerning the law of the Lord, Exalted is He, and His names and attributes, unlike a particular testimony concerning a particular person against whom testimony is given; the implication of such testimony may not be established in reality. He distorted the issue by claiming that a report through which Allah has made the community accountable and made Himself known to them, on the tongue of His Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him), in affirming His names and attributes, could be false and untrue in reality. For it is one of Allah's proofs against His servants, and Allah's proofs cannot be false or untrue; rather, they can only be true in reality. It is not permissible for the proofs of truth and falsehood to be equal, nor is it permissible for a lie about Allah, His law, and His religion to be indistinguishable from the revelation He sent down to His Messenger and by which He made His creation accountable, such that one cannot be distinguished from the other. For the difference between truth and falsehood, truthfulness and lying, and the revelation of Satan and the revelation of the angel from Allah is too clear for either to be confused with the other. Indeed, Allah has placed a light upon the truth.
