For us, and until the Day of Resurrection—or are they not enduring? If they say, "Rather, they are enduring until the Day of Resurrection," they have returned to our position and acknowledged that the truth in all that Allah, Exalted is He, revealed concerning religion is clear: what He did not reveal has not been conveyed to us, and this remains so until the Day of Resurrection. This is precisely our position: that a report transmitted by a trustworthy individual from another like him, with an unbroken chain back to the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), is certainly true in what it makes clear, and entails both knowledge and action.

But if they say, "Rather, they are not enduring," they have fallen into a grave matter and declared that much of the religion has been lost, that the conveyance of many of the laws has ceased, and that the Messenger of Allah's (peace and blessings be upon him) clarification of much of the religion has vanished so completely that it can never be recovered. This is the position of the Rafidites—or worse than theirs, for the Rafidites claimed that the true religion exists with a person whose presence in the world is assured, whereas these people have abolished it from the entire world. We seek refuge in Allah from both positions.

Furthermore, Allah, Exalted is He, says: {Say, "My Lord has forbidden only indecencies, both open and hidden, sin, wrongful aggression, associating with Allah that for which He has sent down no authority, and saying about Allah what you do not know."} And He, Exalted is He, says: {They are nothing but names that you and your fathers have given them; Allah has sent down no authority for them. They follow nothing but conjecture and what their souls desire, though guidance has come to them from their Lord.} And He, Exalted is He, says: {They have no knowledge of it; they follow nothing but conjecture, and conjecture can avail nothing against the truth.} And Allah, Exalted is He, censures a people who said: {When it was said, "Allah's promise is true, and there is no doubt about the Hour," you said, "We do not know what the Hour is. We only suppose; we are not certain."} And He, Exalted is He, says: {Those who associate others with Allah will say, "Had Allah willed, we would not have associated anything with Him, nor would our fathers, nor would we have forbidden ..."}
