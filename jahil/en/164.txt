And the Exalted says: "Whoever seeks a religion other than Islam, it will never be accepted from him, and in the Hereafter he will be among the losers." And the Exalted says: "Indeed, the religion with Allah is Islam. Those who were given the Scripture differed only after knowledge had come to them, out of mutual envy. And whoever disbelieves in the signs of Allah, then Allah is swift in reckoning." And the Exalted says: "Humanity was one community, then Allah sent the prophets as bearers of good news and warners, and sent down with them the Book in truth to judge between people concerning that over which they differed. None differed over it except those who had been given it, after clear proofs had come to them, out of mutual envy. Then Allah guided those who believed to the truth concerning that over which they differed, by His permission. And Allah guides whomever He wills to a straight path." Abu Muhammad said: We say to those who hold it possible that what Allah, Exalted is He, commanded His Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) to explain to us of the law of Islam has not been preserved, and that it may have been altered and mixed with fabricated lies in such a way that they can never be distinguished: Tell us, does Allah's perfecting the religion, His choosing Islam as our religion, and His refusal to accept any religion other than Islam all remain in force for us until the Day of Resurrection, or were they only for the Companions (may Allah be pleased with them), or for neither the Companions nor us? One of these positions must be taken. If they say, "For neither the Companions nor us," then whoever says this is an unbeliever, for he openly denies Allah, Exalted is He. No Muslim would say this.
