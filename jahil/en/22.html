The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) obligates those in the farthest reaches of the earth to believe in him and learn his laws. If they die in that state, they die as unbelievers and go to the Fire. This contradicts the words of Allah, Exalted is He: {Allah does not burden any soul beyond its capacity. It will have what it has earned, and it will bear what it has acquired} (al-Baqara: 286), for no one is capable of knowing the unseen.

If they say, "This is the proof of the group who hold that no one is bound by any of the religious laws until they reach him," we say: They have no proof in this, because everything people are charged with is within their capacity and what their constitution can bear. However, they are excused because it has not reached them, and they have not been charged with it in a way that would bring punishment if they failed to do it. Rather, they have been charged with it in a way that does not bring punishment until it reaches them. "Whoever learns from the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) that a ruling has been prescribed for him, whether in general terms or without its specific text reaching him, is obliged to exert himself in seeking that ruling; otherwise, he is disobedient to Allah, Exalted is He. Allah, Exalted is He, says: {So ask the people of knowledge if you do not know} (al-Nahl: 43)." (1)

Ibn Hazm also said: "Allah, Exalted is He, says: {We would never punish until We had sent a messenger} (al-Isra: 15). Thus it is established that no unbeliever is punished at all until the warning of the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him) reaches him. As for one to whom mention of the Prophet Muhammad (peace and blessings be upon him) and what he brought has reached, but who can find no one in his land to tell him about it, he is obliged to leave it for a land where he can investigate the facts. Were it not for his statement, peace be upon him, that there is no prophet after him, we would be obliged to do this with everyone we hear claiming prophethood." Then he said: "Anyone among us who is in a remote place where he can find no one to teach him the laws of his religion, whether man or woman, is obliged to travel to a place where they can find ..."
