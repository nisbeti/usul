As for legal rulings, there is no disagreement among the people of the faith that no oath is required of someone who denies something in a debate concerning matters other than legal rulings. Ali said: When two disputants disagree, one affirming something and the other denying it, each must provide evidence for the truth of his claim, as we explained above, in accordance with the ruling of the words of Allah, Exalted is He. Whichever of them establishes proof, his statement is correct. They cannot both establish proof, because the truth cannot lie in two opposites, and it is impossible for something to be both false and true at the same time and in the same respect. If both are unable to establish evidence—and this is possible—the ruling on that matter is to suspend judgment: neither affirming nor denying it, but leaving it within the realm of possibility. For if the one affirming it established proof, the matter would indeed be affirmed as true; and if the one denying it established proof, it would be false and denied. If neither of them establishes proof, it is said of that matter: "It is possible that it is true, and possible that it is false; but we neither affirm it nor rule by it, nor do we assert categorically that it is false." This is precisely what the words of Allah, Exalted is He, state: {Do not pursue that of which you have no knowledge. Indeed, the hearing, the sight, and the heart—all of these will be questioned.} It has also been narrated from the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him), in a hadith concerning the People of the Book: "We neither affirm nor deny; rather, we say: Allah knows best."
((Allah, Exalted is He, says: {Do not pursue that of which you have no knowledge. Indeed, the hearing, the sight, and the heart—all of these will be questioned.} And He says, censuring a people who said: {And when it was said, 'Allah's promise is true, and there is no doubt about the Hour,' you said, 'We do not know what the Hour is. We only suppose, and we are not certain.'} And He says: {They are nothing but names that you and your forefathers have given them; Allah has sent down no authority for them. They follow nothing but conjecture and what their souls desire, though guidance has certainly come to them from their Lord.} And He says: {They have no knowledge of it; they follow nothing but conjecture, and conjecture avails nothing against the truth.} Thus, by the explicit words of Allah, Exalted is He, from which no Muslim turns away, it is established that conjecture is other than the truth; and since it is other than the truth, it is falsehood and a lie, without doubt, for there is no third possibility. The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: 'Beware of conjecture, for conjecture is the most false of speech.' Abu Muhammad said: This is the truth, and no one who hears it can turn away from it.))
