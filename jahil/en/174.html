Ali said: Since this position has now been established with certainty, and everything else has been invalidated, let us, with the help of Allah Almighty, discuss its classification. We say, and through Allah Almighty we seek support: We are certain, praise be to Allah, that no law which the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) commanded, recommended, or practiced could be lost without reaching someone among his community, either through mass transmission or through the transmission of a trustworthy narrator from another trustworthy narrator, until it reached him (peace and blessings be upon him). We are also absolutely certain that Allah Almighty would not single out for transmitting it someone whose transmission, even if he were upright, could not establish proof. We are also absolutely certain that there could not be a law in which its trustworthy narrator errs without a clear and evident explanation establishing that he has erred in it. And we are also absolutely certain, or that He would declare…

Allah, Mighty and Majestic, from one whose transmission has established proof for us, concerning the wording of a hadith containing a ruling that he attributes to someone whose transmission establishes proof, until he traces it back to the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him). Likewise, we state categorically and affirm that every report that has only ever been transmitted as a mursal report, or has only ever been narrated by an unknown narrator or one whose discrediting is established, is undoubtedly a false report, fabricated and not uttered by the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him). For if it were possible that it was true, it would be a valid ruling of the Sharia, but one not binding upon us, since proof has not been established against us concerning it.

Ali said: This ruling that we have set out applies only to what has been transmitted by those whose uprightness is agreed upon, such as the Companions and the trustworthy Successors, then Shu'ba, Sufyan, Sufyan, Malik, and the other imams of their time and those who came after them, down to us and until the Day of Resurrection; and to everyone whose criticism is established, such as al-Hasan ibn Umara, Jabir al-Ju'fi, and all the other narrators whose criticism is established.
