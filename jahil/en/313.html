Second: Determining the effective cause of this ruling and its meaning means verifying that the effective cause we have identified is present in a person, so that we may judge him to be one of the Kharijites.

Let us begin by identifying the effective cause. The first question that comes to mind here is: What characteristics of the Kharijites did the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) mention? First, we list the descriptions that could constitute the effective cause of the ruling. Then we refine the effective cause by excluding incidental descriptions (those that do not affect the ruling). What remains are the descriptions that may serve as the effective cause of the ruling, upon which the designation and, in turn, the punishment follow.

The characteristics mentioned by the Messenger of Allah are that they are young in age (that is, young people), and foolish in understanding (that is, ignorant, with foolish minds). They speak with the words of the best of creation (that is, they recite the Quran or quote hadiths). In the hadith it says, "They recite the Quran, but it does not go beyond their throats." It has been said that this means they do not act upon it, and it has also been said that their recitation is not accepted. Then they kill the people of Islam and leave the idolaters alone. Then they leave the religion as an arrow passes through its prey (that is, they leave it entirely and become unbelievers, or they leave it through innovation or misguidance without becoming unbelievers, as Ibn Battal related from the majority). Then, "they will not return to it until the arrow returns to its notch." "People will emerge from the direction of the East." He said: "Their distinguishing mark is shaving"—or he said, "removing the hair" (and removing the hair means shaving it off). Then: "Their sign is a black man, one of whose upper arms is like a woman's breast, or like a piece of flesh that wobbles. They will emerge at a time when the people are divided."
