The second view: Fighting in their ranks is permissible in order to free oneself from captivity. This is the view of al-Awzai, al-Thawri, and Ahmad ibn Hanbal.
Al-Awzai said: "They may fight only if they stipulate that, should they prevail, they will return them to the land of Islam."(95) Mukhtasar Ikhtilaf al-Ulama (3/454)
Al-Thawri said: "They may fight alongside them."(96) Mukhtasar Ikhtilaf al-Ulama (3/454)
Abu Dawud said: "I said to Ahmad: If an enemy were to attack the people of Constantinople, and the king said to the captives, 'Go out and fight, and I will give you such-and-such,' what then? He replied: If he tells them, 'I will set you free,' then there is no harm in it, in the hope that they will escape."(97) Masa'il al-Imam Ahmad, narrated by Abu Dawud al-Sijistani (332)
.

Preferred view: It appears to the researcher that the second view is stronger, and that there is no evidence prohibiting fighting alongside them in order to secure one's release from captivity. Accordingly, fighting alongside them in this case does not constitute allegiance to them. And Allah knows best.

It says in al-Muhalla bi al-Athar (11/356):
If we are able to set the unbelievers among the people of war against one another, so that they fight each other, and Muslims enter among them and use them as a means to harm others, that is good.
The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: "Allah supports this religion through people who have no share [in the Hereafter]," as was narrated to us by Abd Allah ibn Rabi, who said: Muhammad ibn Muawiya narrated to us, who said: Ahmad ibn Shuaib informed me, who said: Imran ibn Bakkar ibn Rashid narrated to us, who said: Abu al-Yaman narrated to us, who said: Shuaib—namely, Ibn Abi Hamza—narrated to us, from al-Zuhri, who said: Said ibn al-Musayyib informed me, who said: Abu Hurayra narrated to us that the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: "Allah indeed supports this religion through a wicked man."

It is noteworthy that Ibn Hazm's view is that it is permissible to enter the ranks of the polytheists in order to inflict harm on another polytheist, and this is very similar to our present issue.
