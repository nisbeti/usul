"As for one to whom an unsound report from the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) has reached, and an interpreter, an ignorant person, or a sinner whom he did not know to be sinful has deemed it authentic for him, that is the extent of his knowledge. If he acts on the falsehood that has reached him, he is excused on account of his ignorance and bears no sin, for he did not deliberately incline toward sin, and deeds are judged by intentions. He is a mujtahid, rewarded once for his intention and purpose of seeking good and obedience to Allah and His Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him). If he acts contrary to what has reached him, then the sin is that of one who takes lightly his opposition to the Messenger (peace and blessings be upon him): either he does so knowingly, in which case he is a sinner, or he does so with that intention, in which case he is an unbeliever. And success is from Allah, Exalted is He."

He said: "The correct view is that we have found Allah, Exalted is He, to condemn those who affirm something without knowledge, and to condemn those who deny something without knowledge. He says, Exalted is He: {Say: My Lord has only forbidden indecencies, both outward and inward, sin, wrongful aggression, associating with Allah that for which He has sent down no authority, and saying about Allah what you do not know.} Allah, Exalted is He, has forbidden, in the explicit wording of this verse, anyone to say anything about Allah, Mighty and Majestic, whose truth he does not know. The truth of anything beyond the first principles of reason and the immediate evidence of the senses can be known only through proof. Thus, this verse obliges anyone who claims to establish something to produce proof for it; otherwise, he has done what is forbidden to him. And He says, Exalted is He: {Rather, they have denied what they did not comprehend in knowledge, and whose interpretation had not yet come to them. In the same way, those before them denied. So see what was the end of the wrongdoers.} Thus, He, Exalted is He, condemned a person for denying something without knowing that it is false. And He says, Exalted is He: {They say, 'None shall enter Paradise unless he is a Jew or a Christian.' Those are their wishes. Say: 'Bring your proof, if you are truthful.'} Thus, Allah, Exalted is He, required everyone who claims to be truthful to produce proof; otherwise, his statement is invalid. We have found that everyone who denies something claims to be truthful in denying it, and everyone who affirms something claims to be truthful in affirming it. Therefore, both groups are obliged to produce proof for their claims, if they are truthful.
