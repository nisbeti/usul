He said regarding the issue of elections:

"Some people are brought to see those dazzling advertisements bearing, in bold letters, 'Islam is the solution,' and similar slogans with which the idolatrous legislators deceive ordinary Muslims. They vote for them and choose them out of love for Islam and loyalty to its Sharia, without knowing or intending the blocked, idolatrous method those representatives follow to enforce some of the limits of the Sharia, as they claim. All of this must be taken into consideration, along with the fact that the voter has not personally engaged in legislation, sworn to respect unbelieving laws, submitted to them for judgment, or committed any of the other statements or actions of unbelief that the representatives engage in. It is well known that the voter does not do or personally engage in all of this (*); rather, he votes and appoints as his representative the person he chooses.

If he intends to authorize or appoint someone to act on his behalf who commits these explicit acts of unbelief, then his ruling is the same as that of the person he appointed, for the ruling on one who provides support is the ruling on the one who acts directly ... So long as he supports him with this intention, his ruling is the same ..."
