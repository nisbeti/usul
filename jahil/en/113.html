(Statements of the scholars and reports from the early generations that they transmitted concerning the absence of an excuse for those who interpret the texts)

The statement of Harb al-Kirmani (34): He said, may Allah have mercy on him: "Whoever claims that our utterances of the Quran and our recitation of it are created, while the Quran is the speech of Allah, is a wicked Jahmi innovator. Whoever does not declare these people and all the Jahmiyyah to be unbelievers is like them." [Questions of Imam Ahmad, as narrated by Harb al-Kirmani, p. 360]

The statement of Ziyad ibn Ayyub al-Tusi, known as Dalawayh (35): He said, may Allah have mercy on him: "Whoever says that the Quran is created is an unbeliever; there is no doubt about it." He was asked, "Should one listen to someone who does not declare them unbelievers?" He replied, "No, absolutely not." He was asked, "I have relatives among them whom I treat kindly and greet. What should I do?" He replied, "No; do not attend their funerals or visit them." (Weak) [Reported by al-Qadi Abu Ya'la in Tabaqat al-Hanabila (1/422).] He said: Khal informed us
