A report was narrated from him that contradicts it. If he contradicts it after the proof has been established against him through the report, as I have explained, in a matter whose true nature cannot be known, then the one ignorant of it is excused, for such knowledge cannot be attained through reason, reflection, or thought.

This includes God, Exalted is He, informing us that He is All-Hearing and All-Seeing, and that He has two hands, as He says: {Rather, His two hands are outstretched}. And that He has a right hand, as He says: {And the heavens will be folded in His right hand}. And that He has a face, as He says: {Everything will perish except His face}, and: {And the face of your Lord, full of majesty and honor, will remain}. And that He has a foot, according to the Messenger of God (peace and blessings be upon him), who said: "Until the Lord places His foot in it"—meaning Hell.

And that He laughs at His believing servant, according to the Prophet's words about the man who was killed in the path of God: "He met God, Mighty and Majestic, while He was laughing at him."

And that He descends every night to the lowest heaven, according to a report from the Messenger of God (peace and blessings be upon him).

And that He is not one-eyed, according to the Prophet's words when he mentioned the Antichrist: "He is one-eyed, but your Lord is not one-eyed." And that the believers will see their Lord on the Day of Resurrection with their eyes, just as they see the sun when no cloud veils it, and just as they see the full moon, according to the Prophet's words. And that He has fingers, according to the Prophet's words: "There is no heart except that it is between two fingers of the fingers of the Most Merciful."
