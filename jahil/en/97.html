It has not been established to our satisfaction that Abu Hanifa held that the Quran was created.

Its chain of transmission is sound—in fact, exceptionally sound, as Shaykh Amr al-Misri said.

Furthermore, for the sake of argument, let us concede to these Jahmi innovators that Abu Hanifa held that the Quran was created.

Ibn Abi Hatim narrated it in his book al-Sunna—as Imam al-Dhahabi mentioned in al-Uluw:

He said:

Ahmad ibn Muhammad ibn Muslim narrated to us; Ali ibn al-Hasan al-Kara'i narrated to us, saying: Abu Yusuf said:

"I debated Abu Hanifa for six months, and we came to agree that whoever says the Quran is created is an unbeliever."

Its chain of transmission is fair, as Shaykh Amr al-Misri said in his book Abu Hanifa and the Attribution to Him of the View That the Quran Is Created.

Imam al-Bayhaqi, may Allah have mercy on him, also recorded in al-Asma wa al-Sifat, and said: "[Its narrators are trustworthy],"

that Abu Yusuf, the companion of Imam Abu Hanifa, may Allah have mercy on them both, was asked whether Abu Hanifa held Jahm's view—that the Quran was created. He replied: "Allah forbid! I do not say that either."
