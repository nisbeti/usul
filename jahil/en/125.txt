One is knowledge of the names of Allah and of what He has told us about Himself in detail. I will mention the evidence for this in detail later. Some of the Companions were unaware that Allah would be seen, that He knows what we conceal, that He holds us accountable for what our tongues have uttered, and that He had decreed our deeds for us in advance, among other things. The same applies to the question posed by the disciples and to the man who scattered his own ashes. Thus, detailed belief in knowledge of every particular is not a condition for someone to be a believer; the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) had already accepted the faith of his people without that. This also includes someone who knows one of Allah's attributes but interprets it as something linguistically sound and plausible, citing the words of the Exalted: {There is nothing like unto Him}. Likewise, someone may believe that judgment belongs to none but Allah, yet support one of the candidates—not so that he may rule by other than what Allah has revealed, but intending to lessen harm to the Muslims—citing his words (peace and blessings be upon him): "Actions are only by intentions." This is while remaining free of approving the wrong of ruling by other than what Allah has revealed, whether in word or deed, and while disapproving of it; and while not thereby assisting in violating the sanctity of a Muslim through killing, fornication, sodomy, or anything else. For these things—that is, approving the wrong, fabricating lies about Allah, changing the religion, and violating the sanctity of a Muslim—are not made permissible by coercion, as we have explained previously elsewhere. This, then, is an interpretation that permits committing an act of disbelief in order to avert harm from the Muslims, citing the concession granted to Muhammad ibn Maslama, who sought the Prophet's permission to speak ill of Ka'b ibn al-Ashraf in order to save the Muslims from his evil. The story of al-Hajjaj ibn Ilat, when he sought to recover his property from the polytheists, is exactly the same. This is a kind of interpretation subject to the conditions stated above. And Allah knows best.
