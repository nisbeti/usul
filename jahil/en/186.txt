One, he said: The report from the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) has greater claim on us that we should turn to it. If you introduce the possibility of error among those who report from him, then that possibility will arise for you in every report transmitted that contradicts the report that came from the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him). (If you say: "We establish it on the basis of the report of truthful people,") then what is established from the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) has greater claim, in our view, to be followed.
As you can see, he explicitly stated that if one person reports from another, from the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him), and it is known that the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said it, then the narrator is to be believed in our view and not contradicted. This is his explicit statement in al-Risala. There, he only denied that the knowledge gained from it is equal to the knowledge gained from the text of the Book and from a mass-transmitted report. This is correct, for knowledge varies in strength and weakness. Al-Qadi said, in the narration of Hanbal from Ahmad concerning the hadiths about the vision: "We believe in them and know that they are true." He said: "Thus he affirmed certain knowledge of them." A group of our companions adopted the apparent meaning of these words and said: "A solitary report, if it concerns the Sharia, yields knowledge." He said: "In my view, this is to be understood in a sound sense of Ahmad's words: that it yields knowledge by way of inference, not by way of necessity."
Inference yields knowledge in four ways:
The first is that the community receives it with acceptance; this indicates that it is true, because the community does not agree upon error. The community's acceptance indicates that proof of its soundness has been established for them, since it is customary that the community does not collectively accept a solitary report for which proof has not been established; rather, some people accept it and others reject it.
