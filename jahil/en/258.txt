3. As for the third category, they are the affirmers of the divine attributes who oppose the Jahmiyya, but who have a degree of Jahmism. They are those who affirm Allah's names and attributes in general, but reject some of His names and attributes that are transmitted in reports, or otherwise, and interpret them, just as the earlier group interpreted all of His attributes.
Among them are those who affirm His attributes transmitted in the Quran, but not those transmitted in hadith, as is the case with many theologians and jurists, and a group of hadith scholars.
Among them, too, are those who affirm, in general, the attributes transmitted in reports as well, but deny and negate some of what is established by textual evidence and reason. This is the case with Abu Muhammad ibn Kullab and his followers.
Abu al-Hasan al-Ashari and groups of jurists, theologians, hadith scholars, and Sufis fall into this category. They are closer to the pure Ahl al-Sunna than they are to the Jahmiyya, Rafidites, Kharijites, and Qadariyya. However, a group affiliated with them is closer to the Jahmiyya than to the pure Ahl al-Sunna: these people dispute with the Mu'tazila very strongly over the attributes they affirm, more strongly than they dispute with the rest of those who affirm the attributes over what they deny.
As for later scholars, they have increasingly allied themselves with and drawn close to the Mu'tazila, and have preferred them over the Ahl al-Sunna and those who affirm the attributes. They have opposed their predecessors. Some of them come close to one another in what they deny and affirm, and most people say that these groups contradict themselves in combining denial and affirmation.
It is clear from the preceding passage that the Asharis fall under the third category, of whom Ibn Taymiyya said that they are closer to the pure Ahl al-Sunna than to the Jahmiyya. This confirms that they are not Jahmiyya. He also described them as "those who oppose the Jahmiyya," which likewise confirms that they are not the Jahmiyya whom some of the Successors are reported to have declared disbelievers without qualification.
Some have tried to use the following transmitted report as evidence that the Asharis are Jahmiyya. They said:
"The Imam al-Tirmidhi said in al-Jami al-Kabir, commenting on hadith number 662, as follows: 'This is a hasan sahih hadith. A similar report has been narrated from Aisha, from the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him). More than one of the people of knowledge has said concerning this hadith and reports like it that…"
