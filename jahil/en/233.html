58 Say, "Have you considered what Allah has sent down for you as provision, of which you have made some unlawful and some lawful?" Say, "Has Allah given you permission, or are you fabricating lies against Allah?" 59 What, then, do those who fabricate lies against Allah think will happen to them on the Day of Resurrection? Indeed, Allah is gracious to people, but most of them are not grateful. 60 Thus Allah made the one who speaks about religion without knowledge one of two men: a man whom Allah has permitted to speak in this way, or a man who fabricates lies against Allah. Allah threatened this speaker without knowledge with what he will face on the Day of Resurrection. This shows that this is neither an excuse nor a barrier to establishing the proof against him.

And those whom they invoke besides Allah do not follow partners; they follow nothing but conjecture, and they do nothing but guess. 66

They say, "Allah has taken a son." Exalted is He! He is Self-Sufficient; to Him belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth. You have no authority for this. Do you say about Allah what you do not know? 68 Say, "Indeed, those who fabricate lies..."
