This is contrary to Islam and constitutes a complete departure from it. If they say, "It is not permissible for the ruling of a revealed law to lapse when the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) died while it remained binding upon us and had not been abrogated," we say to them: "Then on what basis did you permit this kind of preservation of the revealed law, but not the complete preservation of the revealed law, such that no falsehood ever commanded by Allah Most High could become mixed with it in a way that makes it impossible to distinguish the truth commanded by Allah Most High from the falsehood He never commanded?" They have no way out of this, and there is no difference between someone who forbids the lapse of a true revealed law but permits its being mixed with falsehood, and someone who forbids truth in the revealed law from being mixed with falsehood but permits the lapse of a true revealed law. All of this is false and utterly impermissible, and we are assured that it has not occurred, praise be to Allah. Once this is established, it is certain that we are assured, praise be to Allah, that the report of a trustworthy narrator from another like him, traced back to the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), is true beyond doubt and entails both action and knowledge. Allah Most High also said: {With clear proofs and scriptures. And We sent down to you the Reminder so that you may make clear to people what was sent down to them, and so that they may reflect.} And He Most High said: {O Messenger, convey what has been sent down to you from your Lord. If you do not, then you have not conveyed His message. And Allah will protect you from the people. Indeed, Allah does not guide the disbelieving people.} We ask them: Did the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) make clear what Allah had sent down to him, or did he not make it clear? And did he convey what Allah had sent down to him, or did he not convey it? One of these must be the case. They say that he (peace and blessings be upon him) conveyed what Allah had sent down to him, made it clear to the people, and established proof against those to whom it was conveyed. We then ask them: Does that conveyance and clarification remain?
