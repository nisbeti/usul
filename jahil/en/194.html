(Evidence that one is not deemed an unbeliever on account of interpretation or ignorance, provided one has general faith and does not falsely attribute anything to Allah, whether by affirming or denying it)

By this heading I mean that whoever, following evidence that led him to a mistaken action or belief, does so while seeking nothing but Allah's ruling on the matter, and does not lie about Allah or deny anything of Him merely on the basis of conjecture without proof, is excused. Likewise, whoever is ignorant of something in the Sharia that he is not required to know, while submitting and holding general faith, and without falsely attributing anything to Allah by affirming or denying it, is also excused. A number of legal proofs indicate that a Muslim is excused for ignorance in matters of creed. They are:

First: The legal texts that show that one who errs is excused, such as the words of Allah, Exalted is He: {Our Lord, do not take us to task if we forget or make a mistake} (al-Baqara 2:286).

Allah, Exalted is He, said, "I have done so," as in Sahih Muslim (126).

And His words, Exalted is He: {There is no blame upon you for what you do by mistake, but only for what your hearts intend. And Allah is All-Forgiving, Most Merciful} (al-Ahzab 33:5).
