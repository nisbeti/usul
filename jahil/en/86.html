Among these, certainly, is the foundation of monotheism, which entails devoting worship exclusively to Allah in all its forms. To undermine this foundation under the pretext of an interpretation that permits associating partners with Allah, Exalted is He, and taking rivals alongside Him is among the clearest forms of falsehood—the very falsehood that all the messengers were sent to abolish and denounce.

It is stated in al-Muhalla (al-Muhalla bi al-Athar, 11/334):

"It is thus established that interpretations differ. Any group that, in pursuing its aims, interprets matters in a way that erases some part of the Sunnah—for example, one who adopts the view of the Kharijites in order to remove authority from Quraysh, or to lead people back to the view that stoning is invalid, or that sinners are unbelievers, or to the expropriation of Muslims' property, or to the killing of children and women, or to proclaiming that divine decree is invalid, or that the vision of Allah is impossible, or that Allah, Exalted is He, knows nothing until it comes to be, or to disavowing some of the Companions, or to denying intercession, or to abandoning the practice of established Sunnahs from the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), and calling people to refer instead to someone below the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), or to withholding zakat, or refusing to render a right owed to a Muslim or a right owed to Allah, Exalted is He—such people are not excused on the basis of a corrupt interpretation, for this is utter ignorance. As for one who advocates an interpretation that does not invalidate any Sunnah, but is like Muawiya's interpretation that he should exact retribution from Uthman's killers before pledging allegiance to Ali, he is excused, because this does not entail rejecting any part of the religion; rather, it is a particular error concerning a specific incident that does not extend beyond it.

"As for one who rose up solely for worldly gain, as Yazid ibn Muawiya, Marwan ibn al-Hakam, and Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan did when they rose against Ibn al-Zubayr; and as Marwan ibn Muhammad did when he rose against Yazid ibn al-Walid; and those who also rose on Marwan's behalf, they are not excused, because they had no interpretation at all: it was sheer aggression."
