((Ali, Allah Most High has enjoined upon every group that it warn its people, and has enjoined upon their people that they accept its warning, in His saying, Exalted is He: {The believers should not all go forth. Why, then, should not a group from every faction go forth, so that they may gain understanding of the religion and warn their people when they return to them, so that they may beware?} He has thus warned against opposing the warning of a group. In Arabic, the word "group" applies to a part of something, as we have explained. No two Muslims would disagree that if a trustworthy Muslim entered the land of unbelief, called a people to Islam, recited the Quran to them, and taught them the religious laws, they would be obliged to accept it, and proof would thereby have been established against them. The same would apply if the caliph or an emir sent a messenger to a king among the unbelievers or to a nation among the unbelievers.
He called them to Islam and taught them the Quran and the laws of religion.
