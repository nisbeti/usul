And the Blessed and Exalted said: "Follow what has been revealed to you from your Lord. There is no god but Him, and turn away from the polytheists."

And the Blessed and Exalted said: "We sent no messenger before you without revealing to him: 'There is no god but Me, so worship Me.'"

In this verse, Allah informed His Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) that the prophets before him came to know of monotheism through hearing and revelation.

And He said: "Say: If I go astray, I go astray only to my own detriment; and if I am guided, it is by what my Lord reveals to me. He is All-Hearing, Near."

Ibrahim inferred His oneness from His precise and perfect acts: the rising and setting of the sun, the appearance and disappearance of the moon, and the appearance and setting of the stars. Then he said: "If my Lord does not guide me, I will surely be among the people who have gone astray." Thus he knew that guidance came through hearing, and likewise that knowledge of the messengers is obligatory through hearing.

Allah, the Blessed and Exalted, said: "Say: O people, I am the Messenger of Allah to you all, to Him belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth. There is no god but Him; He gives life and causes death. So believe in Allah and His Messenger, the unlettered Prophet, who believes in Allah and His words, and follow him so that you may be guided."

And He said: "We would never punish until We had sent a messenger."

And the Blessed and Exalted said: "So that people would have no argument against Allah after the messengers."
