As for the People of the Sunnah, they placed the Book and the Sunnah before them and sought the religion through them. Whatever occurred to them by way of reason, thoughts, or opinions, they presented to the Book and the Sunnah. If they found it in agreement with them, they accepted it and thanked Allah for showing it to them and granting them success in it. If they found it contrary to them, they abandoned what had occurred to them and turned to the Book and the Sunnah, directing suspicion toward themselves. For the Book and the Sunnah guide only to the truth, whereas a person's opinion may be true or false.
This is the view of Abu Sulayman al-Darani, who was the most singular of the people of his time. He said, "Whenever my soul suggested something to me, I sought two witnesses for it from the Book and the Sunnah. If they were produced, I accepted it; otherwise, I rejected it."
(He said): Among the things that show that the people of hadith are upon the truth is that if you were to examine all their authored books, from beginning to end, old and new, you would find that despite the differences in their lands and times, the great distances between their homes, and each one of them residing in a different region, they follow a single course and a single pattern in matters of belief. They adhere to a way from which they neither deviate nor turn aside; their hearts in this regard are as one heart. Their transmitted reports show no disagreement or division in anything, however slight. Indeed, if you gathered everything they have said and transmitted from their predecessors, you would find it as though it had come from one heart and been spoken by one tongue. Is there any clearer proof of the truth than this? Allah, Exalted is He, says: {Do they not reflect upon the Quran? Had it been from anyone other than Allah, they would have found much inconsistency in it} [al-Nisa: 82]. And He, Exalted is He, says: {And hold fast, all together, to the rope of Allah, and do not be divided. And remember Allah's favor upon you when you were}
