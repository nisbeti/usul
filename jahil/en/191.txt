Section

As for al-Athram's report from Ahmad that he would not testify on the basis of a report from the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him), yet act upon it, this is a report transmitted by al-Athram alone. It is not found in Ahmad's Questions or in the Book of the Sunnah. Rather, al-Qadi related that he had found it in the Book of the Meanings of Hadith. Al-Athram did not say that he had heard this from Ahmad; perhaps it had reached him through someone who had mistakenly attributed those words to him. None of Ahmad's companions transmitted this from him. Rather, what is authentically transmitted from him is that he affirmed that the ten were in Paradise, although the report concerning this is a solitary report. Perhaps his hesitation to testify was out of scrupulousness. He would affirm that certain things were forbidden, yet refrain from applying the word "forbidden" to them; and he would affirm that certain things were forbidden, yet refrain, out of scrupulousness, from applying the word "obligatory" to them. Instead, he would say, "I disapprove of such-and-such," or, "I recommend such-and-such." This is common in his answers.

In one place he said: "We do not testify that any of the people of the qibla is in the Fire on account of a sin he committed or a major sin he perpetrated, unless there is a hadith about it; then we affirm it and know that it is as reported, but we do not state the testimony explicitly. Nor do we testify that anyone is in Paradise on account of a righteous deed he performed or a good deed he did, unless there is a hadith about it; then we accept it as reported, according to what has been transmitted, but we do not state it explicitly." Al-Qadi said: "By 'we do not state the testimony explicitly,' I understand him to mean that we do not make a definitive judgment about it."
