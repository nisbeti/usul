The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) also informed us that whoever reconciles people will be granted a tremendous reward. Imam Ahmad narrated in his Musnad, from the hadith of Abu al-Darda (may Allah be pleased with him), that the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) said: "Shall I tell you what is better in rank than prayer, fasting, and charity?" They said, "Yes." He said, "Reconciling people, for discord among people is the shaver." (2) In another narration: "I do not mean that it shaves hair; rather, it shaves away religion." (3)

Through reconciliation, the brotherhood of Islam is realized. Allah, Exalted is He, says: ﴾The believers are but brothers, so make peace between your two brothers, and fear Allah so that you may be shown mercy.﴿ [al-Hujurat: 10].
