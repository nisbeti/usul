You follow nothing but conjecture, and you do nothing but guess."} It has been established that Allah, Exalted is He, has made it obligatory for us to act upon the report of a trustworthy individual, transmitted from another like him, all the way back to the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him); and to say, "The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) commanded such-and-such," "He (peace and blessings be upon him) said such-and-such," and "He (peace and blessings be upon him) did such-and-such." He has forbidden speaking about His religion on the basis of conjecture, and Allah, Exalted is He, has forbidden us to say anything about Him without knowledge. If the report in question could contain a lie or an error, Allah, Exalted is He, would have commanded us to say about Him what we do not know, and would have made it obligatory for us to rule in religion on the basis of conjecture, which we cannot be certain of, which is falsehood that avails nothing against the truth, and which is not the guidance that has come to us from Allah, Exalted is He. This is the lie, the slander, and the falsehood that it is unlawful to utter, and that Allah, Exalted is He, has forbidden us to utter, as well as the forbidden fabrication. It is therefore certain that the report in question is true, its unseen content being established beyond doubt, and that it entails both knowledge and action. And Allah, Exalted is He, is the source of success. Thus, anyone who says that acting upon a solitary report is obligatory, yet that it is still conjecture, that its unseen content is not established beyond doubt, and that it does not entail knowledge, is saying that Allah, Exalted is He, has charged us to say about Him what we have no knowledge of, and to rule in our religion on the basis of conjecture, which He, Exalted is He, has forbidden us to use as a basis for judgment.
