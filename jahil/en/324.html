If matters escalate to the point that they terrorize the roads and seize the Muslims' property by force, without any interpretation, or shed blood in the same way, then they are waging war, and the rulings on those who wage war apply to them. If matters escalate further, to the point that they violate the consensus, then they are apostates: all their property is then taken as spoils, one-fifth is set aside, and the remainder is distributed. And success is from Allah, Exalted is He.)) For Ibn Hazm, consensus meant matters known by necessity. He explained how the Kharijites violated consensus, saying in al-Muhalla bil-Athar (11/334):

Thus it is established that interpretation varies. Any group that, in pursuing its aim, interpreted the texts in a way that erased some part of the Sunnah—such as those who adopted the Kharijite position in order to remove authority from Quraysh; to return people to the view that stoning is invalid; to declare sinners unbelievers; to demand loans from the Muslims; to kill children and women; to proclaim that divine decree is invalid; to deny the vision of Allah; to claim that Allah, Exalted is He, knows nothing until it comes to be; to disavow some of the Companions; to deny intercession; to invalidate acting upon established Sunnahs from the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him); and to call for recourse to anyone other than the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him); or to refuse to pay zakat, or to withhold any right owed to a Muslim or any right owed to Allah, Exalted is He—such people are not excused on the basis of their corrupt interpretation, for it is utter ignorance.
