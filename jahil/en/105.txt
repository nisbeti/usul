Al-Tabari said in his book al-Tabṣir:

((Abu Ja'far said:
As for that without knowledge of which a person's profession of faith is not valid for us, and without which the ruling of unbelief is not lifted from him, it is what we mentioned earlier. (He had mentioned that He is the Creator and Disposer, unlike anything else, powerful, speaking, and knowing.)
This is because no one who has reached the age of religious responsibility is excused for being ignorant of the attributes we mentioned earlier, whether a messenger from Allah, exalted is He, came to him or not, and whether he has seen any creation besides himself or has seen no one but himself.
